Leading Organizational Change

Day 77 là bài học chuyên sâu 3 giờ về dẫn dắt thay đổi tổ chức: truyền thông tầm nhìn thay đổi, quản lý kháng cự, xây dựng liên minh thay đổi, và duy trì đà thay đổi. Đây là kỹ năng then chốt của mọi nhà lãnh đạo cấp cao khi dẫn dắt tổ chức qua chuyển đổi.

Thay đổi tổ chức không bắt đầu bằng chiến lược. Nó bắt đầu bằng câu chuyện — câu chuyện về tại sao chúng ta phải thay đổi, tại sao bây giờ, và tại sao cùng nhau. Nhà lãnh đạo giỏi không áp đặt thay đổi — họ truyền cảm hứng để mọi người tự nguyện thay đổi.

Block 1 · 30 phút

Vocabulary & Change Concepts 12 items

12 thuật ngữ leading organizational change cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, 2 ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

organizational change

/ˌɔːrɡənəˈzeɪʃənl tʃeɪndʒ/

Thay đổi tổ chức

Ghi chú: Organizational change là quá trình thay đổi cấu trúc, quy trình hoặc văn hóa của tổ chức. Đây là thách thức lớn nhất của lãnh đạo cấp cao.
  • Collocation: drive organizational change, lead organizational change, manage organizational change
  • Example 1: Leading organizational change requires clear vision and strong communication.
  • Example 2: We are undergoing significant organizational change to adapt to market conditions.
  • Native alternative: organizational transformation, business transformation

change management

/tʃeɪndʒ ˈmænɪdmənt/

Quản lý thay đổi

Ghi chú: Change management là quy trình có hệ thống để dẫn dắt tổ chức qua thay đổi. Bao gồm communication, training, và support.
  • Collocation: change management strategy, effective change management, change management process
  • Example 1: Effective change management is critical for successful transformation.
  • Example 2: Our change management strategy focuses on communication and employee engagement.
  • Native alternative: change management, transformation management

change agent

/tʃeɪndʒ ˈeɪdʒənt/

Người dẫn dắt thay đổi

Ghi chú: Change agent là người hoặc nhóm dẫn dắt thay đổi trong tổ chức. Thường là lãnh đạo hoặc change champions.
  • Collocation: change agent, effective change agent, become a change agent
  • Example 1: As a change agent, my role is to inspire and guide the team through transformation.
  • Example 2: We need to identify change agents in each department to drive adoption.
  • Native alternative: change leader, change champion

resistance to change

/rɪˈzɪstəns tuː tʃeɪndʒ/

Kháng cự thay đổi

Ghi chú: Resistance to change là phản ứng tự nhiên của con người với thay đổi. Lãnh đạo giỏi phải biết quản lý kháng cự này.
  • Collocation: overcome resistance to change, manage resistance, address resistance
  • Example 1: We must address resistance to change through clear communication and involvement.
  • Example 2: Resistance to change is natural, but we can overcome it through engagement.
  • Native alternative: resistance, change resistance

change readiness

/tʃendʒ ˈredɪnəs/

Sự sẵn sàng thay đổi

Ghi chú: Change readiness là mức độ sẵn sàng của tổ chức và nhân viên cho thay đổi. Cần đánh giá trước khi triển khai.
  • Collocation: assess change readiness, build change readiness, change readiness assessment
  • Example 1: We need to assess change readiness before launching the transformation.
  • Example 2: Building change readiness requires clear communication and leadership support.
  • Native alternative: readiness for change, organizational readiness

change communication

/tʃeɪndʒ kəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃn/

Truyền thông thay đổi

Ghi chú: Change communication là quá trình truyền thông về thay đổi cho toàn bộ tổ chức. Phải rõ ràng, nhất quán và thường xuyên.
  • Collocation: change communication strategy, effective change communication, change communication plan
  • Example 1: Our change communication strategy focuses on transparency and regular updates.
  • Example 2: Effective change communication is critical for successful transformation.
  • Native alternative: change communication, transformation communication

change adoption

/tʃeɪndʒ əˈdɑːpʃn/

Sự chấp nhận thay đổi

Ghi chú: Change adoption là quá trình nhân viên chấp nhận và áp dụng thay đổi. Đây là mục tiêu cuối cùng của change management.
  • Collocation: drive change adoption, accelerate change adoption, change adoption rate
  • Example 1: We need to accelerate change adoption through training and support.
  • Example 2: Change adoption rate has increased significantly after the communication campaign.
  • Native alternative: change adoption, adoption of change

sustaining change

/səˈsteɪnɪ tʃeɪndʒ/

Duy trì thay đổi

Ghi chú: Sustaining change là duy trì thay đổi sau khi triển khai. Đây là bước khó nhất trong change management.
  • Collocation: sustaining change, sustain change, sustain the change
  • Example 1: Sustaining change requires ongoing leadership support and reinforcement.
  • Example 2: We must focus on sustaining change to ensure long-term success.
  • Native alternative: sustaining change, maintaining change

change leadership

/tʃeɪndʒ ˈlidərʃɪp/

Lãnh đạo thay đổi

Ghi chú: Change leadership là khả năng lãnh đạo tổ chức qua thay đổi. Khác với change management (quy trình), change leadership là về con người và tầm nhìn.
  • Collocation: change leadership, effective change leadership, demonstrate change leadership
  • Example 1: Effective change leadership requires vision, communication, and empathy.
  • Example 2: We need strong change leadership to guide the organization through transformation.
  • Native alternative: change leadership, transformational leadership

transformational leadership

/ˌtrænsfərˈmeɪʃənl ˈliːdərʃɪp/

Lãnh đạo chuyển đổi

Ghi chú: Transformational leadership là phong cách lãnh đạo truyền cảm hứng để mọi người vượt qua giới hạn bản thân và đạt được tầm nhìn chung.
  • Collocation: transformational leadership, practice transformational leadership, transformational leader
  • Example 1: Transformational leadership inspires people to achieve extraordinary results.
  • Example 2: We need transformational leadership to drive organizational transformation.
  • Native alternative: transformational leadership, transformative leadership

change champion

/tʃeɪndʒ ˈtæmpiən/

Người ủng hộ thay đổi

Ghi chú: Change champion là người ủng hộ và thúc đẩy thay đổi trong tổ chức. Thường là influencers trong các phòng ban.
  • Collocation: change champion, identify change champions, empower change champions
  • Example 1: We need to identify change champions in each department to drive adoption.
  • Example 2: Change champions play a critical role in driving change adoption.
  • Native alternative: change champion, change advocate

change momentum

/tʃendʒ moʊˈmentəm/

Đà thay đổi

Ghi chú: Change momentum là đà và động lực của thay đổi. Lãnh đạo phải duy trì momentum để đảm bảo thay đổi thành công.
  • Collocation: build change momentum, maintain change momentum, sustain momentum
  • Example 1: We must build change momentum through quick wins and visible progress.
  • Example 2: Maintaining change momentum requires consistent leadership support.
  • Native alternative: change momentum, transformation momentum
Block 2 · 40 phút

Phrases & Change Frameworks 8 patterns

8 mẫu câu leading change dựa trên Kotter's 8-Step Change Model. Mỗi pattern có công thức, 3 ví dụ theo ngữ cảnh, substitution drill và lỗi cần tránh.

Creating a sense of urgency...

Công thức: Creating a sense of urgency + reason/urgency. Bước 1 trong Kotter's model: tạo cảm giác cấp bách để mọi người thấy cần thay đổi ngay.
Communicating the why

Creating a sense of urgency is the first step in driving organizational change. We must act now or risk falling behind competitors.

Substitution drill

Thay reason: market conditions are changing rapidly / customer expectations are evolving / technology is disrupting our industry.

Building a guiding coalition...

Công thức: Building a guiding coalition + who/why. Bước 2: xây dựng liên minh dẫn dắt gồm các lãnh đạo có ảnh hưởng để dẫn dắt thay đổi.
Building coalition

Building a guiding coalition of influential leaders is essential for successful change. We need leaders from each department to champion this change.

Substitution drill

Thay who: senior leaders / change champions / cross-functional leaders. Thay why: to drive adoption / to ensure alignment / to sustain momentum.

Communicating the vision for change...

Công thức: Communicating the vision for change + how/why. Bước 3: truyền thông tầm nhìn thay đổi một cách rõ ràng và nhất quán.
Communicating vision

Communicating the vision for change clearly and consistently is critical for alignment. We must communicate the why, what, and how of this change.

Substitution drill

Thay how: through town halls / through regular updates / through leadership communication. Thay why: to ensure understanding / to build commitment / to drive engagement.

Empowering others to act on the vision...

Công thức: Empowering others to act on the vision + how. Bước 4: trao quyền cho người khác hành động theo tầm nhìn bằng cách loại bỏ rào cản.
Empowering action

Empowering others to act on the vision requires removing barriers and providing support. We must empower our teams to make decisions and take action.

Substitution drill

Thay how: by removing obstacles / by providing resources / by empowering decision-making. Thay action: to drive change / to implement changes / to adopt new ways.

Generating short-term wins...

Công thức: Generating short-term wins + why/how. Bước 5: tạo ra những thắng lợi ngắn hạn để xây dựng đà và duy trì thay đổi.
Generating wins

Generating short-term wins is essential for building momentum and sustaining change. We must celebrate quick wins to maintain momentum.

Substitution drill

Thay why: to build confidence / to demonstrate progress / to sustain engagement. Thay how: through quick wins / through visible results / through early successes.

Consolidating gains and producing more change...

Công thức: Consolidating gains and producing more change + why. Bước 6: củng cố những gì đã đạt được và tạo ra nhiều thay đổi hơn.
Consolidating gains

Consolidating gains and producing more change is critical for long-term success. We must build on our successes to drive further transformation.

Substitution drill

Thay why: to sustain momentum / to drive continuous improvement / to achieve transformation. Thay how: by building on successes / by leveraging wins / by accelerating change.

Anchoring new approaches in the culture...

Công thức: Anchoring new approaches in the culture + how/why. Bước 7: neo đậu các cách tiếp cận mới vào văn hóa để duy trì thay đổi lâu dài.
Anchoring change

Anchoring new approaches in the culture is the final step in sustaining change. We must embed new behaviors into our culture to make change permanent.

Substitution drill

Thay how: through reinforcement / through recognition / through systems. Thay why: to make change permanent / to sustain transformation / to embed new ways.

Addressing resistance to change...

Công thức: Addressing resistance to change + how. Kỹ năng then chốt: quản lý kháng cự thay đổi thông qua thấu cảm, truyền thông và tham gia.
Addressing resistance

Addressing resistance to change requires empathy, communication, and involvement. We must listen to concerns and involve people in the change process.

Substitution drill

Thay how: through active listening / through involvement / through support. Thay approach: addressing concerns / engaging stakeholders / empowering change agents.

Listening & Case Study 2 pieces

Nghe 2 bài listening chuyên sâu: (1) CEO announcing organizational change, (2) Change leader addressing resistance. Mỗi bài nghe 3 lần với mục tiêu khác nhau.

Piece 1: CEO Announcing Organizational Change (20 phút)

  1. Nghe lần 1 (5 phút): Nghe toàn bộ để nắm ý chính và cấu trúc bài phát biểu.
  2. Nghe lần 2 (7 phút): Nghe để nhận diện 8 patterns của Kotter's model.
  3. Nghe lần 3 (8 phút): Nghe để phân tích cấu trúc, ngôn ngữ, và kỹ thuật truyền thông.

Transcript - Piece 1

[Opening - Creating Urgency]Good morning everyone. Thank you for being here today. I want to talk about why we need to change and why we need to act now. Our industry is being disrupted by technology, and customer expectations are evolving rapidly. If we don't change now, we risk falling behind our competitors. That's why we're launching this transformation initiative.

[Building Coalition]To drive this change successfully, we're building a guiding coalition of leaders from each department. These change champions will help us communicate the vision and drive adoption across the organization.

[Communicating Vision]Our vision is clear: to become the digital leader in our industry by 2025. This means transforming how we work, how we serve customers, and how we innovate.

[Empowering Action]To make this vision real, we need to empower our teams to act. This means removing barriers, providing resources, and empowering decision-making at all levels.

[Generating Wins]We will generate short-term wins to build momentum. In the next 90 days, we will deliver three quick wins that demonstrate progress and build confidence. These wins will help us consolidate gains and produce more change.

[Anchoring Change]Finally, we will anchor these new approaches in our culture. This means embedding new behaviors into our systems, recognition, and ways of working. This is how we will sustain change for the long term.

[Addressing Resistance]I know change is challenging, and I know there will be resistance. But I also know that together, we can achieve extraordinary results. I'm committed to this journey, and I need your commitment too. Let's make this transformation happen together. Thank you.

Comprehension Questions - Piece 1

  • Question 1: Why does the CEO say we need to change now?
  • Question 2: What is the vision for change?
  • Question 3: How will the organization empower teams to act?
  • Question 4: What are the three quick wins in the next 90 days?
  • Question 5: How will the organization anchor change in culture?

Sample Answers - Piece 1

1. Because the industry is being disrupted by technology and customer expectations are evolving rapidly.
2. To become the digital leader in the industry by 2025.
3. By removing barriers, providing resources, and empowering decision-making at all levels.
4. Three quick wins that demonstrate progress and build confidence.
5. By embedding new behaviors into systems, recognition, and ways of working.

Piece 2: Change Leader Addressing Resistance (20 phút)

  1. Nghe lần 1 (5 phút): Nghe toàn bộ để nắm cách tiếp cận và cấu trúc.
  2. Nghe lần 2 (7 phút): Nghe để nhận diện kỹ thuật xử lý kháng cự.
  3. Nghe lần 3 (8 phút): Nghe để phân tích ngôn ngữ thấu cảm và kỹ thuật thuyết phục.

Transcript - Piece 2

[Acknowledging Concerns]Thank you for sharing your concerns. I hear you, and I understand that change is challenging. Let me address your concerns directly.

[Addressing Concern 1]First, I understand that this change feels overwhelming. Change is always challenging, and it's normal to feel uncertain. But I want to assure you that we're committed to supporting you through this journey.

[Addressing Concern 2]Second, I understand your concern about workload. We're committed to providing additional resources and support to help you manage the transition. We will also provide training and coaching to help you develop the skills needed for this change.

[Addressing Concern 3]Third, I understand your concern about job security. Let me be clear: this change is about transforming how we work, not about reducing headcount. In fact, this transformation will create new opportunities for growth and development.

[Closing]I know change is difficult, and I appreciate your honesty in sharing your concerns. Together, we will navigate this change successfully. I'm committed to this journey, and I need your commitment too. Let's make this transformation happen together. Thank you.

1. The change feels overwhelming, concern about workload, and concern about job security.
2. By providing additional resources, support, training, and coaching.
3. The change is about transforming how we work, not reducing headcount. It will create new opportunities.
4. Their commitment to the journey.

Speaking & Practice 5 rounds

Nói 5 vòng tăng dần: từ communicating why đến full change leadership speech 15 phút. Mục tiêu là thể hiện change leadership capability.

Round 1 · 3 phút (Communicating Why)

Chỉ tập trung vào creating urgency và communicating the why of change.

Good morning everyone. Thank you for being here today. I want to talk about why we need to change and why we need to act now. Our industry is being disrupted by technology, and customer expectations are evolving rapidly. If we don't change now, we risk falling behind our competitors. That's why we're launching this transformation initiative. Our vision is clear: to become the digital leader in our industry by 2025. This is why we must act now, and this is why we must act together.

Round 2 · 5 phút (Building Coalition)

Thêm building guiding coalition và empowering action.

Good morning everyone. Thank you for being here today. I want to talk about why we need to change and why we need to act now. Our industry is being disrupted by technology, and customer expectations are evolving rapidly. If we don't change now, we risk falling behind our competitors. To drive this change successfully, we're building a guiding coalition of leaders from each department. These change champions will help us communicate the vision and drive adoption across the organization. To make this vision real, we need to empower our teams to act. This means removing barriers, providing resources, and empowering decision-making at all levels. Together, we will make this transformation happen.

Round 3 · 7 phút (Communicating Vision)

Thêm communicating vision chi tiết hơn và generating short-term wins.

Good morning everyone. Thank you for being here today. I want to talk about why we need to change and why we need to act now. Our industry is being disrupted by technology, and customer expectations are evolving rapidly. If we don't change now, we risk falling behind our competitors. Our vision is clear: to become the digital leader in our industry by 2025. This means transforming how we work, how we serve customers, and how we innovate. To drive this change successfully, we're building a guiding coalition of leaders from each department. These change champions will help us communicate the vision and drive adoption. We will generate short-term wins to build momentum. In the next 90 days, we will deliver three quick wins that demonstrate progress and build confidence. Together, we will make this transformation happen.

Round 4 · 10 phút (Addressing Resistance)

Thêm addressing resistance và consolidating gains.

Good morning everyone. Thank you for being here today. I want to talk about why we need to change and why we need to act now. Our industry is being disrupted by technology, and customer expectations are evolving rapidly. If we don't change now, we risk falling behind our competitors. Our vision is clear: to become the digital leader in our industry by 2025. To drive this change successfully, we're building a guiding coalition of leaders from each department. We will generate short-term wins to build momentum. In the next 90 days, we will deliver three quick wins. I know change is challenging, and I know there will be resistance. But I also know that together, we can achieve extraordinary results. We will consolidate gains and produce more change. Finally, we will anchor these new approaches in our culture. This is how we will sustain change for the long term. Together, we will make this transformation happen.

Round 5 · 15 phút (Full Change Leadership Speech)

Full change leadership speech với tất cả 8 steps của Kotter's model.

Good morning everyone, and thank you for being here today. I want to talk about why we need to change and why we need to act now. Our industry is being disrupted by technology, customer expectations are evolving rapidly, and competitors are moving fast. If we don't change now, we risk falling behind. That's why we're launching this transformation initiative.

Our vision is clear: to become the digital leader in our industry by 2025. This means transforming how we work, how we serve customers, and how we innovate. This is our vision, and this is why we must act now.

To drive this change successfully, we're building a guiding coalition of leaders from each department. These change champions will help us communicate the vision and drive adoption across the organization. We need leaders at every level to champion this change.

To make this vision real, we need to empower our teams to act. This means removing barriers, providing resources, and empowering decision-making at all levels. We must empower our people to make decisions and take action.

We will generate short-term wins to build momentum. In the next 90 days, we will deliver three quick wins that demonstrate progress and build confidence. These wins will help us consolidate gains and produce more change.

We will consolidate gains and produce more change. We will build on our successes to drive further transformation. We will leverage our wins to accelerate change across the organization.

Finally, we will anchor these new approaches in our culture. This means embedding new behaviors into our systems, recognition, and ways of working. This is how we will sustain change for the long term.

I know change is challenging, and I know there will be resistance. But I also know that together, we can achieve extraordinary results. I'm committed to this journey, and I need your commitment too. Let's make this transformation happen together. Thank you.

Output & Application 3 tasks

Output cuối ngày gồm 3 phần: viết script, ghi âm final, và viết change communication plan. Hoàn thành đủ 3 phần này trước khi chuyển sang Day 78.

Task 1: Viết Change Leadership Script 300-400 từ

Viết một change leadership script chuyên nghiệp, dùng trong buổi town hall hoặc all-hands meeting.

Yêu cầu

  • Dùng ít nhất 8 từ vựng trong mục Vocabulary.
  • Dùng đủ 8 patterns của Kotter's model.
  • Có cấu trúc: Opening → Urgency → Vision → Coalition → Empowerment → Wins → Consolidation → Anchoring → Closing.
  • Không viết quá chung chung — phải cụ thể và có ví dụ.
  • Giọng văn truyền cảm hứng, thấu cảm, và chắc chắn.

Model Answer

Good morning everyone, and thank you for being here today. I want to talk about why we need to change and why we need to act now. Our industry is being disrupted by technology, customer expectations are evolving rapidly, and competitors are moving fast. If we don't change now, we risk falling behind. That's why we're launching this transformation initiative.

Our vision is clear: to become the digital leader in our industry by 2025. This means transforming how we work, how we serve customers, and how we innovate. This is our vision, and this is why we must act now.

To drive this change successfully, we're building a guiding coalition of leaders from each department. These change champions will help us communicate the vision and drive adoption across the organization. We need leaders at every level to champion this change.

To make this vision real, we need to empower our teams to act. This means removing barriers, providing resources, and empowering decision-making at all levels. We must empower our people to make decisions and take action.

We will generate short-term wins to build momentum. In the next 90 days, we will deliver three quick wins that demonstrate progress and build confidence. These wins will help us consolidate gains and produce more change.

We will consolidate gains and produce more change. We will build on our successes to drive further transformation. We will leverage our wins to accelerate change across the organization.

Finally, we will anchor these new approaches in our culture. This means embedding new behaviors into our systems, recognition, and ways of working. This is how we will sustain change for the long term.

I know change is challenging, and I know there will be resistance. But I also know that together, we can achieve extraordinary results. I'm committed to this journey, and I need your commitment too. Let's make this transformation happen together. Thank you.

Task 2: Ghi âm Final 10-15 phút

Sau khi viết script, hãy ghi âm phiên bản final 10-15 phút. Mục tiêu là nói rõ, chắc, có ngắt nghỉ và truyền cảm hứng.

Recording Checklist

  • Mở đầu tự nhiên, tạo urgency rõ ràng.
  • Communicating vision rõ ràng và truyền cảm hứng.
  • Building coalition cụ thể với change champions.
  • Empowering action cụ thể với barriers và resources.
  • Generating short-term wins cụ thể với 3 quick wins.
  • Consolidating gains và anchoring change rõ ràng.
  • Addressing resistance thấu cảm và chắc chắn.
  • Closing truyền cảm hứng và kêu gọi commitment.

Task 3: Viết Change Communication Plan

Viết change communication plan ngắn gọn cho organizational change initiative.

Yêu cầu

  • Có mục tiêu truyền thông thay đổi.
  • Có đối tượng mục tiêu (audience).
  • Có thông điệp chính (key messages).
  • Có kênh truyền thông (channels).
  • Có timeline và tần suất.

Model Answer

Change Communication Plan

Objective: To communicate the vision for change and drive adoption across the organization.

Audience: All employees, with focus on department leaders and change champions.

Key Messages: (1) Why we need to change now, (2) Our vision for 2025, (3) How we will empower teams to act, (4) How we will generate quick wins and sustain change.

Channels: Town halls, leadership communication, regular updates, change champion network.

Timeline: Launch in Week 1, quick wins in 90 days, consolidation in 6 months, anchoring in 12 months.

Task 4: Reflection 5 dòng

Viết reflection 5 dòng sau khi nghe lại bản ghi âm:

  • Good 1: 1 điểm bạn đã làm tốt.
  • Good 2: 1 điểm khác bạn đã làm tốt.
  • Improve 1: 1 điểm cần cải thiện.
  • Improve 2: 1 điểm khác cần cải thiện.
  • Keep: 1 kỹ thuật bạn sẽ tiếp tục dùng trong các ngày tới.
Reflection mẫu

Good 1: Tôi đã creating urgency rõ ràng và thuyết phục. Good 2: Tôi đã communicating vision truyền cảm hứng. Improve 1: Tôi cần empowering action cụ thể hơn. Improve 2: Tôi cần addressing resistance thấu cảm hơn. Keep: "Together, we will make this transformation happen."

Common Mistakes

4 lỗi thường gặp khi leading change

Tránh các lỗi này để change leadership của bạn chuyên nghiệp và thuyết phục.

Tránh

Không creating urgency rõ ràng: "We should change sometime."

Nên

Our industry is being disrupted by technology, and we must act now or risk falling behind competitors.

Tránh

Không communicating vision rõ ràng: "We will change things."

Nên

Our vision is clear: to become the digital leader in our industry by 2025 by transforming how we work and serve customers.

Tránh

Không addressing resistance thấu cảm: "You need to accept the change."

Nên

I understand that change is challenging, and I appreciate your honesty in sharing your concerns. Together, we will navigate this successfully.

Tránh

Không có closing truyền cảm hứng: "That's all. Thanks."

Nên

I'm committed to this journey, and I need your commitment too. Together, we will make this transformation happen. Thank you.

Level 1 Week 4 · Executive Communication Day 29 / 30 90 phút Speaking topic: Investor Meeting

Mock Investment Committee

Day 29 là bài test tổng hợp cuối cùng trước Day 30. Bạn sẽ thực hiện một mock investment committee meeting: pitch dự án 10 phút, trả lời 5 câu hỏi hóc búa từ hội đồng, và bảo vệ recommendation của mình. Đây là cơ hội để bạn thực hành toàn bộ kỹ năng đã học trong một tình huống thực tế.

Investment committee không phải là bài kiểm tra — đó là cơ hội. Cơ hội để bạn chứng minh rằng bạn không chỉ hiểu dự án, mà còn hiểu rủi ro, hiểu thị trường, và hiểu tại sao đây là cơ hội đầu tư đúng đắn.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & IC Terms

8 thuật ngữ investment committee cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

investment thesis

/ɪnˈvestmənt ˈθiːsɪs/

Luận điểm đầu tư

Ghi chú: Investment thesis là luận điểm cốt lõi giải thích tại sao đây là cơ hội đầu tư tốt. Thường có 2-3 trụ cột chính.
  • Collocation: investment thesis is based on, core investment thesis, compelling investment thesis
  • Example: Our investment thesis is based on three pillars: strong market demand, proven execution capability, and attractive risk-adjusted returns.
  • Native alternative: investment rationale, investment case

risk-adjusted return

/rɪsk əˈdʒʌstɪd rɪˈtɜːrn/

Lợi nhuận điều chỉnh theo rủi ro

Ghi chú: Risk-adjusted return là lợi nhuận sau khi đã tính đến mức độ rủi ro. Quan trọng hơn lợi nhuận tuyệt đối.
  • Collocation: attractive risk-adjusted return, superior risk-adjusted returns, risk-adjusted return profile
  • Example: The project delivers an 18 percent IRR, which represents an attractive risk-adjusted return given the market conditions.
  • Native alternative: risk-adjusted performance, risk-adjusted yield

due diligence

/djuː ˈdɪlɪdʒəns/

Thẩm định

Ghi chú: Due diligence là quá trình thẩm định toàn diện trước khi đầu tư. Bao gồm legal, financial, operational due diligence.
  • Collocation: conduct due diligence, complete due diligence, comprehensive due diligence
  • Example: We've completed comprehensive due diligence covering legal, financial, and operational aspects of the project.
  • Native alternative: due diligence process, DD process

exit strategy

/ˈeksɪt ˈstrætədʒi/

Chiến lược thoát

Ghi chú: Exit strategy là kế hoạch thoát khỏi khoản đầu tư để thu hồi vốn và lợi nhuận. Cần rõ ràng và khả thi.
  • Collocation: clear exit strategy, viable exit strategy, exit strategy is to
  • Example: Our exit strategy is to sell the completed project to an institutional investor within 18 months of completion.
  • Native alternative: exit plan, exit route

capital allocation

/ˈkæpɪtl ˌæləˈkeɪʃn/

Phân bổ vốn

Ghi chú: Capital allocation là cách phân chia vốn giữa equity và debt. Ảnh hưởng đến risk profile và returns.
  • Collocation: optimal capital allocation, capital allocation strategy, efficient capital allocation
  • Example: The capital allocation is 60 percent equity and 40 percent debt, optimized for risk-adjusted returns.
  • Native alternative: capital structure, funding mix

hurdle rate

/ˈhɜːdl reɪt/

Tỷ suất hurdle (tỷ suất tối thiểu)

Ghi chú: Hurdle rate là tỷ suất lợi nhuận tối thiểu mà nhà đầu tư yêu cầu. Project phải vượt qua hurdle rate mới đáng đầu tư.
  • Collocation: hurdle rate of X percent, exceeds hurdle rate, meets hurdle rate
  • Example: The project IRR of 18 percent exceeds our hurdle rate of 15 percent by 300 basis points.
  • Native alternative: required rate of return, minimum acceptable return

downside protection

/ˈdaʊnsaɪd prəˈtekʃn/

Bảo vệ downside

Ghi chú: Downside protection là các biện pháp bảo vệ khi mọi thứ không theo kế hoạch. Quan trọng để giảm rủi ro.
  • Collocation: built-in downside protection, downside protection mechanisms, provide downside protection
  • Example: The project has built-in downside protection through fixed-price contracts and a 10 percent contingency buffer.
  • Native alternative: downside risk mitigation, downside safeguards

value creation

/ˈvæljuː kriˈeɪʃn/

Tạo giá trị

Ghi chú: Value creation là quá trình tạo ra giá trị cho nhà đầu tư. Cần giải thích rõ value được tạo ra từ đâu.
  • Collocation: value creation drivers, value creation strategy, significant value creation
  • Example: The project creates value through three drivers: market timing, execution excellence, and operational efficiency.
  • Native alternative: value generation, value enhancement
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

6 mẫu câu cho IC meeting: opening pitch, presenting thesis, addressing risks, defending recommendation, handling pushback, closing with conviction.

Let me walk you through the investment case for...

Công thức: Let me walk you through the investment case for + project + structure. Dùng để mở đầu pitch, signpost cấu trúc bài nói.
Opening pitch

Let me walk you through the investment case for Riverside Phase 2. I'll cover the market opportunity, our execution plan, the risk profile, and our recommendation.

Substitution drill

Thay project: Harbor View / Phase 3 expansion. Thay structure: market context, financials, risks, recommendation.

Our investment thesis is based on...

Công thức: Our investment thesis is based on + pillars/drivers. Dùng để trình bày luận điểm đầu tư cốt lõi.
Presenting thesis

Our investment thesis is based on three pillars: strong market demand in the eastern corridor, proven execution capability with Phase 1, and attractive risk-adjusted returns of 18 percent IRR.

Substitution drill

Thay pillars: market timing, execution excellence, operational efficiency / strong fundamentals, proven track record, attractive valuation.

We've mitigated this risk by...

Công thức: We've mitigated this risk by + specific actions. Dùng để giải thích cách giảm thiểu rủi ro cụ thể.
Addressing risks

We've mitigated construction cost risk by locking in fixed-price contracts for 70 percent of the work and building a 10 percent contingency buffer into the budget.

Substitution drill

Thay risk: legal delays / slower absorption / funding risk. Thay actions: early legal milestones / phased launches / secured financing.

The data supports our recommendation because...

Công thức: The data supports our recommendation because + evidence. Dùng để bảo vệ recommendation bằng dữ liệu.
Defending recommendation

The data supports our recommendation because the project delivers 18 percent IRR, exceeding our 15 percent hurdle rate, with built-in downside protection through fixed-price contracts.

Substitution drill

Thay evidence: market demand exceeds supply / execution track record is proven / risk profile is manageable.

I understand your concern, and here's how we address it...

Công thức: I understand your concern about + concern, and here's how we address it + solution. Dùng để handle pushback từ hội đồng.
Handling pushback

I understand your concern about construction delays, and here's how we address it: we've locked in fixed-price contracts, built a 10 percent contingency buffer, and have a proven track record of on-time delivery.

Substitution drill

Thay concern: market slowdown / funding risk / legal delays. Thay solution: phased approach / secured financing / early milestones.

My recommendation is clear: we should...

Công thức: My recommendation is clear: we should + action + urgency/reason. Dùng để kết thúc pitch với recommendation rõ ràng và có tính thuyết phục.
Closing with conviction

My recommendation is clear: we should approve Phase 2 funding by the end of this month, before the fixed-price contracts expire and we lose the pricing advantage.

Substitution drill

Thay action: proceed with the acquisition / increase our allocation / move forward with Phase 3. Thay urgency: before market conditions change / before the window closes.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · IC Meeting with Q&A

Nghe một bài pitch mẫu 10 phút với Q&A từ hội đồng. Đây là mô hình để bạn thực hành mock IC meeting.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm pitch structure và Q&A flow.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện các patterns: investment thesis, risk mitigation, data-driven defense, handling pushback.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích defense techniques: acknowledge concern, provide evidence, restate recommendation.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm (pitch opening, thesis statement, closing recommendation).

Transcript

[Pitch Opening]
Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here. Let me walk you through the investment case for Riverside Phase 2. I'll cover the market opportunity, our execution plan, the risk profile, and our recommendation.

[Investment Thesis]
Our investment thesis is based on three pillars: strong market demand in the eastern corridor where supply is constrained, our proven execution capability demonstrated by Phase 1 which delivered 18 percent IRR, and attractive risk-adjusted returns with built-in downside protection.

[Market Opportunity]
The market opportunity is compelling: demand exceeds supply by 30 percent in the eastern corridor, population growth is 4 percent annually, and there are no competing projects in the pipeline.

[Execution Plan]
Our execution plan leverages our proven track record: we've locked in fixed-price contracts for 70 percent of construction costs, built a 10 percent contingency buffer, and have a clear timeline with milestones.

[Risk Profile]
The risk profile is manageable: we've mitigated construction cost risk through fixed-price contracts, legal delay risk through early milestones, and absorption risk through phased launches.

[Financials]
The project delivers 18 percent IRR, exceeding our 15 percent hurdle rate by 300 basis points, with downside protection through fixed-price contracts and contingency buffer.

[Closing Recommendation]
My recommendation is clear: we should approve Phase 2 funding by the end of this month, before the fixed-price contracts expire and we lose the pricing advantage. Thank you. I'm happy to take any questions.

[Q&A - Question 1]
Committee Member 1: I'm concerned about construction delays. How do you address this?
Presenter: I understand your concern about construction delays, and here's how we address it: we've locked in fixed-price contracts for 70 percent of the work, built a 10 percent contingency buffer into the budget, and have a proven track record of on-time delivery with Phase 1.

[Q&A - Question 2]
Committee Member 2: What if the market slows down?
Presenter: That's a valid concern. We've addressed this by phasing the launches: we'll only launch when pre-sales reach 50 percent, which de-risks the project. Even in a 20 percent price decline scenario, the project still delivers 14 percent IRR, above our hurdle rate.

[Q&A - Question 3]
Committee Member 3: Why now? Why not wait for better market conditions?
Presenter: The data supports moving now because: first, we've locked in favorable pricing with contractors; second, market demand is strong with 30 percent supply constraint; third, waiting 6 months would cost us 5 percent in higher construction costs. The window of opportunity is now.

[Q&A - Question 4]
Committee Member 4: What's your exit strategy?
Presenter: Our exit strategy is to sell the completed project to an institutional investor within 18 months of completion, targeting a 20 percent premium to cost. We have preliminary interest from two institutional investors.

[Q&A - Question 5]
Committee Member 5: What if you can't find a buyer?
Presenter: We've structured the project to be cash-flow positive even without an exit sale. We can hold the asset and generate rental income of 8 percent yield, which covers our cost of capital. So we're not dependent on an exit sale.

[Closing]
Thank you for your questions. My recommendation remains clear: approve Phase 2 funding now to capture the current pricing advantage and strong market demand.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: What are the three pillars of the investment thesis?
  • Q2: How does the presenter address construction delay concerns?
  • Q3: What is the IRR and how does it compare to the hurdle rate?
  • Q4: What is the exit strategy?
  • Q5: What happens if they can't find a buyer?

Sample Answers

1. Three pillars: strong market demand (30% supply constraint), proven execution capability (Phase 1 delivered 18% IRR), attractive risk-adjusted returns with downside protection.
2. Mitigated by: locked in fixed-price contracts for 70% of work, 10% contingency buffer, proven track record of on-time delivery.
3. IRR is 18%, exceeding the 15% hurdle rate by 300 basis points.
4. Exit strategy is to sell to institutional investor within 18 months of completion, targeting 20% premium to cost.
5. Project is cash-flow positive without exit sale: can hold asset and generate 8% rental yield, covering cost of capital.

Shadow 2 · Thesis statement

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: investment thesis, three pillars, risk-adjusted returns, downside protection.
  • Giọng tự tin, chắc chắn khi trình bày thesis và recommendation.
  • Giọng calm, reassuring khi address concerns.
  • Pause sau mỗi pillar để hội đồng tiêu hóa thông tin.
Block 4 · 20 phút

Speaking · Mock IC Meeting

Luyện 3 vòng tăng dần: pitch ngắn → pitch đầy đủ → pitch + Q&A. Mục tiêu: tự tin trình bày và bảo vệ recommendation trước hội đồng.

Round 1 · Pitch 5 phút

Trình bày pitch ngắn 5 phút: opening, thesis, market, execution, risks, recommendation.

Opening: Good morning. Let me walk you through the investment case for Riverside Phase 2.

Thesis: Our investment thesis is based on three pillars: strong market demand, proven execution, and attractive returns.

Market: Demand exceeds supply by 30 percent in the eastern corridor with 4 percent annual population growth.

Execution: We've locked in fixed-price contracts and built a 10 percent contingency buffer.

Risks: We've mitigated construction, legal, and absorption risks through specific actions.

Recommendation: The project delivers 18 percent IRR, exceeding our 15 percent hurdle rate. I recommend we approve funding now.

Round 2 · Pitch 10 phút

Mở rộng pitch lên 10 phút: thêm chi tiết market data, execution details, risk mitigation specifics, financial projections.

Opening (1 min): Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here. Let me walk you through the investment case for Riverside Phase 2. I'll cover the market opportunity, our execution plan, the risk profile, and our recommendation.

Thesis (2 min): Our investment thesis is based on three pillars: strong market demand in the eastern corridor where supply is constrained, our proven execution capability demonstrated by Phase 1 which delivered 18 percent IRR, and attractive risk-adjusted returns with built-in downside protection.

Market (2 min): The market opportunity is compelling: demand exceeds supply by 30 percent in the eastern corridor, population growth is 4 percent annually, and there are no competing projects in the pipeline. The eastern corridor has seen 15 percent price appreciation over the past 3 years.

Execution (2 min): Our execution plan leverages our proven track record: we've locked in fixed-price contracts for 70 percent of construction costs, built a 10 percent contingency buffer, and have a clear timeline with milestones. Phase 1 was delivered on time and 5 percent under budget.

Risks (2 min): The risk profile is manageable: we've mitigated construction cost risk through fixed-price contracts, legal delay risk through early milestones, and absorption risk through phased launches. Even in a downside scenario, the project remains viable.

Financials (1 min): The project delivers 18 percent IRR, exceeding our 15 percent hurdle rate by 300 basis points, with downside protection through fixed-price contracts and contingency buffer.

Closing (1 min): My recommendation is clear: we should approve Phase 2 funding by the end of this month, before the fixed-price contracts expire and we lose the pricing advantage. Thank you. I'm happy to take any questions.

Round 3 · Pitch 10 phút + Q&A

Sau khi pitch 10 phút, trả lời 5 câu hỏi giả định từ hội đồng. Dùng cấu trúc: acknowledge concern → provide evidence → restate recommendation.

Question 1: Construction delays?
I understand your concern about construction delays, and here's how we address it: we've locked in fixed-price contracts for 70 percent of the work, built a 10 percent contingency buffer into the budget, and have a proven track record of on-time delivery with Phase 1.

Question 2: Market slowdown?
That's a valid concern. We've addressed this by phasing the launches: we'll only launch when pre-sales reach 50 percent, which de-risks the project. Even in a 20 percent price decline scenario, the project still delivers 14 percent IRR, above our hurdle rate.

Question 3: Why now?
The data supports moving now because: first, we've locked in favorable pricing with contractors; second, market demand is strong with 30 percent supply constraint; third, waiting 6 months would cost us 5 percent in higher construction costs. The window of opportunity is now.

Question 4: Exit strategy?

Question 5: What if no buyer?

Task 3: Self-Assessment Rubric

Tự chấm mock IC meeting theo 5 tiêu chí:

  • Pitch Structure (1-5): Có opening rõ ràng, thesis rõ ràng, market data, execution plan, risk mitigation, closing recommendation?
  • Q&A Defense (1-5): Acknowledge concerns, provide evidence, restate recommendation?
  • Confidence (1-5): Tự tin, calm, không panic khi address tough questions?
  • Data-Driven (1-5): Dùng số liệu cụ thể để support recommendation?
  • Clarity (1-5): Rõ ràng, dễ hiểu, không quá technical?

Reflection 3 dòng

  • Good: 1 điểm bạn đã làm tốt.
  • Improve: 1 điểm cần cải thiện.
  • Keep: 1 kỹ thuật bạn sẽ tiếp tục dùng.
Reflection mẫu

Good: Tôi đã acknowledge concerns trước khi address, tạo cảm giác lắng nghe. Improve: Tôi cần dùng nhiều data hơn để support recommendation. Keep: "I understand your concern, and here's how we address it..."

Common Mistakes

4 lỗi thường gặp trong IC meeting

Tránh các lỗi này để mock IC meeting của bạn chuyên nghiệp và thuyết phục.

Tránh

Pitch không có cấu trúc rõ ràng, nhảy lung tung giữa các phần.

Nên

"Let me walk you through the investment case. I'll cover market opportunity, execution plan, risk profile, and recommendation."

Tránh

Trả lời Q&A mà không acknowledge concern: "No, that's not a problem."

Nên

"I understand your concern about construction delays, and here's how we address it: fixed-price contracts, contingency buffer, proven track record."

Tránh

Recommendation không rõ ràng: "We could maybe consider moving forward."

Nên

"My recommendation is clear: we should approve Phase 2 funding by end of month to capture the pricing advantage."

Tránh

Panic khi gặp tough question, mất bình tĩnh.

Nên

Pause, acknowledge concern, provide evidence calmly: "That's a valid concern. The data shows..."

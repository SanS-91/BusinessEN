Executive Leadership Communication

Day 73 tập trung vào kỹ năng truyền đạt tầm nhìn và dẫn dắt tổ chức ở cấp executive. Bạn sẽ học cách communicate vision, drive organizational alignment, engage stakeholders, và lead through change — những kỹ năng cốt lõi của nhà lãnh đạo cấp cao.

Leadership communication không phải là ra lệnh. Đó là truyền cảm hứng, tạo sự đồng thuận, và dẫn dắt mọi người qua sự thay đổi. Một nhà lãnh đạo giỏi không chỉ có tầm nhìn — họ có khả năng biến tầm nhìn thành hiện thực thông qua người khác.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Leadership Terms

8 thuật ngữ leadership cốt lõi cho executive leadership communication. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

leadership communication

/ˈliːdərʃɪp kəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃn/

Giao tiếp lãnh đạo

Ghi chú: Leadership communication là kỹ năng truyền đạt tầm nhìn, giá trị và định hướng ở cấp lãnh đạo. Khác với operational communication.
  • Collocation: leadership communication, effective leadership communication, leadership communication skills
  • Example: Effective leadership communication is essential for driving organizational alignment.
  • Native alternative: leadership communication, executive communication

organizational alignment

/ˌɔːrɡənəˈzeɪʃənl əˈlaɪnmənt/

Sự đồng thuận tổ chức

Ghi chú: Organizational alignment là sự đồng thuận của toàn bộ tổ chức về vision, strategy và priorities. Là nền tảng của execution.
  • Collocation: organizational alignment, achieve organizational alignment, drive organizational alignment
  • Example: Our priority is to achieve organizational alignment around the new strategic direction.
  • Native alternative: organizational alignment, strategic alignment

change management

/tʃeɪndʒ ˈmænɪdʒmənt/

Quản lý sự thay đổi

Ghi chú: Change management là quá trình dẫn dắt tổ chức qua sự thay đổi. Bao gồm communication, training, và stakeholder engagement.
  • Collocation: change management, change management strategy, lead change management
  • Example: Leading through change requires strong change management and clear communication.
  • Native alternative: change management, transformation management

stakeholder engagement

/ˈsteɪkhoʊldər ɪnˈɡeɪdʒmənt/

Thu hút stakeholders

Ghi chú: Stakeholder engagement là quá trình thu hút và tương tác với các bên liên quan. Quan trọng để có sự đồng thuận.
  • Collocation: stakeholder engagement, stakeholder engagement strategy, drive stakeholder engagement
  • Example: Effective stakeholder engagement is critical for successful implementation of strategic initiatives.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder engagement, stakeholder involvement

vision alignment

/ˈvɪʒn əˈlaɪnmənt/

Sự đồng thuận về tầm nhìn

Ghi chú: Vision alignment là sự đồng thuận của mọi người về tầm nhìn chiến lược. Là bước đầu tiên trước khi execute.
  • Collocation: vision alignment, achieve vision alignment, drive vision alignment
  • Example: We need to achieve vision alignment across all departments before moving to execution.
  • Native alternative: vision alignment, strategic vision alignment

cross-functional leadership

/krɔːs ˈfʌŋkʃənl ˈliːdərʃɪp/

Lãnh đạo liên phòng ban

Ghi chú: Cross-functional leadership là khả năng lãnh đạo qua nhiều phòng ban. Đòi hỏi influence mà không cần authority trực tiếp.
  • Collocation: cross-functional leadership, cross-functional leadership skills, demonstrate cross-functional leadership
  • Example: Cross-functional leadership requires strong communication and influence skills.
  • Native alternative: cross-functional leadership, matrix leadership

executive sponsorship

/ɪɡˈzekjətɪv ˈspɑːnsərʃɪp/

Sự bảo trợ của lãnh đạo

Ghi chú: Executive sponsorship là sự ủng hộ và bảo trợ từ cấp lãnh đạo cao nhất. Quan trọng để có resources và authority.
  • Collocation: executive sponsorship, secure executive sponsorship, executive sponsorship is critical
  • Example: Executive sponsorship is critical for the success of this strategic initiative.
  • Native alternative: executive sponsorship, senior leadership support

cultural transformation

/ˈkʌltʃərəl ˌtrænsfərˈmeɪʃn/

Chuyển đổi văn hóa

Ghi chú: Cultural transformation là quá trình thay đổi văn hóa tổ chức. Là phần khó nhất của change management.
  • Collocation: cultural transformation, drive cultural transformation, lead cultural transformation
  • Example: Leading cultural transformation requires consistent communication and role modeling.
  • Native alternative: cultural transformation, organizational culture change
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

4 mẫu câu leadership: communicating vision, driving alignment, leading through change, engaging stakeholders.

As a leader, my priority is to...

Công thức: As a leader, my priority is to + leadership action. Dùng để tuyên bố vai trò và ưu tiên lãnh đạo.
Communicating vision

As a leader, my priority is to communicate our vision clearly and ensure organizational alignment.

Substitution drill

Thay action: drive cross-functional collaboration / lead cultural transformation / secure executive sponsorship.

To drive organizational alignment, we need to...

Công thức: To drive organizational alignment, we need to + action. Dùng để nêu các bước tạo sự đồng thuận.
Driving alignment

To drive organizational alignment, we need to ensure every team understands the strategic vision and their role in achieving it.

Substitution drill

Thay action: communicate consistently / engage stakeholders early / align incentives with strategy.

Leading through change requires...

Công thức: Leading through change requires + leadership qualities. Dùng để nhấn mạnh các yếu tố cần thiết khi dẫn dắt thay đổi.
Leading through change

Leading through change requires clear communication, stakeholder engagement, and consistent follow-through.

Substitution drill

Thay qualities: empathy and transparency / patience and persistence / vision and execution.

Our success depends on...

Công thức: Our success depends on + key success factors. Dùng để kết thúc với call to action và nhấn mạnh yếu tố thành công.
Engaging stakeholders

Our success depends on our ability to engage stakeholders, align the organization, and execute with discipline.

Substitution drill

Thay factors: cross-functional collaboration / executive sponsorship / cultural transformation.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Leadership Address

Nghe một bài leadership address mẫu 5 phút. Đây là mô hình để bạn tái hiện với vai trò lãnh đạo của mình.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm vision và leadership priorities.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện 4 patterns: communicating vision, driving alignment, leading through change, engaging stakeholders.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích: giọng điệu leadership, cấu trúc bài nói, tầm nhìn.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm.

Transcript

[Opening]Team, as a leader, my priority is to communicate our vision clearly and ensure organizational alignment.

[Vision]Our strategic vision is to become the leading mixed-use developer in the region, known for quality and innovation.

[Alignment]To drive organizational alignment, we need to ensure every team understands this vision and their role in achieving it.

[Change]Leading through change requires clear communication, stakeholder engagement, and consistent follow-through.

[Engagement]We are undergoing a cultural transformation, and I need your commitment to embrace this change.

[Closing]Our success depends on our ability to engage stakeholders, align the organization, and execute with discipline. Together, we will make this vision a reality. Thank you.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: What is the speaker's priority as a leader?
  • Q2: What is the strategic vision?
  • Q3: What is needed to drive organizational alignment?
  • Q4: What does leading through change require?
  • Q5: What does success depend on?

Sample Answers

1. To communicate the vision clearly and ensure organizational alignment.
2. To become the leading mixed-use developer in the region.
3. Ensuring every team understands the vision and their role.
4. Clear communication, stakeholder engagement, and consistent follow-through.
5. Ability to engage stakeholders, align the organization, and execute with discipline.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Vision

As a leader, my priority is to communicate our vision clearly and ensure organizational alignment.

Shadow 2 · Change

Leading through change requires clear communication, stakeholder engagement, and consistent follow-through.

Shadow 3 · Success

Our success depends on our ability to engage stakeholders, align the organization, and execute with discipline.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: leader, priority, vision, alignment, change, stakeholders, success.
  • Giọng chắc chắn, truyền cảm hứng: "As a LEADER, my PRIORITY is..."
  • Giọng kêu gọi khi nói về commitment: "I need YOUR commitment..."
  • Ngắt nghỉ sau mỗi ý leadership để tạo impact.
  • Kết thúc bằng giọng tự tin và truyền cảm hứng.

Speaking · Leadership Address

Luyện 3 vòng: từ communicating vision đến full leadership address. Mục tiêu là thể hiện executive leadership presence.

Round 1 · 60 giây (Vision & Alignment)

Chỉ trình bày leadership priority, vision và organizational alignment.

Team, as a leader, my priority is to communicate our vision clearly and ensure organizational alignment. Our strategic vision is to become the leading mixed-use developer in the region, known for quality and innovation. To drive organizational alignment, we need to ensure every team understands this vision and their role in achieving it.

Round 2 · 90 giây (Change & Engagement)

Thêm leading through change và stakeholder engagement.

Team, as a leader, my priority is to communicate our vision clearly and ensure organizational alignment. Our strategic vision is to become the leading mixed-use developer in the region. To drive organizational alignment, we need to ensure every team understands this vision and their role. Leading through change requires clear communication, stakeholder engagement, and consistent follow-through. We are undergoing a cultural transformation, and I need your commitment to embrace this change.

Round 3 · 3 phút (Full Leadership Address)

Trình bày leadership address hoàn chỉnh: vision, alignment, change, engagement, closing.

Team, as a leader, my priority is to communicate our vision clearly and ensure organizational alignment. Our strategic vision is to become the leading mixed-use developer in the region, known for quality and innovation. To drive organizational alignment, we need to ensure every team understands this vision and their role in achieving it. Leading through change requires clear communication, stakeholder engagement, and consistent follow-through. We are undergoing a cultural transformation, and I need your commitment to embrace this change. Our success depends on our ability to engage stakeholders, align the organization, and execute with discipline. Together, we will make this vision a reality. Thank you.

Common Mistakes

3 lỗi thường gặp khi leadership communication

Tránh các lỗi này để bài leadership address của bạn executive và truyền cảm hứng.

Tránh

Không có vision rõ ràng: "We need to do better."

Nên

Our strategic vision is to become the leading mixed-use developer in the region, known for quality and innovation.

Tránh

Không nhấn mạnh alignment: "Everyone should know what to do."

Nên

To drive organizational alignment, we need to ensure every team understands this vision and their role in achieving it.

Tránh

Không có call to action: "That's all. Thanks."

Nên

Our success depends on our ability to engage stakeholders, align the organization, and execute with discipline. Together, we will make this vision a reality.

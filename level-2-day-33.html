Building Consensus

Day 33 dạy bạn nghệ thuật xây dựng sự đồng thuận: tìm điểm chung, tạo alignment, và facilitate agreement giữa các stakeholders với maturity và skill.

Consensus không phải là everyone agrees. Đó là everyone feels heard, understood, and committed to moving forward together — even if they don't fully agree.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Consensus Terms

10 thuật ngữ consensus cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

consensus

/kənˈsen.səs/

Sự đồng thuận

Ghi chú: Consensus là sự đồng thuận chung. Không phải everyone agrees, mà là everyone feels heard and committed to moving forward.
  • Collocation: build consensus, reach consensus, consensus building
  • Example: We need to build consensus among all stakeholders before moving forward.
  • Native alternative: agreement, consensus

alignment

/əˈlan.mənt/

Sự đồng bộ, thống nhất

Ghi chú: Alignment là sự thống nhất về hướng đi, mục tiêu. Different từ consensus - alignment focuses on direction, not necessarily full agreement.
  • Collocation: ensure alignment, strategic alignment, alignment across teams
  • Example: We need to ensure alignment across all departments before launching the initiative.
  • Native alternative: alignment, strategic alignment

buy-in

/ˈbaɪ.ɪn/

Sự ủng hộ, đồng ý

Ghi chú: Buy-in là sự ủng hộ và commitment từ stakeholders. Getting buy-in là bước quan trọng trước khi implement changes.
  • Collocation: get buy-in, stakeholder buy-in, buy-in from stakeholders
  • Example: We need to get buy-in from all stakeholders before implementing this change.
  • Native alternative: agreement, support, buy-in

stakeholder

/ˈstek.hoʊl.dər/

Các bên liên quan

Ghi chú: Stakeholders là tất cả những người bị ảnh hưởng bởi decision hoặc có interest trong outcome.
  • Collocation: key stakeholders, stakeholder interests, stakeholder engagement
  • Example: We need to consider the interests of all stakeholders in this decision.
  • Native alternative: stakeholders, interested parties

compromise

/kɒm.prə.maɪz/

Sự thỏa hiệp

Ghi chú: Compromise là sự thỏa hiệp - mỗi bên nhượng bộ một phần để đạt được agreement. Different từ consensus.
  • Collocation: reach a compromise, find a compromise, compromise solution
  • Example: We need to find a compromise that works for everyone involved.
  • Native alternative: compromise, middle ground

common ground

/ˈkɒm.ən ɡraʊnd/

Điểm chung

Ghi chú: Common ground là điểm chung giữa các bên. Finding common ground là bước đầu tiên để build consensus.
  • Collocation: find common ground, common ground between, build on common ground
  • Example: Let's find common ground before we discuss the differences.
  • Native alternative: common ground, shared interests

facilitate

/fəˈsɪl.ɪ.tet/

Tạo điều kiện, hỗ trợ

Ghi chú: Facilitate là tạo điều kiện để quá trình diễn ra smoothly. Facilitator giúp group reach consensus.
  • Collocation: facilitate discussion, facilitate consensus, facilitate agreement
  • Example: My role is to facilitate the discussion and help us reach consensus.
  • Native alternative: facilitate, facilitate

mediate

/miː.di.eɪt/

Hòa giải, trung gian

Ghi chú: Mediate là đóng vai trò trung gian để help two parties reach agreement. Different từ facilitate.
  • Collocation: mediate between, mediate conflict, mediate agreement
  • Example: We need someone to mediate between the two parties to reach an agreement.
  • Native alternative: mediate, mediate

negotiate

/nɪˈɡə.ʃi.eɪt/

Đàm phán, thương lượng

Ghi chú: Negotiate là đàm phán để reach agreement. Different từ mediate - negotiate là direct negotiation between parties.
  • Collocation: negotiate terms, negotiate agreement, negotiate deal
  • Example: We need to negotiate the terms and conditions before finalizing the agreement.
  • Native alternative: negotiate, negotiate

persuade

/pəˈsweɪd/

Thuyết phục

Ghi chú: Persuade là thuyết phục ai đó làm gì đó. Different từ convince - persuade focuses on action, convince focuses on belief.
  • Collocation: persuade someone to, persuade the board, persuade stakeholders
  • Example: We need to persuade the board to approve this proposal.
  • Native alternative: persuade, convince
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

7 mẫu câu building consensus cốt lõi. Mỗi mẫu có công thức, ví dụ, substitution drill, common mistake và native alternative.

Let's find common ground...

Công thức: Let's find common ground + before we discuss differences. Dùng để bắt đầu building consensus bằng cách tìm điểm chung.
Finding common ground

Let's find common ground before we discuss the differences.

Substitution drill

Thay approach: Let's start with what we agree on / Let's identify our shared goals first.

Common mistake

Tránh: "Let's just agree and move on." (Forcing agreement) Nên: "Let's find common ground..."

I understand your concern, and...

Công thức: I understand your concern about + concern, and + solution. Dùng để acknowledge concerns trước khi propose solutions.
Acknowledging concerns

I understand your concern about the timeline, and here's how we can address it.

Substitution drill

Thay concern và solution: about the budget / about the risks / here's our mitigation plan.

Common mistake

Tránh: "You're wrong." (Dismissive) Nên: "I understand your concern, and..."

What if we...

Công thức: What if we + propose solution + that addresses both concerns. Dùng để propose solutions một cách collaborative.
Proposing solutions

What if we try a different approach that addresses both concerns?

Substitution drill

Thay solution: What if we compromise on the timeline / What if we find a middle ground.

Common mistake

Tránh: "We should do it my way." (Dictatorial) Nên: "What if we..."

How can we ensure everyone's voice is heard?

Công thức: How can we ensure everyone's voice is heard + in this decision. Dùng để ensure inclusive process.
Ensuring inclusion

How can we ensure everyone's voice is heard in this decision?

Substitution drill

Thay approach: How do we get everyone's input / How do we make sure all perspectives are considered.

Common mistake

Tránh: "I already know what we should do." (Exclusive) Nên: "How can we ensure everyone's voice is heard?"

Let me summarize what I'm hearing...

Công thức: Let me summarize what I'm hearing + to make sure I understand correctly. Dùng để confirm understanding và show active listening.
Confirming understanding

Let me summarize what I'm hearing to make sure I understand correctly.

Substitution drill

Thay approach: Let me recap what we've discussed / Let me make sure I understand the key points.

Common mistake

Tránh: Assuming you understand without confirming. Nên: "Let me summarize..."

Based on what we've discussed...

Công thức: Based on what we've discussed, + I think we can reach consensus on + topic. Dùng để propose consensus based on discussion.
Proposing consensus

Based on what we've discussed, I think we can reach a consensus on the timeline.

Substitution drill

Thay topic: on the budget / on the approach / on the next steps.

Common mistake

Tránh: "I think we should..." (Without reference to discussion) Nên: "Based on what we've discussed..."

Can we agree on...

Công thức: Can we agree on + moving forward with + approach/decision. Dùng để close với clear agreement.
Closing with agreement

Can we agree on moving forward with this approach?

Substitution drill

Thay approach: on the timeline / on the budget / on the next steps.

Common mistake

Tránh: "We should do this." (Dictatorial) Nên: "Can we agree on..."

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Consensus-Building Discussion

Nghe một bài consensus-building discussion mẫu. Đây là mô hình để bạn thực hành building consensus effectively.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm consensus-building structure và stakeholder engagement.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện các patterns: common ground identification, concern acknowledgment, solution proposal.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích consensus-building techniques: inclusive language, active listening, collaborative problem-solving.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm (common ground opening, concern acknowledgment, closing agreement).

Transcript

[Opening]
Thank you all for coming today. I know we have different perspectives on the timeline, and I appreciate everyone taking the time to share their views.

[Finding Common Ground]
Let's start by finding common ground. I think we all agree that we need to deliver this project on time and within budget. Where we differ is on the approach.

[Acknowledging Concerns]
I understand your concern about the tight timeline, and I hear your concern about the budget constraints. Both are valid concerns that we need to address.

[Proposing Solution]
What if we try a phased approach that addresses both concerns? We could phase 1 focus on the critical path items, and phase 2 address the nice-to-have items. This way, we deliver on time while managing the budget.

[Closing with Agreement]
Based on what we've discussed, can we agree on moving forward with this phased approach? This addresses both the timeline and budget concerns while delivering value.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: What is the common ground identified at the beginning?
  • Q2: What are the two concerns acknowledged?
  • Q3: What solution is proposed?
  • Q4: How does the solution address both concerns?
  • Q5: What is the final agreement proposed?

Sample Answers

1. The common ground is that everyone agrees the project needs to be delivered on time and within budget.
2. The two concerns are: tight timeline and budget constraints.
3. The solution proposed is a phased approach: phase 1 for critical path items, phase 2 for nice-to-have items.
4. The phased approach delivers on time (addressing timeline concern) while managing the budget (addressing budget concern).
5. The final agreement proposed is moving forward with the phased approach.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Common ground opening

Let's start by finding common ground. I think we all agree that we need to deliver this project on time and within budget.

Shadow 2 · Concern acknowledgment

I understand your concern about the tight timeline, and I hear your concern about the budget constraints.

Shadow 3 · Closing agreement

Based on what we've discussed, can we agree on moving forward with this phased approach?

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: common ground, understand your concern, based on what we've discussed.
  • Giọng inclusive và collaborative khi tìm common ground.
  • Giọng empathetic khi acknowledge concerns.
  • Giọng confident và collaborative khi propose solution và close with agreement.
  • Pause sau mỗi key point để stakeholders tiêu hóa thông tin.
Block 4 · 20 phút

Speaking · Consensus-Building Practice

Luyện 3 vòng tăng dần: find common ground → acknowledge concerns + propose solutions → full consensus-building discussion. Mục tiêu: tự tin facilitate agreement effectively.

Round 1 · Find Common Ground

Practice finding common ground và establishing shared goals. Mục tiêu: establish common ground và create collaborative atmosphere.

Thank you all for coming today. I know we have different perspectives on this issue, and I appreciate everyone taking the time to share their views. Let's start by finding common ground. I think we all agree that we need to deliver this project successfully and on time. Where we differ is on the approach. Let's identify what we agree on first, and then we can discuss the differences.

Round 2 · Acknowledge Concerns + Propose Solutions

Practice acknowledging concerns và proposing solutions. Mục tiêu: acknowledge concerns với empathy và propose collaborative solutions.

I understand your concern about the timeline, and I hear your concern about the budget constraints. Both are valid concerns that we need to address. What if we try a phased approach that addresses both concerns? We could phase 1 focus on the critical path items, and phase 2 address the nice-to-have items. This way, we deliver on time while managing the budget. This approach addresses both concerns while delivering value.

Round 3 · Full Consensus-Building Discussion

Full consensus-building discussion: find common ground → acknowledge concerns → propose solutions → close with agreement. Mục tiêu: complete, confident consensus-building discussion.

Thank you all for coming today. I know we have different perspectives on the timeline, and I appreciate everyone taking the time to share their views. Let's start by finding common ground. I think we all agree that we need to deliver this project on time and within budget. Where we differ is on the approach. I understand your concern about the tight timeline, and I hear your concern about the budget constraints. Both are valid concerns. What if we try a phased approach that addresses both concerns? Phase 1 focuses on critical path items, and phase 2 addresses nice-to-have items. This way, we deliver on time while managing the budget. Based on what we've discussed, can we agree on moving forward with this phased approach? This addresses both the timeline and budget concerns while delivering value.

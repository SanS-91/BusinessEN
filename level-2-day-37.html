International Business Negotiation

Day 37 tập trung vào đàm phán quốc tế: chiến lược đàm phán xuyên văn hóa, nhận thức văn hóa, và chuẩn bị đàm phán với đối tác quốc tế. Bạn sẽ thực hành một buổi đàm phán quốc tế 10 phút với đối tác nước ngoài.

Đàm phán quốc tế không chỉ là ngôn ngữ — đó là hiểu văn hóa, hiểu cách đối tác suy nghĩ, và điều chỉnh cách tiếp cận. Người đàm phán giỏi không chỉ nói tiếng Anh — họ hiểu cách người khác nghe và phản hồi.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Negotiation Terms

8 thuật ngữ đàm phán quốc tế cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

cultural awareness

/ˈkltʃərəl əˈwɛənəs/

Nhận thức văn hóa

Ghi chú: Cultural awareness là hiểu biết về văn hóa của đối tác. Quan trọng để tránh hiểu lầm và xây dựng mối quan hệ.
  • Collocation: cultural awareness training, develop cultural awareness, cultural awareness is important
  • Example: Cultural awareness is essential for successful international negotiations.
  • Native alternative: cultural sensitivity, cross-cultural understanding

negotiation strategy

/nɪˌɡoʊʃiˈeɪʃn ˈstrætədʒi/

Chiến lược đàm phán

Ghi chú: Negotiation strategy là chiến lược đàm phán. Có thể là win-win, win-lose, hoặc compromise.
  • Collocation: negotiation strategy, develop strategy, negotiation approach
  • Example: Our negotiation strategy is based on win-win principles and long-term partnership.
  • Native alternative: negotiation approach, bargaining strategy

win-win

/wɪn wɪn/

Cùng thắng

Ghi chú: Win-win là giải pháp mà cả hai bên cùng có lợi. Đây là nguyên tắc cơ bản trong đàm phán quốc tế.
  • Collocation: win-win solution, win-win situation, win-win outcome
  • Example: We believe in win-win solutions where both parties benefit from the partnership.
  • Native alternative: mutually beneficial, win-win

compromise

/ˈkɑːmprəmaɪz/

Thỏa hiệp

Ghi chú: Compromise là thỏa hiệp — cả hai bên nhượng bộ để đạt thỏa thuận. Khác với concession (nhượng bộ đơn phương).
  • Collocation: reach a compromise, willing to compromise, compromise solution
  • Example: We are willing to compromise on the timeline to reach a mutually beneficial agreement.
  • Native alternative: compromise, middle ground

concession

/kənˈseʃn/

Nhượng bộ

Ghi chú: Concession là nhượng bộ — một bên nhượng bộ để đạt thỏa thuận. Thường dùng trong đàm phán.
  • Collocation: make a concession, major concession, concession on terms
  • Example: We are prepared to make a concession on the payment terms to facilitate the deal.
  • Native alternative: concession, concession

cultural sensitivity

/ˈkʌltʃərəl ˌsensəˈtɪvəti/

Nhạy cảm văn hóa

Ghi chú: Cultural sensitivity là nhạy cảm với văn hóa. Quan trọng khi làm việc với đối tác từ các nền văn hóa khác nhau.
  • Collocation: cultural sensitivity, show cultural sensitivity, cultural sensitivity training
  • Example: We need to show cultural sensitivity when negotiating with our Japanese partners.
  • Native alternative: cultural awareness, cross-cultural sensitivity

cross-cultural

/ˌkrɒs ˈkltʃərəl/

Xuyên văn hóa

Ghi chú: Cross-cultural là xuyên văn hóa. Cross-cultural communication là giao tiếp xuyên văn hóa.
  • Collocation: cross-cultural communication, cross-cultural team, cross-cultural negotiation
  • Example: Cross-cultural communication requires understanding different business cultures.
  • Native alternative: cross-cultural, intercultural

negotiation preparation

/nɪˌɡoʊʃiˈeɪʃn ˌprepəˈreɪʃn/

Chuẩn bị đàm phán

Ghi chú: Negotiation preparation là chuẩn bị cho đàm phán. Bao gồm research, strategy, và preparation.
  • Collocation: negotiation preparation, proper preparation, thorough preparation
  • Example: Proper negotiation preparation is essential for successful outcomes.
  • Native alternative: negotiation prep, preparation
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

6 mẫu câu đàm phán quốc tế: opening negotiation, stating position, making concessions, finding common ground, handling cultural differences, closing negotiation.

Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today...

Công thức: Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today + expectation. Dùng để mở đầu đàm phán một cách lịch sự.
Opening negotiation

Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today. We look forward to a productive discussion.

Substitution drill

Thay expectation: a fruitful discussion / a mutually beneficial discussion / a productive partnership.

Our position is...

Công thức: Our position is + position statement. Dùng để nêu rõ vị thế của mình.
Stating position

Our position is that we need a 20 percent equity stake to justify the investment risk.

Substitution drill

Thay position: we need a 15% return / we need board approval / we need regulatory approval.

We are willing to make a concession on...

Công thức: We are willing to make a concession on + area + to + goal. Dùng để đề xuất nhượng bộ.
Making concessions

We are willing to make a concession on the payment terms to facilitate the deal.

Substitution drill

Thay area: the timeline / the equity stake / the governance structure. Thay goal: reach agreement / move forward / close the deal.

Let's find common ground on...

Công thức: Let's find common ground on + area + then + next step. Dùng để tìm điểm chung.
Finding common ground

Let's find common ground on the valuation and then we can discuss the governance structure.

Substitution drill

Thay area: the timeline / the equity structure / the governance. Thay next step: discuss terms / move forward / close the deal.

We understand that in your culture...

Công thức: We understand that in your culture + cultural norm + implication. Dùng để thể hiện hiểu biết văn hóa.
Handling cultural differences

We understand that in your culture, building relationships is important before discussing business.

Substitution drill

Thay cultural norm: consensus is important / hierarchy is respected / relationships come first. Thay implication: we respect that / we understand that / we appreciate that.

We believe this is a win-win opportunity...

Công thức: We believe this is a win-win opportunity + benefit statement. Dùng để kết thúc đàm phán với win-win.
Closing negotiation

We believe this is a win-win opportunity where both parties benefit from the partnership.

Substitution drill

Thay benefit: both sides benefit / both parties gain / mutual benefit. Thay partnership: the deal / the partnership / the collaboration.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · International Negotiation with Japanese Partner

Nghe một buổi đàm phán quốc tế mẫu 10 phút với đối tác Nhật Bản. Đây là mô hình để bạn thực hành đàm phán quốc tế.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm negotiation flow và cultural awareness.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện các patterns: opening, stating position, making concessions, finding common ground.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích cultural sensitivity và win-win approach.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm (opening, position statement, closing).

Transcript

[Opening]Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today. We look forward to a productive discussion.

[Stating Position]Our position is that we need a 20 percent equity stake to justify the investment risk.

[Cultural Awareness]We understand that in your culture, building relationships is important before discussing business.

[Making Concessions]We are willing to make a concession on the payment terms to facilitate the deal.

[Finding Common Ground]Let's find common ground on the valuation and then we can discuss the governance structure.

[Closing]We believe this is a win-win opportunity where both parties benefit from the partnership. Thank you.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: What is the opening statement?
  • Q2: What is their position on equity stake?
  • Q3: What cultural awareness do they show?
  • Q4: What concession are they willing to make?
  • Q5: What is the closing statement?

Sample Answers

1. Opening: Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today. We look forward to a productive discussion.
2. Position: Need 20% equity stake to justify investment risk.
3. Cultural awareness: Understand that building relationships is important before discussing business.
4. Concession: Willing to make concession on payment terms to facilitate the deal.
5. Closing: This is a win-win opportunity where both parties benefit from the partnership.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Opening

Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today. We look forward to a productive discussion.

Shadow 2 · Position

Our position is that we need a 20 percent equity stake to justify the investment risk.

Shadow 3 · Closing

We believe this is a win-win opportunity where both parties benefit from the partnership.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Giọng lịch sự, tôn trọng khi opening: "Thank you for taking the time..."
  • Giọng chắc chắn khi stating position: "Our position is..."
  • Giọng hiểu biết khi showing cultural awareness: "We understand that..."
  • Giọng hợp tác khi finding common ground: "Let's find common ground..."
Block 4 · 20 phút

Speaking · International Negotiation 10 Minutes

Luyện 3 vòng tăng dần: opening → negotiation → full negotiation. Mục tiêu: tự tin đàm phán với đối tác quốc tế.

Round 1 · Opening (3 phút)

Thực hành mở đầu đàm phán: cultural awareness, relationship building, setting expectations.

Opening: Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today. We look forward to a productive discussion.

Cultural awareness: We understand that in your culture, building relationships is important before discussing business.

Expectation: We believe this is a win-win opportunity where both parties benefit from the partnership.

Round 2 · Negotiation (5 phút)

Thực hành đàm phán: stating position, making concessions, finding common ground.

Position: Our position is that we need a 20 percent equity stake to justify the investment risk.

Concession: We are willing to make a concession on the payment terms to facilitate the deal.

Common ground: Let's find common ground on the valuation and then we can discuss the governance structure.

Round 3 · Full Negotiation (10 phút)

Thực hành đàm phán hoàn chỉnh: opening, position, concessions, common ground, closing.

Opening: Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today. We look forward to a productive discussion.

Position: Our position is that we need a 20 percent equity stake to justify the investment risk.

Cultural awareness: We understand that in your culture, building relationships is important before discussing business.

Concession: We are willing to make a concession on the payment terms to facilitate the deal.

Common ground: Let's find common ground on the valuation and then we can discuss the governance structure.

Closing: We believe this is a win-win opportunity where both parties benefit from the partnership. Thank you.

Recording Task

Ghi âm Round 3 hoàn chỉnh: full international negotiation 10 phút. Đây là buổi đàm phán hoàn chỉnh.

Self-review

  • Opening có lịch sự và tôn trọng văn hóa không?
  • Position có rõ ràng và có lý do không?
  • Concessions có hợp lý và có mục đích không?
  • Common ground có hợp tác và win-win không?
  • Closing có win-win và cảm ơn không?

Task 3: Reflection 3 dòng

Sau khi hoàn thành, hãy viết ngắn gọn 3 dòng:

  • Good: 1 điểm bạn đã làm tốt.
  • Improve: 1 điểm cần cải thiện.
  • Keep: 1 kỹ thuật bạn sẽ tiếp tục dùng.
Reflection mẫu

Good: Tôi đã thể hiện cultural awareness và tôn trọng văn hóa đối tác. Improve: Tôi cần rõ ràng hơn về position và concessions. Keep: "We believe this is a win-win opportunity..."

Common Mistakes

4 lỗi thường gặp trong đàm phán quốc tế:

1. Tránh: Không thể hiện cultural awareness: "Let's get down to business."

Nên: "Thank you for taking the time. We understand that building relationships is important."

2. Tránh: Position không rõ ràng: "We want a good deal."

Nên: "Our position is that we need a 20 percent equity stake to justify the investment risk."

3. Tránh: Concessions không có mục đích: "We can compromise."

Nên: "We are willing to make a concession on the payment terms to facilitate the deal."

4. Tránh: Closing không win-win: "This is our final offer."

Nên: "We believe this is a win-win opportunity where both parties benefit from the partnership."

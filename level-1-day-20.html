Level 1 Week 3 · Finance & Investment Day 20 / 30 90 phút Speaking topic: Risk Assessment

Risk Analysis

Day 20 đưa bạn vào trung tâm của quản trị rủi ro dự án. Bạn sẽ học ngôn ngữ của Construction Risk, Legal Risk, Sales Risk, Funding Risk, Market Risk và Execution Risk — bộ công cụ để trình bày một risk assessment thuyết phục với hội đồng đầu tư và ban lãnh đạo.

Một risk assessment tốt không phải là danh sách những điều có thể sai. Đó là một lập luận logic: rủi ro nào quan trọng nhất, khả năng xảy ra và tác động ra sao, và chúng ta đã làm gì để giảm thiểu chúng. Người nói tốt biến nỗi sợ thành sự tự tin có kiểm soát.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Risk Terms

8 thuật ngữ risk cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative. Đây là bộ từ vựng bạn sẽ dùng để trình bày risk assessment.

Construction Risk

/kənˈstrʌkʃn rɪsk/

Rủi ro xây dựng

Ghi chú: Construction risk là rủi ro liên quan đến quá trình xây dựng — chi phí vượt dự toán, chậm tiến độ, chất lượng.
  • Collocation: construction risk, construction cost escalation, construction delay
  • Example: The main construction risk is cost escalation and delays, which we mitigate with fixed-price contracts.
  • Native alternative: build risk, development risk

Legal Risk

/ˈliːɡl rɪsk/

Rủi ro pháp lý

Ghi chú: Legal risk là rủi ro liên quan đến pháp lý — chậm giấy phép, thay đổi quy định, tranh chấp.
  • Collocation: legal risk, regulatory risk, legal exposure
  • Example: The main legal risk is delay in obtaining permits, which we mitigate by engaging legal counsel early.
  • Native alternative: regulatory risk, compliance risk

Sales Risk

/seɪlz rɪsk/

Rủi ro bán hàng

Ghi chú: Sales risk là rủi ro liên quan đến bán hàng — hấp thụ chậm hơn kỳ vọng, giá bán thấp hơn dự kiến.
  • Collocation: sales risk, absorption risk, sales velocity risk
  • Example: The main sales risk is slower-than-expected absorption, which we mitigate by phasing the project to match sales.
  • Native alternative: demand risk, market absorption risk

Funding Risk

/ˈfʌndɪŋ rɪsk/

Rủi ro tài trợ vốn

Ghi chú: Funding risk là rủi ro liên quan đến nguồn vốn — khó huy động vốn, lãi suất tăng, giải ngân chậm.
  • Collocation: funding risk, financing risk, liquidity risk
  • Example: The main funding risk is difficulty securing financing, which we mitigate by securing committed financing upfront.
  • Native alternative: financing risk, capital risk

Market Risk

/ˈmɑːrkɪt rɪsk/

Rủi ro thị trường

Ghi chú: Market risk là rủi ro liên quan đến thị trường — suy thoái, thay đổi cung cầu, biến động giá.
  • Collocation: market risk, market downturn, market volatility
  • Example: The main market risk is a market downturn affecting demand, which we mitigate by targeting real demand rather than speculation.
  • Native alternative: market downturn risk, cyclical risk

Execution Risk

/ˌeksɪˈkjuːʃn rɪsk/

Rủi ro thực thi

Ghi chú: Execution risk là rủi ro liên quan đến năng lực thực thi — phối hợp kém, thiếu năng lực, quản lý yếu.
  • Collocation: execution risk, implementation risk, operational risk
  • Example: The main execution risk is poor coordination between teams, which we mitigate with clear governance and regular reviews.
  • Native alternative: implementation risk, delivery risk

mitigation

/ˌmɪtɪˈɡeɪʃn/

Biện pháp giảm thiểu

Ghi chú: Mitigation là biện pháp giảm thiểu rủi ro. Luôn nêu mitigation kèm theo mỗi rủi ro khi trình bày.
  • Collocation: risk mitigation, mitigation plan, mitigation measures
  • Example: We mitigate construction risk by using fixed-price contracts and maintaining a 10 percent contingency buffer.
  • Native alternative: risk reduction, risk management

risk appetite

/rɪsk ˈæpɪtaɪt/

Mức chấp nhận rủi ro

Ghi chú: Risk appetite là mức rủi ro tối đa mà tổ chức chấp nhận. Dùng để kết luận risk assessment.
  • Collocation: risk appetite, within risk appetite, risk tolerance
  • Example: After mitigation, all residual risks are within our risk appetite, which is moderate for this project type.
  • Native alternative: risk tolerance, risk threshold
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

6 mẫu câu giúp bạn trình bày risk assessment theo từng phần: risk identification, mitigation, likelihood/impact, worst case, risk appetite và residual risk.

The main risk is..., which could lead to...

Công thức: The main risk is + rủi ro + which could lead to + hậu quả. Dùng để nêu rủi ro và hậu quả tiềm tàng.
Risk statement

The main risk is construction cost escalation, which could lead to budget overruns and project delays.

Substitution drill

Thay rủi ro và hậu quả: legal delays, which could lead to construction start delays / sales risk, which could lead to cash flow pressure.

Common mistake

Tránh nêu rủi ro mà không nói hậu quả. Nên giải thích rủi ro đó ảnh hưởng thế nào đến dự án.

We mitigate this by...

Công thức: We mitigate this by + biện pháp. Dùng để nêu cách giảm thiểu rủi ro.
Mitigation statement

We mitigate this by using fixed-price contracts and maintaining a 10 percent contingency buffer.

Substitution drill

Thay biện pháp: engaging legal counsel early / phasing the project to match sales / securing committed financing upfront.

Register note

Luôn nêu mitigation kèm theo mỗi rủi ro. Nhà đầu tư muốn biết bạn kiểm soát rủi ro thế nào.

The likelihood is low/medium/high, but the impact is...

Công thức: The likelihood is + mức + but the impact is + mức. Dùng để đánh giá likelihood và impact của rủi ro.
Likelihood/impact statement

The likelihood is low, but the impact is high — so we maintain a contingency buffer to be safe.

Substitution drill

Thay mức: medium likelihood, high impact / low likelihood, low impact / high likelihood, medium impact.

Common mistake

Tránh chỉ nói rủi ro "cao" hay "thấp" chung chung. Nên tách rõ likelihood và impact.

In the worst case,...

Công thức: In the worst case, + kịch bản xấu nhất (+ nhưng vẫn kiểm soát được). Dùng để nêu kịch bản xấu nhất nhưng cho thấy vẫn kiểm soát được.
Worst case statement

In the worst case, construction costs could escalate by 20 percent, but our contingency buffer would still cover it.

Substitution drill

Thay kịch bản: sales could drop by 30%, but we have a liquidity buffer / permits could be delayed by 6 months, but we have contingency plans.

Register note

Nên nêu worst case nhưng kèm theo "but we can still handle it" — cho thấy bạn đã chuẩn bị.

Our risk appetite is...

Công thức: Our risk appetite is + mức (+ và các rủi ro đều nằm trong ngưỡng). Dùng để kết luận risk assessment.
Risk appetite statement

Our risk appetite is moderate for this project type, and all residual risks fall within that threshold.

Substitution drill

Thay mức: conservative / moderate / aggressive / within acceptable limits.

Common mistake

Tránh kết luận "no risks" hoặc "all risks are managed" mà không nêu cụ thể. Nên nêu risk appetite và residual risk.

The residual risk after mitigation is...

Công thức: The residual risk after mitigation is + mức. Dùng để nêu rủi ro còn lại sau khi đã giảm thiểu.
Residual risk statement

The residual risk after mitigation is low, which is within our risk appetite for this project type.

Substitution drill

Thay mức: low / low to medium / medium / acceptable / within tolerance.

Common mistake

Tránh chỉ nói đã mitigate mà không nói residual risk còn bao nhiêu. Nên nêu rõ mức residual risk.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Risk Assessment Presentation

Nghe một bài trình bày risk assessment mẫu. Đây là mô hình để bạn tái hiện với số liệu thật của mình.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm cấu trúc bài trình bày risk assessment.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện các patterns: main risk, mitigate, likelihood/impact, worst case, residual risk.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích cấu trúc: overview → top 5 risks → mitigation → residual risk → key messages.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm.

Transcript

[Opening & Overview]
Good morning everyone. I'd like to walk you through the risk assessment for the Riverside Township project, and explain how we plan to manage the key risks. Let me start with an overview. We have identified six main categories of risk: construction risk, legal risk, sales risk, funding risk, market risk, and execution risk. I'll walk you through the top five risks, ranked by their combined likelihood and impact.

[Risk 1: Construction]
First, construction risk. This is rated as medium likelihood but high impact. The main concern is construction cost escalation and delays. We mitigate this by using fixed-price contracts where possible, maintaining a 10 percent contingency buffer, and conducting regular progress reviews. The residual risk after mitigation is low.

[Risk 2: Legal]
Second, legal risk. This is rated as low likelihood but high impact. The main concern is delays in obtaining permits and approvals. We mitigate this by engaging legal counsel early, completing legal milestones before major construction, and maintaining regular dialogue with authorities. The residual risk after mitigation is low.

[Risk 3: Sales]
Third, sales risk. This is rated as medium likelihood and medium impact. The main concern is slower-than-expected absorption. We mitigate this by phasing the project to match sales, maintaining flexible pricing, and building a strong sales pipeline. The residual risk after mitigation is low to medium.

[Risk 4: Funding]
Fourth, funding risk. This is rated as low likelihood but high impact. The main concern is difficulty securing financing or rising interest rates. We mitigate this by securing committed financing upfront, maintaining a liquidity buffer, and using fixed-rate financing where possible. The residual risk after mitigation is low.

[Risk 5: Market]
Fifth, market risk. This is rated as medium likelihood and medium impact. The main concern is a market downturn affecting demand and pricing. We mitigate this by targeting real demand rather than speculation, maintaining a diversified product mix, and building in pricing flexibility. The residual risk after mitigation is medium.

[Key Messages & Summary]
So what are the key messages from this risk assessment? There are three. First, our top risks are well-understood and have clear mitigation plans. Second, after mitigation, all residual risks are at acceptable levels within our risk appetite. Third, we have contingency buffers and regular monitoring to catch emerging risks early. To summarize, this project has a well-structured risk management framework. The key risks are identified, mitigated, and monitored. I'm happy to take any questions.

Comprehension Questions

  • Question 1: What are the six main categories of risk identified?
  • Question 2: Which risk is rated medium likelihood but high impact, and how is it mitigated?
  • Question 3: Which risk has low likelihood but high impact, and what is the mitigation?
  • Question 4: What is the residual risk for market risk after mitigation?
  • Question 5: What are the three key messages from the risk assessment?

Sample Answers

1. The six categories are: construction risk, legal risk, sales risk, funding risk, market risk, and execution risk. 2. Construction risk is rated medium likelihood, high impact. Mitigated by fixed-price contracts, contingency buffer, and regular reviews. 3. Legal risk is rated low likelihood, high impact. Mitigated by engaging legal counsel early and completing legal milestones first. 4. The residual risk for market risk after mitigation is medium. 5. The three key messages are: risks are well-understood with clear mitigation, residual risks are within risk appetite, and contingency buffers with monitoring catch emerging risks.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Risk identification

The main risk is construction cost escalation, which could lead to budget overruns and project delays.

Shadow 2 · Mitigation

We mitigate this by using fixed-price contracts and maintaining a 10 percent contingency buffer.

Shadow 3 · Residual risk

The residual risk after mitigation is low, which is within our risk appetite for this project type.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Đọc rõ các mức: low, medium, high — nhấn vào mức độ rủi ro.
  • Đọc rõ các con số: 10 percent contingency, 20 percent cost escalation.
  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: mitigate, residual, within risk appetite.
  • Ngắt rõ giữa các phần: overview → risks → mitigation → residual → key messages.
Block 4 · 20 phút

Speaking · Risk Assessment trong 3–5 phút

Luyện 3 vòng tăng dần. Mục tiêu cuối cùng là trình bày risk assessment trong 3–5 phút theo cấu trúc: overview → top risks → mitigation → residual risk → key messages.

Speaking Structure

Bài trình bày risk assessment 3–5 phút nên đi theo 5 phần:

  1. Opening: giới thiệu risk assessment và các loại rủi ro.
  2. Top risks: các rủi ro hàng đầu với likelihood và impact.
  3. Mitigation: cách giảm thiểu từng rủi ro.
  4. Residual risk: rủi ro còn lại sau mitigation.
  5. Key messages & summary: 3 thông điệp + kết luận.

Checklist khi trình bày risk assessment

  • Có các loại rủi ro được xác định rõ ràng.
  • Có likelihood và impact cho mỗi rủi ro.
  • Có mitigation cho mỗi rủi ro.
  • Có residual risk sau mitigation.
  • Có 3 key messages rõ ràng.

Round 1 · Opening + Risk Overview + Top 2 Risks

Xây phần mở đầu và 2 rủi ro hàng đầu. Mục tiêu khoảng 1–2 phút.

Good morning. I'd like to walk you through the risk assessment for the Riverside Township project. We have identified six main categories of risk, and I'll walk you through the top five, ranked by likelihood and impact.

First, construction risk — rated medium likelihood but high impact. The main concern is cost escalation and delays. Second, legal risk — rated low likelihood but high impact. The main concern is delays in obtaining permits.

Round 2 · Thêm Mitigation + Risk Matrix

Thêm mitigation cho các rủi ro. Mục tiêu khoảng 3 phút khi nối với Round 1.

Let me explain how we mitigate these risks. For construction risk, we use fixed-price contracts where possible, maintain a 10 percent contingency buffer, and conduct regular progress reviews. For legal risk, we engage legal counsel early, complete legal milestones before major construction, and maintain regular dialogue with authorities.

For sales risk, we phase the project to match sales and maintain flexible pricing. For funding risk, we secure committed financing upfront and maintain a liquidity buffer. For market risk, we target real demand rather than speculation.

Round 3 · Full Risk Assessment 3–5 phút

Nối Round 1 + Round 2, thêm residual risk và 3 key messages. Mục tiêu trọn vẹn 3–5 phút.

So what is the residual risk after mitigation? For construction, legal, and funding risks, the residual risk is low. For sales risk, it's low to medium. For market risk, it's medium. All residual risks fall within our risk appetite.

So what are the key messages from this risk assessment? There are three. First, our top risks are well-understood and have clear mitigation plans. Second, after mitigation, all residual risks are at acceptable levels within our risk appetite. Third, we have contingency buffers and regular monitoring to catch emerging risks early.

To summarize, this project has a well-structured risk management framework. The key risks are identified, mitigated, and monitored. I'm happy to take any questions.

Recording Task

Ghi âm Round 3 — bài trình bày risk assessment 3–5 phút với số liệu thật của bạn. Nếu chưa đủ thời lượng, hãy mở rộng bằng cách giải thích kỹ hơn từng mitigation hoặc thêm ví dụ.

Self-review

  • Bạn có các loại rủi ro được xác định rõ ràng không?
  • Bạn có likelihood và impact cho mỗi rủi ro không?
  • Bạn có mitigation cho mỗi rủi ro không?
  • Bạn có residual risk sau mitigation không?
  • Bạn có 3 key messages rõ ràng không?

Lỗi cần tránh

  • Liệt kê rủi ro mà không có mitigation.
  • Không tách rõ likelihood và impact.
  • Không nêu residual risk sau mitigation.
  • Kết luận "no risks" thay vì nêu risk appetite.

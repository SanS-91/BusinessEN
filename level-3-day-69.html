Mock Investment Committee

Day 69 là bài đánh giá cuối Level 2: bạn sẽ mô phỏng một buổi Investment Committee hoàn chỉnh với 15 phút pitch và 10 phút Q&A. Đây là cơ hội để bạn tích hợp tất cả kỹ năng đã học: financial modeling, IRR & NPV, sensitivity analysis, risk assessment, và executive presentation.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & IC Terms

12 thuật ngữ Investment Committee cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

investment committee

/ɪnˈvestmənt kəˈmɪti/

Ủy ban đầu tư

Ghi chú: Investment Committee (IC) là nhóm ra quyết định đầu tư. Thường gồm senior management, CFO, và investment professionals.
  • Collocation: present to IC, IC approval, IC meeting, IC members
  • Example: We will present the investment case to the IC next week for approval.
  • Native alternative: investment committee, IC, investment committee members

investment thesis

/nˈvestmənt ˈθiːsɪs/

Luận điểm đầu tư

Ghi chú: Investment thesis là luận điểm chính giải thích tại sao đây là cơ hội đầu tư tốt. Phải rõ ràng, có logic, và có bằng chứng.
  • Collocation: investment thesis, strong investment thesis, clear investment thesis
  • Example: Our investment thesis is based on strong market demand, proven track record, and attractive risk-adjusted returns.
  • Native alternative: investment thesis, investment rationale

IRR (Internal Rate of Return)

/ˌaɪ ɑːr ˈɑːr/

Tỷ suất hoàn vốn nội bộ

Ghi chú: IRR là tỷ suất lợi nhuận mà tại đó NPV = 0. IRR > hurdle rate = dự án đáng đầu tư. IRR là metric quan trọng nhất trong investment decision.
  • Collocation: project IRR, IRR of X%, IRR exceeds hurdle rate
  • Example: The project IRR is 18%, which exceeds our hurdle rate of 15% by 300 basis points.
  • Native alternative: IRR, internal rate of return

NPV (Net Present Value)

/ˌen piː ˈviː/

Giá trị hiện tại ròng

Ghi chú: NPV là giá trị hiện tại của dòng tiền tương lai trừ đi vốn đầu tư. NPV > 0 = dự án tạo giá trị. NPV càng cao càng tốt.
  • Collocation: positive NPV, NPV of X million, NPV exceeds zero
  • Example: The project has a positive NPV of 42 million dollars, indicating strong value creation.
  • Native alternative: NPV, net present value

payback period

/ˈpeɪbæk ˈpɪəriəd/

Thời gian hoàn vốn

Ghi chú: Payback period là thời gian cần để thu hồi vốn đầu tư ban đầu. Payback ngắn hơn benchmark = tốt hơn.
  • Collocation: payback period of X years, shorter payback, payback exceeds benchmark
  • Example: The payback period is 4 years, which is faster than our 5-year benchmark.
  • Native alternative: payback period, payback

hurdle rate

/ˈhɜrdl reɪt/

Tỷ suất hurdle (tỷ suất tối thiểu)

Ghi chú: Hurdle rate là tỷ suất lợi nhuận tối thiểu mà nhà đầu tư yêu cầu. Project phải vượt qua hurdle rate mới đáng đầu tư.
  • Collocation: hurdle rate of X%, exceeds hurdle rate, meets hurdle rate
  • Example: Our hurdle rate is 15%, and the project IRR of 18% exceeds this by 300 basis points.
  • Native alternative: hurdle rate, required rate of return

sensitivity analysis

/ˌsensəˈtɪvəti əˈnæləsɪs/

Phân tích độ nhạy

Ghi chú: Sensitivity analysis là phân tích độ nhạy — test xem thay đổi trong assumptions ảnh hưởng thế nào đến IRR và NPV.
  • Collocation: sensitivity analysis, conduct sensitivity analysis, sensitivity to assumptions
  • Example: We conducted sensitivity analysis to test how changes in key assumptions affect the IRR.
  • Native alternative: sensitivity analysis, what-if analysis

risk mitigation

/rɪsk ˌmɪtɪˈɡeɪʃn/

Giảm thiểu rủi ro

Ghi chú: Risk mitigation là các biện pháp giảm thiểu rủi ro. IC members luôn hỏi về risk mitigation strategies.
  • Collocation: risk mitigation strategies, risk mitigation measures, effective risk mitigation
  • Example: Our risk mitigation strategies include fixed-price contracts and phased launches.
  • Native alternative: risk mitigation, risk reduction

exit strategy

/ˈeksɪt ˈstrætədʒi/

Chiến lược thoát

Ghi chú: Exit strategy là kế hoạch thoát khỏi đầu tư để thu hồi vốn và lợi nhuận. IC members luôn hỏi về exit strategy.
  • Collocation: exit strategy, clear exit strategy, exit strategy is to
  • Example: Our exit strategy is to sell the completed project to an institutional investor within 18 months.
  • Native alternative: exit strategy, exit plan

risk-adjusted return

/rɪsk əˈdʒstɪd rɪˈtɜːrn/

Lợi nhuận điều chỉnh theo rủi ro

Ghi chú: Risk-adjusted return là lợi nhuận sau khi đã tính đến mức độ rủi ro. Returns phải risk-adjusted để so sánh công bằng giữa các cơ hội.
  • Collocation: attractive risk-adjusted return, superior risk-adjusted returns, risk-adjusted return profile
  • Example: The project delivers an 18 percent IRR, which represents an attractive risk-adjusted return given the market conditions.
  • Native alternative: risk-adjusted return, risk-adjusted performance

investment memo

/ɪnˈvestmənt ˈmemoʊ/

Bản ghi nhớ đầu tư

Ghi chú: Investment memo là tài liệu tóm tắt investment case. Thường 1-2 trang, dùng để trình bày trước IC.
  • Collocation: investment memo, 1-page investment memo, prepare investment memo
  • Example: We have prepared a 1-page investment memo summarizing the key investment highlights.
  • Native alternative: investment memo, investment summary

investment highlights

/ɪnˈvestmənt ˈhaɪlaɪts/

Điểm nổi bật đầu tư

Ghi chú: Investment highlights là những điểm nổi bật nhất của investment case. Thường 3-5 điểm chính.
  • Collocation: key investment highlights, investment highlights include, key highlights
  • Example: The key investment highlights are: strong market demand, proven track record, and attractive risk-adjusted returns.
  • Native alternative: investment highlights, key highlights

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

8 mẫu câu giúp bạn trình bày investment case và xử lý Q&A: opening, market context, financials, sensitivity, risks, recommendation, handling questions, closing.

Let me walk you through the investment case...

Công thức: Let me walk you through the investment case for + project + structure. Dùng để mở đầu investment pitch, signpost cấu trúc.
Opening

Let me walk you through the investment case for Riverside Township Phase 2. I'll cover the context, the numbers, the risks, and our recommendation.

Substitution drill

Thay project: the acquisition target / the development project / the JV opportunity. Thay structure: market, financials, risks, recommendation.

The market opportunity is clear...

Công thức: The market opportunity is clear + evidence. Dùng để trình bày market context với bằng chứng cụ thể.
Market context

The market opportunity is clear — demand exceeds supply by 30 percent in this corridor.

Substitution drill

Thay evidence: population growth at 4% annually / limited supply in the pipeline / strong absorption rates.

At a high level, the project delivers...

Công thức: At a high level, the project delivers + key metrics. Dùng để trình bày financial overview ở mức tổng quan.
Financial overview

At a high level, the project delivers an 18 percent IRR with a 4-year payback.

Substitution drill

Thay metrics: 20% IRR with 3-year payback / 15% IRR with 5-year payback.

The model is most sensitive to...

Công thức: The model is most sensitive to + variable + impact. Dùng để trình bày sensitivity analysis.
Sensitivity

The model is most sensitive to selling price. A 10 percent drop reduces IRR to 14 percent, still above our hurdle.

Substitution drill

Thay variable: construction cost / absorption rate / land cost. Thay impact: reduces IRR to 12% / reduces NPV to 30M.

We've mitigated each risk with...

Công thức: We've mitigated each risk with + mitigation strategies. Dùng để trình bày risk mitigation.
Risk mitigation

We've mitigated each risk with specific actions: fixed-price contracts, early milestones, and phased launches.

Substitution drill

Thay strategies: fixed-price contracts / contingency buffer / phased launches / early legal approvals.

The bottom line is this:...

Công thức: The bottom line is this: + conclusion. Dùng để kết luận mạnh mẽ trước recommendation.
Conclusion

The bottom line is this: this project creates strong value with manageable risk.

Substitution drill

Thay conclusion: the timing is right / the risk-reward profile is compelling / the market opportunity is clear.

My recommendation is clear:...

Công thức: My recommendation is clear: + action + deadline. Dùng để kết thúc pitch với recommendation rõ ràng.
Recommendation

My recommendation is clear: approve Phase 2 funding by the end of this month.

Substitution drill

Thay action: proceed with the acquisition / approve the budget / greenlight Phase 3. Thay deadline: by end of Q1 / by March 31st.

That's a good question. Let me address it directly...

Công thức: That's a good question. Let me address it directly + answer. Dùng để xử lý tough questions trong Q&A.
Handling questions

That's a good question. Let me address it directly. The IRR is 18%, which exceeds our hurdle rate by 300 basis points.

Substitution drill

Thay answer: the NPV is positive / the payback is 4 years / the risk is manageable.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Investment Committee Pitch

Nghe một bài investment pitch mẫu 15 phút với Q&A 10 phút. Đây là mô hình để bạn tái hiện với dự án thật của mình.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ pitch 1 lần để nắm cấu trúc 10 slides.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện các patterns: signpost, transition, key takeaway, bottom line, call to action.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích: mỗi slide có 1 key message không? Số liệu có được giải thích không?
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm.

Transcript

[Slide 1: Opening]Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here. Let me walk you through the investment case for Riverside Township Phase 2. I'll cover the context, the numbers, the risks, and our recommendation.

[Slide 2: Context]Riverside Township is a 50-hectare mixed-use development in the eastern corridor. Phase 1 is 65 percent sold, well above our 50 percent target. The key takeaway here is that demand is real and proven.

[Slide 3: Financials]Now let me transition to the financials. At a high level, the project delivers an 18 percent IRR with a 4-year payback. What this means is that we recover our capital faster than our 5-year benchmark.

[Slide 4: Sensitivity]The model is most sensitive to selling price. A 10 percent drop reduces IRR to 14 percent, still above our 12 percent hurdle. The key takeaway is that the project is resilient even under stress.

[Slide 5: Risks]The three main risks are construction cost escalation, legal delays, and slower absorption. We've mitigated each with fixed-price contracts, early legal milestones, and phased launches.

[Slide 6: Conclusion]The bottom line is this: Riverside Phase 2 creates strong value with manageable risk. My recommendation is clear: approve Phase 2 funding by the end of this month. Thank you. I'm happy to take any questions.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: What is the project's IRR and payback period?
  • Q2: What is the model most sensitive to?
  • Q3: What are the three main risks and their mitigations?
  • Q4: What is the speaker's recommendation and deadline?
  • Q5: What is the key takeaway from the sensitivity analysis?
  • Q6: What is the key takeaway from the market context?

Sample Answers

1. IRR is 18% with a 4-year payback, faster than the 5-year benchmark.
2. Most sensitive to selling price — a 10% drop reduces IRR to 14%, still above the 12% hurdle.
3. Construction cost escalation (fixed-price contracts), legal delays (early milestones), slower absorption (phased launches).
4. Approve Phase 2 funding by end of this month.
5. The project is resilient even under stress.
6. Demand is real and proven — Phase 1 is 65% sold vs 50% target.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Opening

Let me walk you through the investment case. I'll cover the context, the numbers, the risks, and our recommendation.

Shadow 2 · Transition

Now let me transition to the financials. At a high level, the project delivers an 18 percent IRR with a 4-year payback.

Shadow 3 · Bottom line

The bottom line is this: Riverside Phase 2 creates strong value with manageable risk. My recommendation is clear: approve Phase 2 funding by the end of this month.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào signpost: "FIRST... SECOND... FINALLY..."
  • Nhấn vào số liệu: "18 PERCENT", "4-YEAR payback", "12 percent HURDLE".
  • Giọng chắc chắn khi kết luận: "The BOTTOM LINE is THIS..."
  • Giọng ấm, tự tin khi mở đầu; giọng mạnh, rõ khi call to action.
  • Ngắt nghỉ sau mỗi slide để người nghe tiêu hóa thông tin.

Speaking · Investment Pitch 10 Slides

Luyện 3 vòng tăng dần. Mục tiêu cuối cùng: trình bày 10 slides trong 15 phút mà không đọc slide.

Round 1 · 5 Slides (5 phút)

Trình bày Slides 1–5: Title, Context, Overview, Financials, Sensitivity.

Slide 1: Good morning. Let me walk you through the investment case for Riverside Phase 2.

Slide 2: The market opportunity is clear — demand exceeds supply by 30 percent in this corridor.

Slide 3: At a high level, this is a 50-hectare mixed-use development with 1,200 units.

Slide 4: The key takeaway here is an 18 percent IRR with a 4-year payback.

Slide 5: What this means is that even under stress, the project remains viable.

Round 2 · 7 Slides (7 phút)

Thêm Slides 6–7: Risks và Comparison.

Slide 6: Now let me transition to risks. The three main risks are construction cost escalation, legal delays, and slower absorption. We've mitigated each with specific actions.

Slide 7: Compared with the alternative, Riverside offers a higher IRR and lower risk. The key takeaway is that this is the better risk-adjusted opportunity.

Round 3 · Full 10 Slides (15 phút)

Hoàn chỉnh 10 slides: thêm Timeline, Recommendation, Q&A.

Slide 8: Our timeline is on track. Groundbreaking is complete, and we expect topping out by Q3.

Slide 9: The bottom line is this: Riverside Phase 2 creates strong value with manageable risk. My recommendation is clear: approve Phase 2 funding by the end of this month.

Slide 10: Thank you for your time. I'm happy to take any questions.

Output · Investment Memo & Pitch

Output cuối ngày: hoàn thiện 1-page investment memo, ghi âm/ghi hình 15 phút pitch, tự chấm theo rubric.

Task 1: 1-Page Investment Memo

Tạo 1-page investment memo cho dự án thật của bạn. Mỗi phần có 1 key message + 1–2 số liệu.

Model Answer · 1-Page Investment Memo

1-Page Investment Memo

Riverside Township Phase 2 — Investment Pitch

1. Market Context: Demand exceeds supply by 30% in the eastern corridor. Population growth at 4% annually. Key: Demand is real

2. Project Overview: 50 hectares, 1,200 units, mixed-use. Phase 1 is 65% sold (target: 50%). Key: Proven track record

3. Financial Overview: IRR: 18% (hurdle: 15%). NPV: +42M USD. Payback: 4 years. Key: IRR exceeds hurdle by 300 bps

4. Sensitivity Analysis: Most sensitive to ASP. 10% price drop → IRR 14% (still above hurdle). Key: Resilient under stress

5. Key Risks & Mitigation: Construction cost (fixed-price contracts), legal (early milestones), absorption (phased launches). Key: Risks are managed

6. Recommendation: Approve Phase 2 funding by end of month. Fixed-price contracts expire. Key: Approve now

Task 2: Ghi âm / Ghi hình 15 phút

Ghi âm hoặc ghi hình bài trình bày 10 slides. Mục tiêu: không đọc slide, giải thích số liệu, có transition, có conclusion.

Recording Checklist

  • Opening rõ mục tiêu (signpost cấu trúc).
  • Mỗi slide có đúng 1 key message.
  • Số liệu được giải thích, không chỉ đọc số.
  • Có transition giữa mỗi slide.
  • Có conclusion / recommendation rõ ràng.
  • Không đọc slide — nói bằng lời của bạn.

Task 3: Self-Assessment Rubric

Self-Assessment 8 Criteria for Investment Pitch

1. Opening rõ mục tiêu
Needs work: Không signpost, người nghe không biết bài nói đi đâu.
Good: Có signpost nhưng thiếu hook gây chú ý.
Excellent: Hook mạnh + signpost rõ: "I'll cover context, numbers, risks, and recommendation."

2. Market context rõ ràng
Needs work: Không có market context.
Good: Có market context nhưng thiếu số liệu.
Excellent: Market context với số liệu cụ thể: "Demand exceeds supply by 30%."

3. Financial metrics rõ ràng
Needs work: Đọc số mà không giải thích.
Good: Giải thích một phần.
Excellent: "IRR is 18%, exceeding our 15% hurdle by 300 bps, which means the project creates significant value."

4. Sensitivity analysis rõ ràng
Needs work: Không có sensitivity analysis.
Good: Có sensitivity nhưng thiếu impact.
Excellent: "Most sensitive to ASP. 10% drop reduces IRR to 14%, still above hurdle."

5. Risk mitigation rõ ràng
Needs work: Không có risk mitigation.
Good: Có risk mitigation nhưng thiếu cụ thể.
Excellent: "We've mitigated each risk with specific actions: fixed-price contracts, early milestones, phased launches."

6. Có transition giữa các slide
Needs work: Nhảy đột ngột giữa các slide.
Good: Có transition nhưng đơn giản.
Excellent: "Now let me transition from the market analysis to the financial projections..."

7. Conclusion / Recommendation
Needs work: Kết thúc đột ngột.
Good: Có kết luận nhưng thiếu call to action.
Excellent: "The bottom line is this: approve Phase 2 funding by end of month. My recommendation is clear."

8. Không đọc slide
Needs work: Đọc nguyên văn slide.
Good: Có đọc slide nhưng có giải thích thêm.
Excellent: Nói bằng lời của bạn, không đọc slide.

Reflection 3 dòng

Good: 1 điểm bạn đã làm tốt.

Improve: 1 điểm cần cải thiện.

Keep: 1 kỹ thuật bạn sẽ tiếp tục dùng.

Reflection mẫu

Good: Tôi đã có transition rõ ràng giữa các slide và không đọc slide. Improve: Tôi cần giải thích số liệu sâu hơn thay vì chỉ đọc số. Keep: "The bottom line is this..."

Mastery Checklist

Bạn hoàn thành Day 69 khi có thể

Trước khi sang Level 3, hãy đảm bảo bạn đã hoàn thành bài investment pitch hoàn chỉnh.

  • Tôi có thể dùng investment committee, investment thesis, IRR, NPV.
  • Tôi có thể dùng payback period, hurdle rate, sensitivity analysis, risk mitigation.
  • Tôi có thể dùng exit strategy, risk-adjusted return, investment memo, investment highlights.
  • Tôi có thể mở đầu với hook + signpost cấu trúc.
  • Tôi có thể trình bày market context với số liệu cụ thể.
  • Tôi có thể giải thích financial metrics: IRR, NPV, payback.
  • Tôi có thể trình bày sensitivity analysis với impact cụ thể.

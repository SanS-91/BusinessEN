Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Crisis Terms

8 thuật ngữ crisis management cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

crisis

/ˈkraɪsɪs/

Khủng hoảng

Ghi chú: Crisis là tình huống khẩn cấp, nghiêm trọng. Trong business, thường là budget crisis, liquidity crisis, hoặc reputation crisis.
  • Collocation: facing a crisis, crisis management, crisis situation, crisis communication
  • Example: We are currently facing a budget crisis that requires immediate attention.
  • Native alternative: emergency, critical situation

turnaround

/ˈtːrnəraʊnd/

Tái cấu trúc / Xoay chuyển tình thế

Ghi chú: Turnaround là chiến lược xoay chuyển tình thế từ xấu sang tốt. Thường dùng trong crisis management.
  • Collocation: turnaround strategy, turnaround plan, company turnaround
  • Example: We have implemented a turnaround strategy to address the budget overrun.
  • Native alternative: recovery plan, restructuring

contingency

/kənˈtɪndʒənsi/

Kế hoạch dự phòng

Ghi chú: Contingency là kế hoạch dự phòng cho tình huống khẩn cấp. Contingency plan là kế hoạch dự phòng.
  • Collocation: contingency plan, contingency fund, activate contingency
  • Example: We have activated our contingency plan to address the budget overrun.
  • Native alternative: backup plan, contingency measure

stakeholder

/ˈsteɪkhoʊldər/

Các bên liên quan

Ghi chú: Stakeholder là các bên liên quan: investors, employees, customers, regulators. Trong crisis, cần giao tiếp minh bạch với tất cả.
  • Collocation: key stakeholders, stakeholder communication, stakeholder engagement
  • Example: We are committed to transparent communication with all stakeholders during this crisis.
  • Native alternative: stakeholders, interested parties

reputation

/ˌrepjuˈteɪʃn/

Danh tiếng

Ghi chú: Reputation là danh tiếng công ty. Trong crisis, reputation risk là rủi ro lớn nhất cần quản lý.
  • Collocation: reputation risk, protect reputation, reputation management
  • Example: We are taking immediate action to protect our reputation during this crisis.
  • Native alternative: brand reputation, corporate reputation

liquidity

/lɪˈkwɪdəti/

Thanh khoản

Ghi chú: Liquidity là khả năng thanh khoản. Liquidity crunch là khủng hoảng thanh khoản.
  • Collocation: liquidity crisis, liquidity crunch, liquidity position
  • Example: We are facing a liquidity crunch but have secured emergency funding.
  • Native alternative: cash flow, liquidity

restructuring

/ˌriːstrʌktʃərɪŋ/

Tái cấu trúc

Ghi chú: Restructuring là quá trình tái cấu trúc công ty để cải thiện hiệu quả hoạt động.
  • Collocation: restructuring process, company restructuring, restructuring plan
  • Example: We are undergoing a restructuring process to improve operational efficiency.
  • Native alternative: restructuring, reorganization

transparency

/trænsˈpærənsi/

Sự minh bạch

Ghi chú: Transparency là sự minh bạch. Trong crisis, transparency là yếu tố then chốt để duy trì niềm tin.
  • Collocation: full transparency, transparent communication, commitment to transparency
  • Example: We are committed to full transparency with our stakeholders throughout this process.
  • Native alternative: transparency, openness
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

6 mẫu câu crisis communication: acknowledging challenge, stating priority, activating contingency, committing to transparency, providing updates, outlining next steps.

We are currently facing a challenge...

Công thức: We are currently facing a challenge + that requires action. Dùng để thừa nhận vấn đề một cách trực tiếp và chuyên nghiệp.
Acknowledging challenge

We are currently facing a budget challenge that requires immediate attention.

Substitution drill

Thay challenge: liquidity challenge / operational challenge / market challenge.

Our immediate priority is to...

Công thức: Our immediate priority is to + action. Dùng để nêu ưu tiên hàng đầu trong crisis.
Stating priority

Our immediate priority is to stabilize cash flow and restore stakeholder confidence.

Substitution drill

Thay action: address the budget overrun / restore operational efficiency / protect our reputation.

We have activated our contingency plan...

Công thức: We have activated our contingency plan to + action. Dùng để thông báo đã kích hoạt kế hoạch dự phòng.
Activating contingency

We have activated our contingency plan to address the budget overrun.

Substitution drill

Thay action: address the liquidity crunch / restore operational efficiency / protect our reputation.

We are committed to transparency...

Công thức: We are committed to transparency + with stakeholders. Dùng để cam kết minh bạch với các bên liên quan.
Committing to transparency

We are committed to full transparency with our stakeholders throughout this process.

Substitution drill

Thay stakeholders: with our investors / with our employees / with our customers.

We will provide regular updates...

Công thức: We will provide regular updates + to stakeholders + on progress. Dùng để cam kết cung cấp thông tin cập nhật.
Providing updates

We will provide regular updates to the Board on our progress.

Substitution drill

Thay stakeholders: to investors / to employees / to customers.

Our next steps are...

Công thức: Our next steps are to + actions. Dùng để nêu các bước tiếp theo.
Outlining next steps

Our next steps are to stabilize operations, restore confidence, and return to growth.

Substitution drill

Thay actions: address the budget / restore operations / return to profitability.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Crisis Briefing to the Board

Nghe một buổi crisis briefing mẫu 10 phút trước Ban lãnh đạo về vượt ngân sách 15%. Đây là mô hình để bạn thực hành crisis communication.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm crisis situation và turnaround strategy.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện các patterns: acknowledging challenge, stating priority, activating contingency.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích communication techniques: transparency, regular updates, next steps.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm (problem statement, solution, next steps).

Transcript

[Opening]Good morning, Board members. Thank you for being here. I want to be direct with you today. We are currently facing a budget challenge: we are 15 percent over budget due to supply chain disruptions and material cost increases.

[Priority]Our immediate priority is to stabilize cash flow and restore stakeholder confidence.

[Contingency Plan]We have activated our contingency plan: we have secured emergency funding of $10 million, implemented cost-cutting measures of 10 percent across all departments, and renegotiated contracts with key suppliers.

[Transparency]We are committed to full transparency with the Board throughout this process. We will provide weekly updates on our progress.

[Next Steps]Our next steps are to stabilize operations by Q2, restore profitability by Q3, and return to growth by Q4. Thank you. I'm happy to take any questions.

[Q&A - Question 1]Board Member 1: How did we get into this situation?
Presenter: We got into this situation due to supply chain disruptions and material cost increases beyond our control.

[Q&A - Question 2]Board Member 2: What's the impact on profitability?
Presenter: The impact is a 15 percent budget overrun, but our contingency plan will restore profitability by Q3.

[Q&A - Question 3]Board Member 3: What's the timeline for recovery?
Presenter: Our timeline is stabilize by Q2, restore profitability by Q3, and return to growth by Q4.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: What is the crisis situation?
  • Q2: What is the immediate priority?
  • Q3: What are the three components of the contingency plan?
  • Q4: What is the commitment to the Board?
  • Q5: What is the timeline for recovery?

Sample Answers

1. Crisis: 15% budget overrun due to supply chain disruptions and material cost increases.
2. Immediate priority: stabilize cash flow and restore stakeholder confidence.
3. Contingency plan: secured $10M emergency funding, 10% cost-cutting, renegotiated supplier contracts.
4. Commitment: full transparency with weekly updates.
5. Timeline: stabilize by Q2, restore profitability by Q3, return to growth by Q4.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Problem statement

We are currently facing a budget challenge: we are 15 percent over budget due to supply chain disruptions.

Shadow 2 · Solution

We have activated our contingency plan: secured $10 million emergency funding, 10 percent cost-cutting, and renegotiated contracts.

Shadow 3 · Next steps

Our next steps are to stabilize operations by Q2, restore profitability by Q3, and return to growth by Q4.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Giọng nghiêm túc, trực tiếp khi nêu vấn đề: "We are currently facing..."
  • Giọng tự tin, quyết đoán khi nêu giải pháp: "We have activated..."
  • Giọng cam kết khi nêu commitment: "We are committed to..."
  • Nhấn vào các mốc thời gian: Q2, Q3, Q4.
Block 4 · 20 phút

Speaking · Crisis Briefing 10 Minutes

Luyện 3 vòng tăng dần: problem statement → solution → full briefing + Q&A. Mục tiêu: tự tin trình bày crisis briefing trước Ban lãnh đạo.

Round 1 · Problem Statement (3 phút)

Trình bày vấn đề khủng hoảng: acknowledging challenge, stating priority, activating contingency.

Opening: Good morning, Board members. I want to be direct with you today.

Problem: We are currently facing a budget challenge: we are 15 percent over budget due to supply chain disruptions and material cost increases.

Priority: Our immediate priority is to stabilize cash flow and restore stakeholder confidence.

Contingency: We have activated our contingency plan: secured $10 million emergency funding, 10 percent cost-cutting, and renegotiated contracts.

Round 2 · Solution & Next Steps (5 phút)

Trình bày giải pháp và các bước tiếp theo: transparency, regular updates, next steps.

Transparency: We are committed to full transparency with the Board throughout this process. We will provide weekly updates on our progress.

Next steps: Our next steps are to stabilize operations by Q2, restore profitability by Q3, and return to growth by Q4.

Closing: Thank you. I'm happy to take any questions.

Round 3 · Full Briefing + Q&A (10 phút)

Sau khi briefing 5 phút, trả lời 3 câu hỏi giả định từ Ban lãnh đạo. Dùng cấu trúc: acknowledge question → provide answer → restate commitment.

Question 1: How did we get into this situation?

We got into this situation due to supply chain disruptions and material cost increases beyond our control.

Question 2: What's the impact on profitability?

The impact is a 15 percent budget overrun, but our contingency plan will restore profitability by Q3.

Question 3: What's the timeline for recovery?

Our timeline is stabilize by Q2, restore profitability by Q3, and return to growth by Q4.

Recording Task

Ghi âm Round 3 hoàn chỉnh: crisis briefing 5 phút + trả lời 3 câu hỏi. Đây là crisis briefing hoàn chỉnh.

Self-review

  • Briefing có cấu trúc rõ ràng (problem, priority, contingency, next steps)?
  • Mỗi câu trả lời Q&A có acknowledge question + answer + restate?
  • Bạn có giọng nghiêm túc, tự tin khi trình bày?
  • Bạn có nhấn vào các mốc thời gian (Q2, Q3, Q4)?
  • Bạn có cam kết transparency và regular updates?
Block 5 · 15 phút

Output · Crisis Communication Memo

Output cuối ngày: chuẩn bị crisis communication memo, ghi âm crisis briefing hoàn chỉnh, tự chấm theo rubric.

Task 1: Crisis Communication Memo

Chuẩn bị crisis communication memo cho tình huống vượt ngân sách 15%. Memo cần có: problem, priority, contingency, transparency, next steps.

Model Answer · Crisis Communication Memo

Crisis Communication Memo

To: Board of Directors

From: [Your Name]

Date: [Date]

Subject: Budget Overrun & Turnaround Strategy

1. Problem Statement

We are currently facing a budget challenge: we are 15 percent over budget due to supply chain disruptions and material cost increases beyond our control.

2. Immediate Priority

Our immediate priority is to stabilize cash flow and restore stakeholder confidence.

3. Contingency Plan

We have activated our contingency plan:

  • Secured $10 million emergency funding
  • Implemented 10 percent cost-cutting across all departments
  • Renegotiated contracts with key suppliers

4. Commitment to Transparency

We are committed to full transparency with the Board throughout this process. We will provide weekly updates on our progress.

5. Next Steps & Timeline

Our next steps are:

  • Stabilize operations by Q2
  • Restore profitability by Q3
  • Return to growth by Q4

Thank you. I'm happy to take any questions.

Task 2: Ghi âm Crisis Briefing

Ghi âm crisis briefing hoàn chỉnh: briefing 5 phút + trả lời 3 câu hỏi giả định. Đây là bài test tổng hợp.

Recording Checklist

  • Briefing có cấu trúc rõ ràng (problem, priority, contingency, next steps)?
  • Mỗi câu trả lời Q&A có acknowledge question + answer + restate?
  • Bạn có giọng nghiêm túc, tự tin khi trình bày?
  • Bạn có nhấn vào các mốc thời gian (Q2, Q3, Q4)?
  • Bạn có cam kết transparency và regular updates?

Task 3: Reflection 3 dòng

Sau khi hoàn thành, hãy viết ngắn gọn 3 dòng:

  • Good: 1 điểm bạn đã làm tốt.
  • Improve: 1 điểm cần cải thiện.
  • Keep: 1 kỹ thuật bạn sẽ tiếp tục dùng.
Reflection mẫu

Good: Tôi đã trình bày problem statement một cách trực tiếp và chuyên nghiệp. Improve: Tôi cần nhấn mạnh hơn vào các mốc thời gian. Keep: "We are committed to full transparency..."

Common Mistakes

4 lỗi thường gặp trong crisis communication:

1. Tránh: Giấu vấn đề hoặc nói vòng vo: "We are facing some challenges..."

Nên: "We are currently facing a budget challenge: we are 15 percent over budget."

2. Tránh: Không nêu priority rõ ràng: "We will try to fix this..."

Nên: "Our immediate priority is to stabilize cash flow and restore stakeholder confidence."

3. Tránh: Không cam kết transparency: "We will keep you informed..."

Nên: "We are committed to full transparency with weekly updates."

4. Tránh: Không nêu timeline cụ thể: "We will fix this soon..."

Nên: "Our timeline is stabilize by Q2, restore profitability by Q3, return to growth by Q4."

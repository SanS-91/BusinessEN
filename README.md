# BusinessEN
Business English
# 90-Day Mini MBA English Roadmap

> **Objective**
>
> Build professional English fluency for international business, real estate investment, and executive leadership through a structured 90-day program with increasing difficulty.

---

# Program Structure

| Level | Duration | Focus | Target |
|--------|----------|-------|--------|
| Level 1 | Day 1–30 | Business English Foundation | Work confidently in an international office |
| Level 2 | Day 31–60 | Real Estate & Investment English | Discuss projects, finance and investments |
| Level 3 | Day 61–90 | Executive English | Present, negotiate and influence senior stakeholders |

---

# Level 1 — Business English Foundation
**Day 1–30**

## Goal

Develop confidence communicating in everyday business situations.

By the end of Level 1 you should be able to:

- Introduce yourself professionally
- Participate in meetings
- Write business emails
- Explain project status
- Handle common workplace conversations
- Present simple business ideas

# Expected Outcomes

After 90 days you will be able to:

✅ Communicate naturally with international colleagues.

✅ Write professional emails and reports.

✅ Present project financials confidently.

✅ Explain feasibility studies.

✅ Discuss investment opportunities.

✅ Negotiate with partners and investors.

✅ Present before senior management.

✅ Handle executive Q&A confidently.

---

# Recommended Study Time

- 60–90 minutes per day
- 6 days per week
- Weekly review every Sunday
- Monthly capstone presentation

---

# Final Capstone

At the end of Day 90, learners complete an Executive Investment Pitch including:

- Project Overview
- Market Analysis
- Financial Highlights
- Investment Rationale
- Risks & Mitigation
- Q&A Session

This capstone integrates all communication, financial and leadership skills developed throughout the program.

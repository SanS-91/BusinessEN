Crisis Communication & Media Handling

Day 74 tập trung vào kỹ năng giao tiếp trong khủng hoảng: quản lý truyền thông, phát ngôn trước báo chí, và bảo vệ danh tiếng tổ chức. Bạn sẽ học cách soạn thảo holding statement, xử lý media inquiry, và thể hiện executive presence trong tình huống áp lực cao.

Trong khủng hoảng, im lặng không phải là vàng. Im lặng là khoảng trống để tin đồn lấp đầy. Một nhà lãnh đạo giỏi không trốn tránh truyền thông — họ kiểm soát thông điệp, thể hiện sự đồng cảm, và cam kết hành động.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Crisis Terms

8 thuật ngữ crisis communication cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

crisis communication

/ˈkraɪsɪs kəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃn/

Giao tiếp khủng hoảng

Ghi chú: Crisis communication là quá trình truyền thông trong tình huống khủng hoảng. Mục tiêu là kiểm soát thông điệp và bảo vệ danh tiếng.
  • Collocation: crisis communication strategy, effective crisis communication, manage crisis communication
  • Example: Effective crisis communication is essential for maintaining stakeholder trust during a crisis.
  • Native alternative: crisis communication, crisis PR

media inquiry

/ˈmiːdiə ɪnˈkwaɪəri/

Yêu cầu từ truyền thông

Ghi chú: Media inquiry là yêu cầu phỏng vấn hoặc thông tin từ báo chí. Cần xử lý nhanh chóng và chuyên nghiệp.
  • Collocation: handle media inquiries, respond to media inquiries, media inquiry from press
  • Example: We have received several media inquiries regarding the recent incident.
  • Native alternative: media inquiry, press inquiry

public statement

/ˈpʌblɪk ˈsteɪtmənt/

Tuyên bố công khai

Ghi chú: Public statement là tuyên bố chính thức của tổ chức trước công chúng. Phải rõ ràng, nhất quán và được phê duyệt.
  • Collocation: issue a public statement, release a public statement, official public statement
  • Example: The CEO will issue a public statement addressing the situation.
  • Native alternative: public statement, official statement

reputation management

/ˌrepjuˈteɪʃn ˈmændʒmənt/

Quản lý danh tiếng

Ghi chú: Reputation management là quá trình bảo vệ và xây dựng danh tiếng tổ chức, đặc biệt quan trọng trong khủng hoảng.
  • Collocation: reputation management, protect reputation, reputation management strategy
  • Example: Reputation management is critical during a crisis to maintain stakeholder confidence.
  • Native alternative: reputation management, brand reputation management

stakeholder reassurance

/ˈsteɪkhoʊldər ˌriəˈʃʊrəns/

Sự trấn an các bên liên quan

Ghi chú: Stakeholder reassurance là hành động trấn an các bên liên quan (khách hàng, nhân viên, nhà đầu tư) trong khủng hoảng.
  • Collocation: provide stakeholder reassurance, stakeholder reassurance is critical
  • Example: Providing stakeholder reassurance is critical to prevent panic and misinformation.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder reassurance, stakeholder confidence

crisis response team

/ˈkraɪsɪs rɪˈspɑːns tiːm/

Đội phản ứng khủng hoảng

Ghi chú: Crisis response team là nhóm chuyên trách xử lý khủng hoảng, bao gồm PR, legal, và senior management.
  • Collocation: activate crisis response team, crisis response team is ready
  • Example: The crisis response team has been activated and is managing the situation.
  • Native alternative: crisis response team, crisis management team

media briefing

/ˈmiːdiə ˈbriːfɪŋ/

Họp báo / Briefing truyền thông

Ghi chú: Media briefing là buổi họp hoặc briefing với báo chí để cung cấp thông tin chính thức.
  • Collocation: hold a media briefing, press briefing, media briefing schedule
  • Example: We will hold a media briefing at 3 PM to address the recent developments.
  • Native alternative: media briefing, press conference

holding statement

/ˈhoʊldɪŋ ˈsteɪtmənt/

Tuyên bố giữ chỗ

Ghi chú: Holding statement là tuyên bố tạm thời khi chưa có đầy đủ thông tin, nhằm kiểm soát thông điệp ban đầu.
  • Collocation: issue a holding statement, prepare a holding statement
  • Example: We have issued a holding statement while we investigate the full details of the incident.
  • Native alternative: holding statement, initial statement
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

4 mẫu câu crisis communication: acknowledging situation, stating priority, providing updates, reassuring stakeholders.

We are aware of the situation and are currently...

Công thức: We are aware of the situation and are currently + action. Dùng để thừa nhận tình huống và nêu hành động đang thực hiện.
Acknowledging situation

We are aware of the situation and are currently investigating the full details of the incident.

Substitution drill

Thay action: investigating the root cause / addressing the issue / coordinating with authorities.

Our immediate priority is to...

Công thức: Our immediate priority is to + priority action. Dùng để nêu ưu tiên hàng đầu trong khủng hoảng.
Stating priority

Our immediate priority is to ensure the safety of our employees and customers.

Substitution drill

Thay priority: to contain the situation / to support affected parties / to resolve the issue.

We will provide further updates as soon as...

Công thức: We will provide further updates as soon as + condition. Dùng để cam kết cập nhật thông tin.
Providing updates

We will provide further updates as soon as more information becomes available.

Substitution drill

Thay condition: as soon as the investigation is complete / as soon as we have confirmed details.

We remain committed to...

Công thức: We remain committed to + commitment + action. Dùng để trấn an stakeholders và thể hiện trách nhiệm.
Reassuring stakeholders

We remain committed to our core values and will take full responsibility for resolving this matter.

Substitution drill

Thay commitment: our customers' safety / transparency and accountability / delivering on our promises.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Media Briefing

Nghe một bài media briefing mẫu 5 phút. Đây là mô hình để bạn tái hiện với vai trò người phát ngôn.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm tình huống và hành động.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện 4 patterns: acknowledging, priority, updates, reassurance.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích: giọng điệu executive, cấu trúc briefing, sự đồng cảm.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm.

Transcript

[Acknowledging]Good afternoon. We are aware of the situation regarding the incident at our facility this morning.

[Priority]Our immediate priority is to ensure the safety of all employees and to support those affected.

[Actions]We are currently investigating the full details of the incident and working closely with local authorities.

[Updates]We will provide further updates as soon as more information becomes available.

[Reassurance]We remain committed to transparency and will take full responsibility for resolving this matter.

[Next Steps]Our crisis response team is actively managing the situation, and we will hold a media briefing at 3 PM today. Thank you.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: What is the speaker aware of?
  • Q2: What is the immediate priority?
  • Q3: What actions are currently being taken?
  • Q4: When will further updates be provided?
  • Q5: What is the organization committed to?

Sample Answers

1. The incident at the facility this morning.
2. To ensure the safety of all employees and support those affected.
3. Investigating full details and working with local authorities.
4. As soon as more information becomes available.
5. Transparency and taking full responsibility.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Acknowledging

We are aware of the situation regarding the incident at our facility this morning.

Shadow 2 · Priority

Our immediate priority is to ensure the safety of all employees and to support those affected.

Shadow 3 · Reassurance

We remain committed to transparency and will take full responsibility for resolving this matter.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: aware, immediate priority, safety, investigating, committed, responsibility.
  • Giọng điềm đạm, chắc chắn, thể hiện sự đồng cảm: "Our IMMEDIATE PRIORITY is..."
  • Ngắt nghỉ sau mỗi ý để tạo sự rõ ràng.
  • Kết thúc bằng giọng nghiêm túc và cam kết.
Block 4 · 20 phút

Speaking · Media Briefing

Luyện 3 vòng: từ holding statement đến full media briefing. Mục tiêu là thể hiện executive presence và kiểm soát thông điệp.

Round 1 · 60 giây (Holding Statement)

Chỉ phát holding statement: acknowledging, priority, và next steps.

Good afternoon. We are aware of the situation regarding the incident at our facility this morning. Our immediate priority is to ensure the safety of all employees and to support those affected. We are currently investigating the full details and will provide further updates as soon as more information becomes available. Thank you.

Round 2 · 90 giây (Actions & Updates)

Thêm actions đang thực hiện và reassurance.

Good afternoon. We are aware of the situation regarding the incident at our facility this morning. Our immediate priority is to ensure the safety of all employees and to support those affected. We are currently investigating the full details of the incident and working closely with local authorities. We will provide further updates as soon as more information becomes available. We remain committed to transparency and will take full responsibility for resolving this matter.

Round 3 · 3 phút (Full Media Briefing)

Trình bày media briefing hoàn chỉnh: acknowledging, priority, actions, updates, reassurance, next steps.

Good afternoon. We are aware of the situation regarding the incident at our facility this morning. Our immediate priority is to ensure the safety of all employees and to support those affected. We are currently investigating the full details of the incident and working closely with local authorities. We will provide further updates as soon as more information becomes available. We remain committed to transparency and will take full responsibility for resolving this matter. Our crisis response team is actively managing the situation, and we will hold a media briefing at 3 PM today. Thank you.

Output · Media Briefing Script

Output cuối ngày: viết holding statement / press release script, ghi âm final, tự chấm theo rubric.

Task 1: Viết Media Briefing Script 200-250 từ

Viết một holding statement / press release script chuyên nghiệp, dùng trong tình huống khủng hoảng.

Yêu cầu

  • Dùng ít nhất 4 từ vựng trong mục Vocabulary.
  • Dùng đủ 4 patterns: acknowledging, priority, updates, reassurance.
  • Có cấu trúc: Acknowledging → Priority → Actions → Updates → Reassurance → Next Steps.
  • Giọng văn executive, đồng cảm, chắc chắn.
  • Không đổ lỗi hoặc phỏng đoán khi chưa có thông tin.

Model Answer

Good afternoon. We are aware of the situation regarding the incident at our facility this morning. Our immediate priority is to ensure the safety of all employees and to support those affected. We are currently investigating the full details of the incident and working closely with local authorities. We will provide further updates as soon as more information becomes available. We remain committed to transparency and will take full responsibility for resolving this matter. Our crisis response team is actively managing the situation, and we will hold a media briefing at 3 PM today. Thank you.

Ghi âm media briefing 3 phút. Mục tiêu là thể hiện executive presence và kiểm soát thông điệp.

Common Mistakes

3 lỗi thường gặp khi crisis communication

Tránh các lỗi này để bài media briefing của bạn professional và kiểm soát được thông điệp.

Tránh

Né tránh hoặc không acknowledging: "No comment."

Nên

We are aware of the situation and are currently investigating the full details.

Tránh

Đổ lỗi hoặc phỏng đoán khi chưa có thông tin: "It's not our fault."

Nên

We are investigating the full details and will take full responsibility for resolving this matter.

Tránh

Không có commitment hoặc reassurance: "We will see."

Nên

We remain committed to transparency and will provide further updates as soon as more information becomes available.

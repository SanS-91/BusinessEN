Strategic Stakeholder Management

Day 75 tập trung vào quản lý stakeholders ở cấp chiến lược: xác định key stakeholders, xây dựng mối quan hệ chiến lược, và tạo sự đồng thuận giữa các bên liên quan. Bạn sẽ học cách trình bày stakeholder strategy và drive stakeholder alignment ở cấp executive.

Quản lý stakeholders không phải là quản lý mối quan hệ. Đó là xây dựng liên minh chiến lược, tạo sự đồng thuận, và dẫn dắt các bên cùng hướng đến mục tiêu chung. Một nhà lãnh đạo giỏi không chỉ quản lý stakeholders — họ biến stakeholders thành allies.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Stakeholder Terms

8 thuật ngữ stakeholder management cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

stakeholder mapping

/ˈsteɪkhoʊldər ˈmæpŋ/

Xác định stakeholders

Ghi chú: Stakeholder mapping là quá trình xác định và phân loại các bên liên quan theo mức độ ảnh hưởng và quan tâm.
  • Collocation: stakeholder mapping process, conduct stakeholder mapping, stakeholder mapping matrix
  • Example: We need to conduct stakeholder mapping to identify key stakeholders and their influence levels.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder mapping, stakeholder identification

stakeholder engagement

/ˈsteɪkhoʊldər nˈɡeɪdʒmənt/

Thu hút stakeholders

Ghi chú: Stakeholder engagement là quá trình tương tác và làm việc với các bên liên quan để tạo sự đồng thuận.
  • Collocation: stakeholder engagement strategy, effective stakeholder engagement, drive stakeholder engagement
  • Example: Effective stakeholder engagement is critical for project success.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder engagement, stakeholder involvement

stakeholder alignment

/ˈsteɪkhoʊldər əˈlaɪnmənt/

Sự đồng thuận của stakeholders

Ghi chú: Stakeholder alignment là sự đồng thuận của tất cả stakeholders về mục tiêu và chiến lược. Là yếu tố then chốt của thành công.
  • Collocation: achieve stakeholder alignment, stakeholder alignment is critical, drive stakeholder alignment
  • Example: Achieving stakeholder alignment is critical before moving to execution.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder alignment, stakeholder consensus

key stakeholders

/kiː ˈstekhoʊldərz/

Các bên liên quan chính

Ghi chú: Key stakeholders là những bên có ảnh hưởng lớn đến dự án: investors, banks, partners, regulators, senior management.
  • Collocation: key stakeholders, identify key stakeholders, engage key stakeholders
  • Example: We need to identify and engage key stakeholders early in the project.
  • Native alternative: key stakeholders, primary stakeholders

stakeholder influence

/steɪkhoʊldər ˈɪnfluəns/

Ảnh hưởng của stakeholders

Ghi chú: Stakeholder influence là mức độ ảnh hưởng của mỗi stakeholder đến quyết định và kết quả dự án.
  • Collocation: stakeholder influence, high influence stakeholders, stakeholder influence level
  • Example: We need to understand each stakeholder's influence level and engagement approach.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder influence, stakeholder power

stakeholder strategy

/ˈsteɪkhoʊldər ˈstrætədi/

Chiến lược stakeholders

Ghi chú: Stakeholder strategy là chiến lược quản lý và engage với từng nhóm stakeholders khác nhau.
  • Collocation: stakeholder strategy, stakeholder management strategy, develop stakeholder strategy
  • Example: Our stakeholder strategy focuses on building strategic relationships with key stakeholders.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder strategy, stakeholder engagement strategy

stakeholder analysis

/steɪkhoʊldər əˈnæləsɪs/

Phân tích stakeholders

Ghi chú: Stakeholder analysis là phân tích interests, influence, và engagement approach của từng stakeholder.
  • Collocation: stakeholder analysis, conduct stakeholder analysis, stakeholder analysis matrix
  • Example: We conducted stakeholder analysis to understand each stakeholder's interests and influence.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder analysis, stakeholder assessment

strategic relationships

/strəˈtiːdʒɪk rɪˈleɪʃnɪps/

Mối quan hệ chiến lược

Ghi chú: Strategic relationships là các mối quan hệ chiến lược với key stakeholders, khác với transactional relationships.
  • Collocation: strategic relationships, build strategic relationships, maintain strategic relationships
  • Example: Building strategic relationships with key stakeholders is essential for long-term success.
  • Native alternative: strategic relationships, strategic partnerships
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

4 mẫu câu stakeholder management: identifying stakeholders, engaging stakeholders, aligning stakeholders, building strategic relationships.

We have identified key stakeholders including...

Công thức: We have identified key stakeholders including + stakeholders list. Dùng để nêu danh sách key stakeholders.
Identifying stakeholders

We have identified key stakeholders including investors, banks, JV partners, and regulatory authorities.

Substitution drill

Thay stakeholders: senior management / government agencies / community leaders / strategic partners.

Our engagement strategy focuses on...

Công thức: Our engagement strategy focuses on + engagement approach. Dùng để trình bày chiến lược engage stakeholders.
Engaging stakeholders

Our engagement strategy focuses on building strategic relationships and driving stakeholder alignment.

Substitution drill

Thay approach: regular communication / collaborative decision-making / transparent reporting.

To achieve stakeholder alignment, we need to...

Công thức: To achieve stakeholder alignment, we need to + action. Dùng để nêu các bước tạo sự đồng thuận.
Aligning stakeholders

To achieve stakeholder alignment, we need to ensure all stakeholders understand the strategic vision and their role.

Substitution drill

Thay action: communicate the benefits / address concerns / build consensus.

Building strategic relationships with...

Công thức: Building strategic relationships with + stakeholders + is essential for + outcome. Dùng để nhấn mạnh tầm quan trọng của relationships.
Building relationships

Building strategic relationships with key stakeholders is essential for long-term project success.

Substitution drill

Thay stakeholders: investors / partners / regulators. Thay outcome: project success / strategic alignment / long-term value.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Stakeholder Strategy Presentation

Nghe một bài stakeholder strategy presentation mẫu 5 phút. Đây là mô hình để bạn tái hiện với dự án thật của mình.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm stakeholder strategy tổng thể.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện 4 patterns: identifying stakeholders, engagement strategy, alignment approach, building relationships.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích: stakeholder mapping, engagement approach, alignment strategy.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm.

Transcript

[Opening]Good morning. Today, I will present our stakeholder strategy for the project.

[Identifying Stakeholders]We have identified key stakeholders including investors, banks, JV partners, and regulatory authorities.

[Engagement Strategy]Our engagement strategy focuses on building strategic relationships and driving stakeholder alignment.

[Alignment Approach]To achieve stakeholder alignment, we need to ensure all stakeholders understand the strategic vision and their role in achieving our goals.

[Building Relationships]Building strategic relationships with key stakeholders is essential for long-term project success.

[Implementation]We will implement regular communication, collaborative decision-making, and transparent reporting to maintain strong relationships with all stakeholders. Thank you.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: Who are the key stakeholders identified?
  • Q2: What does the engagement strategy focus on?
  • Q3: What is needed to achieve stakeholder alignment?
  • Q4: Why is building strategic relationships essential?
  • Q5: What will be implemented to maintain relationships?

Sample Answers

1. Investors, banks, JV partners, and regulatory authorities.
2. Building strategic relationships and driving stakeholder alignment.
3. Ensuring all stakeholders understand the strategic vision and their role.
4. For long-term project success.
5. Regular communication, collaborative decision-making, and transparent reporting.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Identifying Stakeholders

We have identified key stakeholders including investors, banks, JV partners, and regulatory authorities.

Shadow 2 · Engagement Strategy

Our engagement strategy focuses on building strategic relationships and driving stakeholder alignment.

Shadow 3 · Building Relationships

Building strategic relationships with key stakeholders is essential for long-term project success.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: stakeholders, strategy, alignment, relationships, essential.
  • Giọng chắc chắn, executive: "Our ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY focuses on..."
  • Ngắt nghỉ sau mỗi nhóm ý để tạo sự rõ ràng.
  • Kết thúc bằng giọng tự tin và chắc chắn.
Block 4 · 20 phút

Speaking · Stakeholder Strategy Presentation

Luyện 3 vòng: từ stakeholder overview đến full stakeholder strategy presentation. Mục tiêu là thể hiện executive stakeholder management capability.

Round 1 · 60 giây (Stakeholder Overview)

Chỉ giới thiệu: identifying key stakeholders và engagement strategy overview.

Good morning. Today, I will present our stakeholder strategy for the project. We have identified key stakeholders including investors, banks, JV partners, and regulatory authorities. Our engagement strategy focuses on building strategic relationships and driving stakeholder alignment. This approach is essential for project success.

Round 2 · 90 giây (Engagement & Alignment)

Thêm alignment approach và building relationships chi tiết hơn.

Good morning. Today, I will present our stakeholder strategy for the project. We have identified key stakeholders including investors, banks, JV partners, and regulatory authorities. Our engagement strategy focuses on building strategic relationships and driving stakeholder alignment. To achieve stakeholder alignment, we need to ensure all stakeholders understand the strategic vision and their role. Building strategic relationships with key stakeholders is essential for long-term project success.

Round 3 · 3 phút (Full Stakeholder Strategy)

Trình bày stakeholder strategy hoàn chỉnh: stakeholders, engagement, alignment, relationships, implementation.

Good morning. Today, I will present our stakeholder strategy for the project. We have identified key stakeholders including investors, banks, JV partners, and regulatory authorities. Our engagement strategy focuses on building strategic relationships and driving stakeholder alignment. To achieve stakeholder alignment, we need to ensure all stakeholders understand the strategic vision and their role in achieving our goals. Building strategic relationships with key stakeholders is essential for long-term project success. We will implement regular communication, collaborative decision-making, and transparent reporting to maintain strong relationships with all stakeholders. This comprehensive approach will ensure all stakeholders are aligned and committed to project success. Thank you.

Common Mistakes

3 lỗi thường gặp khi stakeholder management

Tránh các lỗi này để stakeholder strategy của bạn professional và thể hiện executive capability.

Tránh

Không identifying stakeholders cụ thể: "We have many stakeholders."

Nên

We have identified key stakeholders including investors, banks, JV partners, and regulatory authorities.

Tránh

Không có engagement strategy rõ ràng: "We will work with stakeholders."

Nên

Our engagement strategy focuses on building strategic relationships and driving stakeholder alignment.

Tránh

Không nhấn mạnh strategic relationships: "We need to manage stakeholders."

Nên

Building strategic relationships with key stakeholders is essential for long-term project success.

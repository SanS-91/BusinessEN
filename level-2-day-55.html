Term Sheet Negotiation

Day 55 tập trung vào đàm phán term sheet: key terms, negotiation points, và deal closing. Đây là kỹ năng quan trọng để chốt thỏa thuận đầu tư với các điều khoản rõ ràng và công bằng.

Term sheet không chỉ là danh sách điều khoản. Đó là nền tảng của thỏa thuận đầu tư, nơi các bên thống nhất về equity, governance, profit distribution và exit mechanism. Một term sheet rõ ràng giúp tránh tranh chấp và đảm bảo quá trình closing diễn ra suôn sẻ.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Term Sheet Terms

8 thuật ngữ term sheet cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

Term Sheet

/tɜːrm it/

Bảng điều khoản

Ghi chú: Term sheet là tài liệu tóm tắt các điều khoản chính của thỏa thuận đầu tư trước khi soạn thảo hợp đồng chính thức.
  • Collocation: term sheet, proposed term sheet, negotiate term sheet
  • Example: We need to finalize the term sheet before proceeding to the definitive agreement.
  • Native alternative: term sheet, letter of intent

Key Terms

/kiː tɜːrmz/

Điều khoản chính

Ghi chú: Key terms là các điều khoản quan trọng nhất trong term sheet: equity, valuation, governance, exit.
  • Collocation: key terms, key terms include, negotiate key terms
  • Example: The key terms include equity split, valuation, governance structure, and exit mechanism.
  • Native alternative: key terms, main terms

Negotiation Points

/nɪˌɡoʊʃiˈeɪʃn pɔnts/

Điểm đàm phán

Ghi chú: Negotiation points là các điểm cần đàm phán trong term sheet.
  • Collocation: negotiation points, key negotiation points, main negotiation points
  • Example: The main negotiation points are equity split, board representation, and exit timeline.
  • Native alternative: negotiation points, negotiation items

Deal Closing

/diːl ˈkloʊzɪ/

Chốt giao dịch

Ghi chú: Deal closing là quá trình chốt giao dịch và ký kết hợp đồng chính thức.
  • Collocation: deal closing, closing process, closing date
  • Example: We expect deal closing within 60 days after finalizing the term sheet.
  • Native alternative: deal closing, transaction closing

Conditions Precedent

/kənˈdɪʃnz ˈpriːsɪdənt/

Điều kiện tiên quyết

Ghi chú: Conditions precedent là các điều kiện phải được đáp ứng trước khi giao dịch được hoàn tất.
  • Collocation: conditions precedent, satisfy conditions precedent, conditions precedent include
  • Example: Conditions precedent include regulatory approval and shareholder approval.
  • Native alternative: conditions precedent, closing conditions

Representations & Warranties

/ˌreprɪzenˈteɪʃnz ənd ˈwrəntiz/

Cam kết & Bảo đảm

Ghi chú: Representations & warranties là các cam kết và bảo đảm pháp lý của các bên trong hợp đồng.
  • Collocation: representations and warranties, breach of representations, standard representations
  • Example: The agreement includes standard representations and warranties from both parties.
  • Native alternative: reps and warranties, representations & warranties

Covenants

/ˈkʌvənənts/

Điều ước (điều kiện ràng buộc)

Ghi chú: Covenants là các điều kiện ràng buộc trong hợp đồng. Vi phạm covenant có thể dẫn đến hậu quả pháp lý.
  • Collocation: financial covenants, affirmative covenants, negative covenants
  • Example: The loan agreement includes financial covenants requiring a minimum DSCR of 1.5x.
  • Native alternative: covenants, loan covenants

Escrow

/ˈeskroʊ/

Tài khoản ký quỹ

Ghi chú: Escrow là tài khoản trung gian giữ tiền hoặc tài sản cho đến khi các điều kiện giao dịch được đáp ứng.
  • Collocation: escrow account, funds held in escrow, release from escrow
  • Example: 10% of the purchase price will be held in escrow until closing conditions are satisfied.
  • Native alternative: escrow account, escrow
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

6 mẫu câu đàm phán term sheet: proposing terms, negotiating key terms, handling objections, finding middle ground, closing deal, finalizing agreement.

We propose the following key terms...

Công thức: We propose the following key terms: + terms. Dùng để đề xuất các điều khoản chính.
Proposing terms

We propose the following key terms: 60-40 equity split, 5-year exit timeline, and equal board representation.

Substitution drill

Thay terms: 50-50 split / 3-year exit / majority board seats.

We need to negotiate the following points...

Công thức: We need to negotiate the following points: + points. Dùng để xác định các điểm cần đàm phán.
Negotiating key terms

We need to negotiate the following points: equity split, board representation, and profit distribution.

Substitution drill

Thay points: exit timeline / governance structure / capital call mechanism.

I understand your concern about..., but...

Công thức: I understand your concern about + concern, but + solution. Dùng để xử lý phản đối.
Handling objections

I understand your concern about the exit timeline, but we can offer a clear exit mechanism within 18 months.

Substitution drill

Thay concern: equity split / board control / profit distribution.

Let's find a middle ground on...

Công thức: Let's find a middle ground on + area: + proposal. Dùng để tìm điểm chung.
Finding middle ground

Let's find a middle ground on the equity split: we propose 55-45 with equal board representation.

Substitution drill

Thay area: exit timeline / board seats / profit distribution.

Our recommendation is to finalize the term sheet with...

Công thức: Our recommendation is to finalize the term sheet with + terms. Dùng để chốt thỏa thuận.
Closing deal

Our recommendation is to finalize the term sheet with a 60-40 equity split and clear exit mechanism.

Substitution drill

Thay terms: 50-50 split / equal board seats / clear profit distribution.

We believe this structure works for both parties...

Công thức: We believe this structure works for both parties + reason. Dùng để kết thúc đàm phán.
Finalizing agreement

We believe this structure works for both parties and ensures fair profit distribution.

Substitution drill

Thay reason: ensures strategic alignment / ensures operational control / ensures fair returns.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Term Sheet Negotiation

Nghe một buổi đàm phán term sheet mẫu. Đây là mô hình để bạn thực hành đàm phán term sheet.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm key terms discussion và negotiation flow.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện các patterns: proposing terms, negotiating key terms, handling objections.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích negotiation techniques: finding middle ground, closing deal.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm.

Transcript

[Proposing Terms]Good morning. Thank you for meeting with us today. We propose the following key terms: 60-40 equity split, 5-year exit timeline, and equal board representation.

[Negotiating Key Terms]We need to negotiate the following points: equity split, board representation, and profit distribution.

[Handling Objections]I understand your concern about the exit timeline, but we can offer a clear exit mechanism within 18 months.

[Finding Middle Ground]Let's find a middle ground on the equity split: we propose 55-45 with equal board representation.

[Closing Deal]Our recommendation is to finalize the term sheet with a 55-45 equity split, equal board representation, and a clear exit mechanism within 18 months.

[Finalizing Agreement]We believe this structure works for both parties and ensures fair profit distribution. Thank you.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: What are the proposed key terms?
  • Q2: What are the main negotiation points?
  • Q3: What is the concern about exit timeline?
  • Q4: What is the proposed middle ground on equity split?
  • Q5: What is the final recommendation?

Sample Answers

1. 60-40 equity split, 5-year exit timeline, and equal board representation.
2. Equity split, board representation, and profit distribution.
3. Concern about exit timeline; addressed by offering clear exit mechanism within 18 months.
4. 55-45 equity split with equal board representation.
5. Finalize term sheet with 55-45 equity split, equal board representation, and clear exit mechanism within 18 months.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Proposing terms

We propose the following key terms: 60-40 equity split, 5-year exit timeline, and equal board representation.

Shadow 2 · Handling objections

I understand your concern about the exit timeline, but we can offer a clear exit mechanism within 18 months.

Shadow 3 · Closing deal

Our recommendation is to finalize the term sheet with a 55-45 equity split, equal board representation, and a clear exit mechanism within 18 months.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: key terms, equity split, board representation, exit mechanism.
  • Giọng tự tin, chắc chắn khi đề xuất terms và closing.
  • Giọng hợp tác, tôn trọng khi handling objections.
  • Pause sau mỗi đề xuất để đối tác phản hồi.
Block 4 · 20 phút

Speaking · Term Sheet Negotiation

Luyện 3 vòng tăng dần: đàm phán ngắn → đàm phán đầy đủ → đàm phán hoàn chỉnh. Mục tiêu: tự tin đàm phán term sheet.

Round 1 · 3 phút

Đàm phán ngắn 3 phút: proposing terms, negotiating key terms, handling objections.

Terms: We propose the following key terms: 60-40 equity split, 5-year exit timeline, and equal board representation.

Negotiation: We need to negotiate the following points: equity split, board representation, and profit distribution.

Objections: I understand your concern about the exit timeline, but we can offer a clear exit mechanism within 18 months.

Round 2 · 4 phút

Mở rộng đàm phán lên 4 phút: thêm finding middle ground, closing deal.

Terms (1 min): We propose the following key terms: 60-40 equity split, 5-year exit timeline, and equal board representation.

Negotiation (1 min): We need to negotiate the following points: equity split, board representation, and profit distribution.

Objections (1 min): I understand your concern about the exit timeline, but we can offer a clear exit mechanism within 18 months.

Middle Ground (1 min): Let's find a middle ground on the equity split: we propose 55-45 with equal board representation. Our recommendation is to finalize the term sheet with a 55-45 equity split, equal board representation, and a clear exit mechanism within 18 months.

Round 3 · 5 phút

Đàm phán hoàn chỉnh 5 phút: thêm finalizing agreement.

Terms (1 min): We propose the following key terms: 60-40 equity split, 5-year exit timeline, and equal board representation.

Negotiation (1 min): We need to negotiate the following points: equity split, board representation, and profit distribution.

Objections (1 min): I understand your concern about the exit timeline, but we can offer a clear exit mechanism within 18 months.

Middle Ground (1 min): Let's find a middle ground on the equity split: we propose 55-45 with equal board representation.

Closing (1 min): Our recommendation is to finalize the term sheet with a 55-45 equity split, equal board representation, and a clear exit mechanism within 18 months. We believe this structure works for both parties and ensures fair profit distribution. Thank you.

Recording Task

Ghi âm Round 3 hoàn chỉnh: đàm phán 5 phút. Đây là buổi đàm phán term sheet hoàn chỉnh.

  • Bạn có tự tin, hợp tác khi đàm phán?
  • Bạn có xử lý phản đối của đối tác không?
  • Bạn có đề xuất middle ground và closing rõ ràng không?

Task 1: 1-Page Term Sheet Summary

Chuẩn bị 1-page term sheet summary cho dự án của bạn. Summary cần có: key terms, negotiation points, conditions precedent, exit mechanism.

Model Answer · 1-Page Term Sheet Summary

Term Sheet Summary

Riverside Phase 2 — JV Term Sheet

1. Key Terms

Equity Split: 55-45 (us/partner)

Board Representation: 5 seats (3 from us, 2 from partner)

Exit Timeline: 18 months from project completion

2. Negotiation Points

Equity split: 55-45 with equal board representation

Profit distribution: Proportional to equity ownership

Exit mechanism: Clear exit within 18 months

3. Conditions Precedent

Regulatory approval

Shareholder approval

Finalization of definitive agreement

4. Exit Mechanism

Primary exit: Sell completed project to institutional investor within 18 months

Target: 20% premium to cost

Escrow: 10% of purchase price held in escrow until closing conditions are satisfied

5. Recommendation

Recommendation: Finalize term sheet with 55-45 equity split, equal board representation, and clear exit mechanism within 18 months. The structure ensures strategic alignment and fair profit distribution for both parties.

Recording Checklist

  • Đàm phán có cấu trúc rõ ràng (terms, negotiation, objections, middle ground, closing)?
  • Bạn có tự tin, hợp tác khi đàm phán?
  • Bạn có xử lý phản đối của đối tác không?
  • Bạn có đề xuất middle ground và closing rõ ràng không?

Task 3: Self-Assessment Rubric

Tự chấm đàm phán theo 5 tiêu chí:

  • Structure (1-5): Có terms rõ ràng, negotiation rõ ràng, objections, middle ground, closing?
  • Confidence (1-5): Tự tin, hợp tác, không defensive khi đàm phán?
  • Flexibility (1-5): Có đề xuất middle ground và nhượng bộ hợp lý?
  • Clarity (1-5): Rõ ràng, dễ hiểu, không quá technical?
  • Closing (1-5): Closing rõ ràng, next step rõ ràng?

Reflection 3 dòng

Good: 1 điểm bạn đã làm tốt.

Improve: 1 điểm cần cải thiện.

Keep: 1 kỹ thuật bạn sẽ tiếp tục dùng.

Reflection mẫu

Good: Tôi đã xử lý phản đối của đối tác một cách hợp tác. Improve: Tôi cần đề xuất middle ground cụ thể hơn. Keep: "I understand your concern, but..."

Common Mistakes

4 lỗi thường gặp khi đàm phán term sheet

Tránh các lỗi này để buổi đàm phán term sheet của bạn chuyên nghiệp và thuyết phục.

Tránh

Đàm phán không có cấu trúc rõ ràng, nhảy lung tung giữa các điều khoản.

Nên

"We propose the following key terms: equity split, board representation, exit timeline."

Tránh

Defensive khi đối tác nêu phản đối: "No, that's not possible."

Nên

"I understand your concern about the exit timeline, but we can offer a clear exit mechanism within 18 months."

Tránh

Không đề xuất middle ground: "We need 60% equity stake."

Nên

"Let's find a middle ground on the equity split: we propose 55-45 with equal board representation."

Tránh

Kết thúc đột ngột: "That's all. Thanks." Không có next step.

Nên

"Our recommendation is to finalize the term sheet with a 55-45 equity split. We believe this structure works for both parties. Thank you."

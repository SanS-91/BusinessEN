Strategic Decision-Making

Day 72 tập trung vào framework ra quyết định chiến lược ở cấp executive: phân tích options, đánh giá trade-offs, và đưa ra quyết định với confidence. Bạn sẽ học cách trình bày decision framework, defend decisions trước board, và communicate decisions với stakeholders.

Executive decision-making không phải là chọn option tốt nhất. Đó là chọn option phù hợp nhất với strategic priorities, risk appetite, và organizational capabilities — và có thể defend decision đó trước board và stakeholders.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Decision Terms

8 thuật ngữ decision-making cốt lõi cho executive decision-making. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

decision framework

/dɪˈsʒn ˈfreɪmwɜːrk/

Framework ra quyết định

Ghi chú: Decision framework là framework có cấu trúc để đánh giá options và đưa ra quyết định. Bao gồm criteria, options analysis, và recommendation.
  • Collocation: decision framework, strategic decision framework, decision-making framework
  • Example: We use a structured decision framework to evaluate strategic options and make informed decisions.
  • Native alternative: decision framework, decision-making framework

strategic options

/strəˈtiːdʒɪk ɑːpʃnz/

Các lựa chọn chiến lược

Ghi chú: Strategic options là các lựa chọn chiến lược khả thi. Thường có 2-3 options để so sánh và đánh giá.
  • Collocation: strategic options, evaluate strategic options, strategic options analysis
  • Example: We have identified three strategic options: proceed with Phase 2, delay by 6 months, or pursue alternative investment.
  • Native alternative: strategic options, strategic alternatives

trade-offs

/treɪd ɒfs/

Sự đánh đổi

Ghi chú: Trade-offs là sự đánh đổi giữa các yếu tố. Mỗi option có trade-offs giữa risk, return, và timing.
  • Collocation: key trade-offs, trade-offs between, trade-off analysis
  • Example: The key trade-off is between higher returns with higher risk versus lower returns with lower risk.
  • Native alternative: trade-offs, trade-off decisions

risk appetite

/rɪsk ˈæpɪtaɪt/

Khẩu vị rủi ro

Ghi chú: Risk appetite là mức độ rủi ro mà tổ chức sẵn sàng chấp nhận. Quyết định phải phù hợp với risk appetite.
  • Collocation: risk appetite, risk appetite framework, within our risk appetite
  • Example: This option is within our risk appetite and aligns with our strategic priorities.
  • Native alternative: risk appetite, risk tolerance

decision criteria

/dɪˈsɪʒn kraɪˈtɪəriə/

Tiêu chí quyết định

Ghi chú: Decision criteria là các tiêu chí dùng để đánh giá và so sánh options. Thường bao gồm IRR, NPV, risk, và strategic fit.
  • Collocation: decision criteria, key decision criteria, evaluation criteria
  • Example: Our decision criteria include IRR above 15%, NPV positive, and strategic alignment with our core business.
  • Native alternative: decision criteria, evaluation criteria

go/no-go decision

/ɡoʊ noʊ ɡoʊ dɪˈsɪʒn/

Quyết định go/no-go

Ghi chú: Go/no-go decision là quyết định cuối cùng: proceed (go) hoặc stop (no-go). Đây là decision quan trọng nhất.
  • Collocation: go/no-go decision, make go/no-go decision, go/no-go decision point
  • Example: We need to make a go/no-go decision by end of this month.
  • Native alternative: go/no-go decision, proceed/stop decision

decision rationale

/dɪˈsɪʒn ˈræʃnəl/

Lý do quyết định

Ghi chú: Decision rationale là lý do và logic đằng sau quyết định. Phải rõ ràng và có thể defend trước board.
  • Collocation: decision rationale, clear decision rationale, decision rationale is based on
  • Example: The decision rationale is based on strong financial returns, manageable risk, and strategic alignment.
  • Native alternative: decision rationale, decision logic

stakeholder alignment

/steɪkhoʊldər əˈlaɪnmənt/

Sự đồng thuận của stakeholders

Ghi chú: Stakeholder alignment là sự đồng thuận của tất cả stakeholders về quyết định. Quan trọng để implement decision thành công.
  • Collocation: stakeholder alignment, achieve stakeholder alignment, stakeholder alignment is critical
  • Example: Stakeholder alignment is critical for successful implementation of this strategic decision.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder alignment, stakeholder consensus
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

4 mẫu câu decision-making: presenting options, analyzing trade-offs, making recommendation, defending decision.

We have identified three strategic options...

Công thức: We have identified + number + strategic options: option 1, option 2, or option 3. Dùng để trình bày các options khả thi.
Presenting options

We have identified three strategic options: proceed with Phase 2, delay by 6 months, or pursue alternative investment.

Substitution drill

Thay options: expand to new market / partner with competitor / maintain current strategy.

The key trade-off is between...

Công thức: The key trade-off is between + option A versus + option B. Dùng để phân tích trade-offs giữa các options.
Analyzing trade-offs

The key trade-off is between higher returns with higher risk versus lower returns with lower risk.

Substitution drill

Thay trade-off: faster growth with higher risk / slower growth with lower risk / balanced approach.

Based on our analysis, we recommend...

Công thức: Based on our analysis, we recommend + action + rationale. Dùng để đưa ra recommendation với rationale rõ ràng.
Making recommendation

Based on our analysis, we recommend proceeding with Phase 2, as it offers the best risk-adjusted returns.

Substitution drill

Thay action: proceeding with expansion / pursuing partnership / maintaining current strategy.

This decision is aligned with our strategic priorities because...

Công thức: This decision is aligned with our strategic priorities because + rationale. Dùng để defend decision trước board.
Defending decision

This decision is aligned with our strategic priorities because it delivers strong returns while maintaining manageable risk.

Substitution drill

Thay rationale: it aligns with our growth strategy / it fits our risk appetite / it supports our strategic vision.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Decision Presentation to Board

Nghe một bài decision presentation mẫu trước Board 5 phút. Đây là mô hình để bạn tái hiện với decision thật của mình.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm strategic options và recommendation.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện 4 patterns: presenting options, analyzing trade-offs, making recommendation, defending decision.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích: decision rationale, trade-offs analysis, strategic alignment.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm.

Transcript

[Opening]Good morning, Board members. Today, I will present our strategic decision framework for Phase 2.

[Presenting Options]We have identified three strategic options: proceed with Phase 2, delay by 6 months, or pursue alternative investment.

[Analyzing Trade-offs]The key trade-off is between higher returns with higher risk versus lower returns with lower risk.

[Making Recommendation]Based on our analysis, we recommend proceeding with Phase 2, as it offers the best risk-adjusted returns.

[Defending Decision]This decision is aligned with our strategic priorities because it delivers strong returns while maintaining manageable risk.

[Closing]Thank you. I'm happy to take your questions.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: What are the three strategic options identified?
  • Q2: What is the key trade-off mentioned?
  • Q3: What is the recommendation?
  • Q4: Why is this decision aligned with strategic priorities?

Sample Answers

1. Proceed with Phase 2, delay by 6 months, or pursue alternative investment.
2. Higher returns with higher risk versus lower returns with lower risk.
3. Proceeding with Phase 2.
4. Because it delivers strong returns while maintaining manageable risk.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Presenting Options

We have identified three strategic options: proceed with Phase 2, delay by 6 months, or pursue alternative investment.

Shadow 2 · Making Recommendation

Based on our analysis, we recommend proceeding with Phase 2, as it offers the best risk-adjusted returns.

Shadow 3 · Defending Decision

This decision is aligned with our strategic priorities because it delivers strong returns while maintaining manageable risk.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: strategic options, key trade-off, recommendation, strategic priorities.
  • Giọng chắc chắn khi presenting options và making recommendation.
  • Giọng tự tin khi defending decision.
  • Ngắt nghỉ sau mỗi option và mỗi key point.
Block 4 · 20 phút

Speaking · Decision Presentation

Luyện 3 vòng: từ presenting options đến full decision presentation. Mục tiêu là thể hiện executive decision-making confidence.

Round 1 · 60 giây (Presenting Options)

Chỉ trình bày strategic options và key trade-offs. Chưa cần đưa ra recommendation.

Good morning, Board members. Today, I will present our strategic decision framework for Phase 2. We have identified three strategic options: proceed with Phase 2, delay by 6 months, or pursue alternative investment. The key trade-off is between higher returns with higher risk versus lower returns with lower risk.

Round 2 · 90 giây (Trade-offs & Recommendation)

Thêm analysis trade-offs và making recommendation.

Good morning, Board members. Today, I will present our strategic decision framework for Phase 2. We have identified three strategic options: proceed with Phase 2, delay by 6 months, or pursue alternative investment. The key trade-off is between higher returns with higher risk versus lower returns with lower risk. Based on our analysis, we recommend proceeding with Phase 2, as it offers the best risk-adjusted returns.

Round 3 · 3 phút (Full Decision Presentation)

Trình bày decision hoàn chỉnh: options, trade-offs, recommendation, defense, closing.

Good morning, Board members. Today, I will present our strategic decision framework for Phase 2. We have identified three strategic options: proceed with Phase 2, delay by 6 months, or pursue alternative investment. The key trade-off is between higher returns with higher risk versus lower returns with lower risk. Based on our analysis, we recommend proceeding with Phase 2, as it offers the best risk-adjusted returns. This decision is aligned with our strategic priorities because it delivers strong returns while maintaining manageable risk. Thank you. I'm happy to take your questions.

Output · Decision Framework Presentation

Task 1: Viết Decision Framework Script 200-250 từ

Viết một decision framework script chuyên nghiệp, dùng trong cuộc họp với Hội đồng quản trị.

Yêu cầu

  • Dùng ít nhất 4 từ vựng trong mục Vocabulary.
  • Dùng đủ 4 patterns: presenting options, analyzing trade-offs, making recommendation, defending decision.
  • Có cấu trúc: Opening → Options → Trade-offs → Recommendation → Defense → Closing.
  • Không đi vào chi tiết vận hành — chỉ strategic level.
  • Giọng văn executive, rõ ràng, có confidence.

Model Answer

Good morning, Board members. Today, I will present our strategic decision framework for Phase 2. We have identified three strategic options: proceed with Phase 2, delay by 6 months, or pursue alternative investment. The key trade-off is between higher returns with higher risk versus lower returns with lower risk. Based on our analysis, we recommend proceeding with Phase 2, as it offers the best risk-adjusted returns. This decision is aligned with our strategic priorities because it delivers strong returns while maintaining manageable risk. Thank you. I'm happy to take your questions.

Common Mistakes

3 lỗi thường gặp khi trình bày decision trước Board

Tránh các lỗi này để decision presentation của bạn executive và có confidence.

Tránh

Không trình bày options rõ ràng: "We should do this."

Nên

We have identified three strategic options: proceed with Phase 2, delay by 6 months, or pursue alternative investment.

Tránh

Không phân tích trade-offs: "This is the best option."

Nên

The key trade-off is between higher returns with higher risk versus lower returns with lower risk.

Tránh

Không defend decision: "We recommend this."

Nên

This decision is aligned with our strategic priorities because it delivers strong returns while maintaining manageable risk.

Deal Closing & Final Negotiation

Day 68 tập trung vào giai đoạn cuối cùng của đàm phán: chốt deal, xử lý các phản đối phút chót, và di chuyển đến ký kết. Bạn sẽ học cách tự tin chốt deal, xử lý last-minute objections, và moving to sign-off một cách chuyên nghiệp.

Giai đoạn closing không phải là ép buộc. Đó là xác nhận lại giá trị, xử lý các lo ngại cuối cùng, và dẫn dắt đối tác đến quyết định. Một closing tốt không để lại sự mơ hồ — nó để lại sự rõ ràng và cam kết.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Closing Terms

8 thuật ngữ deal closing cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ và native alternative.

closing conditions

/ˈkloʊzɪŋ kənˈdɪʃnz/

Điều kiện chốt deal

Ghi chú: Closing conditions là các điều kiện phải được đáp ứng trước khi deal được chốt. Ví dụ: regulatory approval, financing.
  • Collocation: closing conditions, satisfy closing conditions, subject to closing conditions
  • Example: The deal is subject to closing conditions, including regulatory approval and financing.
  • Native alternative: closing conditions, conditions precedent

final negotiation

/ˈfaɪnl nɪˌɡoʊʃiˈeɪn/

Đàm phán cuối cùng

Ghi chú: Final negotiation là vòng đàm phán cuối cùng để chốt các điều khoản cuối cùng trước khi ký.
  • Collocation: final negotiation, final negotiation round, enter final negotiation
  • Example: We are entering the final negotiation round to finalize the remaining terms.
  • Native alternative: final negotiation, final round

sign-off

/ˈsaɪn ɒf/

Phê duyệt, ký duyệt

Ghi chú: Sign-off là sự phê duyệt chính thức. Thường dùng khi nói về việc lãnh đạo phê duyệt deal.
  • Collocation: sign-off, get sign-off, final sign-off
  • Example: We need final sign-off from the board before moving to execution.
  • Native alternative: approval, sign-off, approval

binding agreement

/ˈbaɪndɪŋ əˈɡriːmənt/

Thỏa thuận ràng buộc

Ghi chú: Binding agreement là thỏa thuận có tính ràng buộc pháp lý. Khác với non-binding term sheet.
  • Collocation: binding agreement, legally binding agreement, enter into a binding agreement
  • Example: Once signed, this becomes a binding agreement for both parties.
  • Native alternative: binding agreement, binding contract

closing date

/ˈkloʊzɪŋ deɪt/

Ngày chốt deal

Ghi chú: Closing date là ngày chính thức chốt deal và ký kết. Thường được agreed upon trong final negotiation.
  • Collocation: closing date, target closing date, set a closing date
  • Example: We are targeting a closing date of March 31st, subject to regulatory approval.
  • Native alternative: closing date, completion date

final terms

/ˈfaɪnl tɜrmz/

Điều khoản cuối cùng

Ghi chú: Final terms là các điều khoản cuối cùng được agreed upon trước khi ký binding agreement.
  • Collocation: final terms, agree on final terms, finalize final terms
  • Example: We have agreed on the final terms and are ready to move to signing.
  • Native alternative: final terms, final terms and conditions

execution

/ˌeksɪˈkjuʃn/

Thực thi, ký kết

Ghi chú: Execution là quá trình thực thi deal sau khi ký. Trong M&A, execution cũng có nghĩa là ký kết.
  • Collocation: deal execution, execution phase, move to execution
  • Example: Once signed, we will move to the execution phase immediately.
  • Native alternative: execution, implementation

closing statement

/ˈkloʊzɪŋ ˈsteɪtmənt/

Tuyên bố chốt deal

Ghi chú: Closing statement là tuyên bố cuối cùng để chốt deal, thường bao gồm next steps và timeline.
  • Collocation: closing statement, deliver closing statement, final closing statement
  • Example: In my closing statement, I will summarize the key terms and next steps.
  • Native alternative: closing statement, closing remarks
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

4 mẫu câu closing: wrapping up, addressing final concerns, confirming terms, moving to sign-off.

To wrap up our discussion...

Công thức: To wrap up our discussion + summary. Dùng để bắt đầu phần closing, tóm tắt lại các điểm đã đồng thuận.
Wrapping up

To wrap up our discussion, let me summarize the key points we've agreed on.

Substitution drill

Thay summary: the key terms / the next steps / the timeline.

Before we move to sign-off...

Công thức: Before we move to sign-off + action. Dùng để xử lý các lo ngại cuối cùng trước khi ký.
Addressing final concerns

Before we move to sign-off, let's address any final concerns.

Substitution drill

Thay action: clarify the final terms / confirm the timeline / address any remaining issues.

Let's confirm the final terms...

Công thức: Let's confirm the final terms + before action. Dùng để xác nhận lại các điều khoản cuối cùng.
Confirming terms

Let's confirm the final terms before we proceed to signing.

Substitution drill

Thay action: before we move to execution / before we finalize the agreement.

We are ready to move to execution...

Công thức: We are ready to move to execution + condition. Dùng để tuyên bố sẵn sàng chốt deal.
Moving to sign-off

We are ready to move to execution, subject to final sign-off from the board.

Substitution drill

Thay condition: subject to regulatory approval / subject to financing / subject to final terms.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Final Negotiation

Nghe một buổi final negotiation mẫu 5 phút. Đây là mô hình để bạn thực hành closing deal.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm closing sequence.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện các patterns: wrapping up, addressing final concerns, confirming terms, moving to sign-off.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích: cách xử lý last-minute objections, cách chốt timeline.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm.

Transcript

[Wrapping Up]To wrap up our discussion, let me summarize the key points we've agreed on. We've agreed on the valuation, the governance structure, and the exit strategy.

[Addressing Final Concerns]Before we move to sign-off, let's address any final concerns. I understand there's a concern about the closing date. We are targeting March 31st, subject to regulatory approval.

[Confirming Terms]Let's confirm the final terms before we proceed to signing. We've agreed on the valuation at $500 million, with a 20% earn-out based on performance.

[Moving to Sign-off]We are ready to move to execution, subject to final sign-off from the board. Thank you. We look forward to a successful partnership.

Comprehension Questions

  • Question 1: What are the three key points agreed upon?
  • Question 2: What is the target closing date?
  • Question 3: What is the agreed valuation?
  • Question 4: What is the condition for moving to execution?

Sample Answers

1. Valuation, governance structure, and exit strategy.
2. March 31st, subject to regulatory approval.
3. $500 million, with a 20% earn-out based on performance.
4. Subject to final sign-off from the board.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Wrapping Up

To wrap up our discussion, let me summarize the key points we've agreed on.

Shadow 2 · Addressing Concerns

Before we move to sign-off, let's address any final concerns.

Shadow 3 · Moving to Sign-off

We are ready to move to execution, subject to final sign-off from the board.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: wrap up, summarize, sign-off, execution.
  • Giọng chắc chắn khi confirming terms: "Let's CONFIRM the FINAL terms..."
  • Giọng tự tin khi moving to sign-off: "We are READY to move to EXECUTION..."
  • Ngắt nghỉ sau mỗi key point để tạo impact.
Block 4 · 20 phút

Speaking · Deal Closing

Luyện 3 vòng tăng dần. Mục tiêu cuối cùng: trình bày closing statement hoàn chỉnh 3 phút.

Round 1 · Addressing Final Concerns (2 phút)

Chỉ tập trung vào addressing final concerns và confirming timeline.

Before we move to sign-off, let's address any final concerns. I understand there's a concern about the closing date. We are targeting March 31st, subject to regulatory approval. We are confident we can meet this timeline, and we will provide weekly updates on the regulatory process.

Round 2 · Confirming Terms (2 phút)

Thêm confirming final terms và moving to execution.

Let's confirm the final terms before we proceed to signing. We've agreed on the valuation at $500 million, with a 20% earn-out based on performance. We are ready to move to execution, subject to final sign-off from the board. We look forward to a successful partnership.

Round 3 · Full Closing Statement (3 phút)

Hoàn chỉnh closing statement: wrapping up, addressing concerns, confirming terms, moving to sign-off.

To wrap up our discussion, let me summarize the key points we've agreed on. We've agreed on the valuation, the governance structure, and the exit strategy. Before we move to sign-off, let's address any final concerns. I understand there's a concern about the closing date. We are targeting March 31st, subject to regulatory approval. Let's confirm the final terms before we proceed to signing. We've agreed on the valuation at $500 million, with a 20% earn-out based on performance. We are ready to move to execution, subject to final sign-off from the board. Thank you. We look forward to a successful partnership.

Common Mistakes

4 lỗi thường gặp khi closing deal

Tránh các lỗi này để closing statement của bạn chuyên nghiệp và thuyết phục.

Tránh

Không wrapping up: "That's all. Any questions?"

Nên

To wrap up our discussion, let me summarize the key points we've agreed on.

Tránh

Không addressing final concerns: "Any other concerns?"

Nên

Before we move to sign-off, let's address any final concerns.

Tránh

Không confirming final terms: "We agreed on the terms."

Nên

Let's confirm the final terms before we proceed to signing.

Tránh

Không moving to sign-off rõ ràng: "We'll sign soon."

Nên

We are ready to move to execution, subject to final sign-off from the board.

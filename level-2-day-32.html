Professional Persuasion & Influence

Day 32 dạy bạn nghệ thuật thuyết phục trong môi trường chuyên nghiệp: thuyết phục stakeholders, xây dựng consensus, và influence decisions với confidence và maturity.

Persuasion không phải là manipulation. Đó là nghệ thuật trình bày ý tưởng một cách rõ ràng, logic, và compelling để người khác tự nguyện đồng ý và hành động.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Persuasion Terms

8 thuật ngữ persuasion cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

persuasion

/pərˈsweɪ.ʒən/

Nghệ thuật thuyết phục

Ghi chú: Persuasion là nghệ thuật influence người khác thông qua arguments rõ ràng, logic, và compelling. Khác với manipulation.
  • Collocation: persuasion skills, persuasion strategy, effective persuasion
  • Example: Persuasion is the art of influencing others through clear, logical, and compelling arguments.
  • Native alternative: influence, persuasion techniques

influence

/ˈɪn.flu.əns/

Ảnh hưởng, thuyết phục

Ghi chú: Influence là khả năng ảnh hưởng đến decisions và actions của người khác thông qua persuasion.
  • Collocation: influence stakeholders, influence decisions, positive influence
  • Example: We need to influence the stakeholders to support this proposal.
  • Native alternative: persuade, influence others

consensus

/kənˈsen.səs/

Sự đồng thuận

Ghi chú: Consensus là sự đồng thuận chung của tất cả stakeholders. Building consensus takes time nhưng tạo commitment mạnh hơn.
  • Collocation: build consensus, reach consensus, consensus building
  • Example: We need to build consensus among all stakeholders before moving forward.
  • Native alternative: agreement, consensus

stakeholder

/steɪk.hoʊl.dər/

Các bên liên quan

Ghi chú: Stakeholders là tất cả những người bị ảnh hưởng bởi decision hoặc có interest trong outcome.
  • Collocation: key stakeholders, stakeholder interests, stakeholder engagement
  • Example: We need to consider the interests of all stakeholders in this decision.
  • Native alternative: stakeholders, interested parties

compelling argument

/kəmˈpel.ɪ ˈɑːr.ɡjə.mənt/

Lập luận thuyết phục

Ghi chú: Compelling argument là lập luận mạnh mẽ, logic, và convincing khiến người khác phải đồng ý.
  • Collocation: compelling argument, compelling case, compelling evidence
  • Example: We need to present a compelling argument to persuade the board.
  • Native alternative: compelling case, strong argument

buy-in

/ˈbaɪ.ɪn/

Sự đồng ý, ủng hộ

Ghi chú: Buy-in là sự đồng ý và ủng hộ từ stakeholders. Getting buy-in là bước quan trọng trước khi implement changes.
  • Collocation: get buy-in, stakeholder buy-in, buy-in from stakeholders
  • Example: We need to get buy-in from all stakeholders before implementing this change.
  • Native alternative: agreement, support, buy-in

objection

/əbˈdʒek.ʃən/

Sự phản đối, phản đối

Ghi chú: Objection là sự phản đối hoặc concern từ stakeholders. Handling objections effectively là kỹ năng quan trọng.
  • Collocation: handle objections, address objections, potential objections
  • Example: We need to address potential objections before presenting this proposal.
  • Native alternative: concern, objection, concern

persuasion strategy

/pərˈsweɪ.ʒən ˈstræt.ə.dʒi/

Chiến lược thuyết phục

Ghi chú: Persuasion strategy là kế hoạch rõ ràng để influence stakeholders và achieve buy-in.
  • Collocation: persuasion strategy, clear persuasion strategy, effective persuasion strategy
  • Example: We need a clear persuasion strategy to influence the board's decision.
  • Native alternative: influence strategy, persuasion approach
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

6 mẫu câu persuasion cốt lõi. Mỗi mẫu có công thức, ví dụ, substitution drill, common mistake và native alternative.

Based on our analysis, I recommend...

Công thức: Based on our analysis, I recommend + action + because + reason. Dùng để present recommendation với credibility.
Opening with credibility

Based on our analysis, I recommend we proceed with Option A because it offers the best risk-adjusted returns.

Substitution drill

Thay action và reason: proceed with the acquisition / move forward with the partnership / because it aligns with our strategic goals.

Common mistake

Tránh: "I think we should do this." (No credibility) Nên: "Based on our analysis, I recommend..."

The data shows that...

Công thức: The data shows that + evidence + benefit. Dùng để present compelling arguments với evidence.
Presenting evidence

The data shows that this approach will reduce costs by 20% while improving quality.

Substitution drill

Thay evidence và benefit: will increase revenue by 30% / will improve efficiency by 25% / will reduce risks significantly.

Common mistake

Tránh: "I think this will work." (No evidence) Nên: "The data shows that..."

I understand your concern, and...

Công thức: I understand your concern about + concern, and + solution. Dùng để handle objections với empathy.
Handling objections

I understand your concern about the timeline, and here's how we can address it.

Substitution drill

Thay concern và solution: about the budget / about the risks / here's our mitigation plan.

Common mistake

Tránh: "You're wrong." (Dismissive) Nên: "I understand your concern, and..."

If we don't act now, we risk...

Công thức: If we don't act now, we risk + consequence. Dùng để create urgency và motivate action.
Creating urgency

If we don't act now, we risk losing this opportunity to our competitors.

Substitution drill

Thay consequence: losing market share / missing the deadline / losing competitive advantage.

Common mistake

Tránh: "We should do this soon." (No urgency) Nên: "If we don't act now, we risk..."

Let's find a solution that works for everyone...

Công thức: Let's find a solution that works for everyone + benefit. Dùng để build consensus và show inclusivity.
Building consensus

Let's find a solution that works for everyone and addresses all stakeholders' concerns.

Substitution drill

Thay benefit: that benefits all parties / that addresses everyone's concerns / that works for all stakeholders.

Common mistake

Tránh: "My way is the only way." (Exclusive) Nên: "Let's find a solution that works for everyone..."

My recommendation is clear: we should...

Công thức: My recommendation is clear: we should + action + urgency/reason. Dùng để close với clear call to action.
Closing with conviction

My recommendation is clear: we should proceed with this proposal immediately to capture this opportunity.

Substitution drill

Thay action và urgency: move forward with the partnership / proceed with the acquisition / to capture this window of opportunity.

Common mistake

Tránh: "I think we should maybe consider..." (Weak) Nên: "My recommendation is clear: we should..."

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Persuasion Pitch with Stakeholders

Nghe một bài persuasion pitch mẫu với stakeholders. Đây là mô hình để bạn thực hành persuasion effectively.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm persuasion structure và stakeholder engagement.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện các patterns: credibility building, argument presentation, objection handling.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích persuasion techniques: evidence-based arguments, empathy, urgency, call to action.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm (credibility opening, evidence presentation, closing).

Transcript

[Opening with Credibility]
Good morning everyone. Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today. Based on our comprehensive analysis, I'd like to present our recommendation for the new project.

[Presenting Evidence]
The data shows that this approach will reduce costs by 20% while improving quality by 15%. This is based on our analysis of similar projects and market conditions.

[Handling Objections]
I understand your concern about the timeline, and here's how we can address it: we've built in a 3-month buffer to account for potential delays. This ensures we can deliver on time even if unexpected issues arise.

[Creating Urgency]
If we don't act now, we risk losing this opportunity to our competitors who are already moving forward with similar initiatives. The window of opportunity is closing.

[Closing with Conviction]
My recommendation is clear: we should proceed with this proposal immediately to capture this window of opportunity. I'm happy to address any questions or concerns you may have.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: How does the presenter build credibility at the opening?
  • Q2: What evidence does the presenter present?
  • Q3: How does the presenter handle the timeline concern?
  • Q4: What urgency does the presenter create?
  • Q5: What is the presenter's final recommendation?

Sample Answers

1. The presenter builds credibility by saying "Based on our comprehensive analysis..."
2. The data shows the approach will reduce costs by 20% while improving quality by 15%.
3. The presenter handles the timeline concern by saying they've built in a 3-month buffer.
4. The presenter creates urgency by saying "If we don't act now, we risk losing this opportunity to our competitors."
5. The presenter's final recommendation is: "We should proceed with this proposal immediately to capture this window of opportunity."

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Credibility opening

Based on our comprehensive analysis, I'd like to present our recommendation for the new project.

Shadow 2 · Evidence presentation

The data shows that this approach will reduce costs by 20% while improving quality by 15%.

Shadow 3 · Closing with conviction

My recommendation is clear: we should proceed with this proposal immediately to capture this window of opportunity.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: comprehensive analysis, data shows, recommendation, immediately.
  • Giọng tự tin, chắc chắn khi present recommendation.
  • Giọng empathetic khi handle objections.
  • Giọng urgent khi create urgency.
  • Pause sau mỗi key point để stakeholders tiêu hóa thông tin.
Block 4 · 20 phút

Speaking · Persuasion Pitch Practice

Luyện 3 vòng tăng dần: build credibility → present arguments → full persuasion pitch với objection handling. Mục tiêu: tự tin persuade stakeholders effectively.

Round 1 · Build Credibility

Practice opening với credibility. Mục tiêu: establish credibility và present recommendation clearly.

Good morning everyone. Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today. Based on our comprehensive analysis, I'd like to present our recommendation for the new project. The data shows that this approach will reduce costs by 20% while improving quality by 15%.

Round 2 · Present Arguments & Handle Objections

Practice presenting arguments và handling objections. Mục tiêu: present compelling arguments và handle concerns với empathy.

The data shows that this approach will reduce costs by 20% while improving quality by 15%. I understand your concern about the timeline, and here's how we can address it: we've built in a 3-month buffer to account for potential delays. This ensures we can deliver on time even if unexpected issues arise.

Round 3 · Full Persuasion Pitch

Full persuasion pitch với objection handling và closing. Mục tiêu: complete, confident persuasion pitch.

Good morning everyone. Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today. Based on our comprehensive analysis, I'd like to present our recommendation for the new project. The data shows that this approach will reduce costs by 20% while improving quality by 15%. I understand your concern about the timeline, and here's how we can address it: we've built in a 3-month buffer to account for potential delays. If we don't act now, we risk losing this opportunity to our competitors who are already moving forward. My recommendation is clear: we should proceed with this proposal immediately to capture this window of opportunity. I'm happy to address any questions or concerns you may have.

Recording Task

Ghi âm Round 3 hoàn chỉnh: full persuasion pitch với objection handling. Đây là bài test tổng hợp.

Self-review

  • Bạn có establish credibility ở opening?
  • Bạn có present compelling arguments với evidence?
  • Bạn có handle objections với empathy?
  • Bạn có create urgency?
  • Bạn có close với clear call to action?

Task 3: Reflection 3 dòng

Sau khi hoàn thành, hãy viết ngắn gọn 3 dòng:

  • Good: 1 điểm bạn đã làm tốt.
  • Improve: 1 điểm cần cải thiện ở lần sau.
  • Keep: 1 kỹ thuật bạn sẽ tiếp tục dùng.
Reflection mẫu

Good: Tôi đã establish credibility ở opening và present compelling arguments. Improve: Tôi cần handle objections với nhiều empathy hơn. Keep: "Based on our analysis, I recommend..."

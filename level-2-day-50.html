JV/M&A Presentation

Day 50 là ngày tổng hợp Week 7. Bạn sẽ thực hiện một bài trình bày JV/M&A deal hoàn chỉnh 10 phút: structure, terms, strategic rationale, và benefits. Đây là cơ hội để bạn thực hành toàn bộ kỹ năng đã học trong Week 7.

Một bài trình bày JV/M&A không chỉ là liệt kê các điều khoản. Đó là kể một câu chuyện chiến lược: tại sao deal này hợp lý, structure này tối ưu, và benefits này thuyết phục. Người nghe không nhớ các điều khoản — họ nhớ câu chuyện chiến lược bạn kể.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & JV/M&A Terms

8 thuật ngữ JV/M&A cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

Deal Rationale

/diːl ˈræʃnəl/

Lý do giao dịch

Ghi chú: Deal Rationale là lý do chiến lược đằng sau giao dịch JV/M&A. Tại sao deal này hợp lý?
  • Collocation: strategic deal rationale, compelling deal rationale, deal rationale is based on
  • Example: The deal rationale is based on three pillars: market expansion, technology access, and cost synergies.
  • Native alternative: deal rationale, strategic rationale

Strategic Fit

/strəˈtiːdk fɪt/

Sự phù hợp chiến lược

Ghi chú: Strategic Fit là mức độ phù hợp chiến lược giữa hai bên trong giao dịch JV/M&A.
  • Collocation: strong strategic fit, strategic fit between, strategic fit with our strategy
  • Example: This acquisition represents a strong strategic fit with our expansion strategy into the Asian market.
  • Native alternative: strategic fit, strategic alignment

Value Creation

/ˈvæljuː kriˈeɪʃn/

Tạo giá trị

Ghi chú: Value Creation là quá trình tạo ra giá trị cho cổ đông thông qua giao dịch JV/M&A.
  • Collocation: value creation drivers, value creation strategy, significant value creation
  • Example: The primary value creation drivers are revenue synergies and cost optimization.
  • Native alternative: value generation, value enhancement

Deal Structure

/diːl ˈstrʌktʃər/

Cấu trúc giao dịch

Ghi chú: Deal Structure là cấu trúc pháp lý và tài chính của giao dịch JV/M&A.
  • Collocation: proposed deal structure, optimal deal structure, deal structure includes
  • Example: The proposed deal structure includes a 60-40 joint venture with a clear exit strategy.
  • Native alternative: deal structure, transaction structure

Key Terms

/kiː tɜrmz/

Điều khoản chính

Ghi chú: Key Terms là các điều khoản chính trong thỏa thuận JV/M&A.
  • Collocation: key terms of the deal, key terms include, key terms and conditions
  • Example: The key terms of the deal include valuation, governance structure, and exit mechanism.
  • Native alternative: key terms, deal terms

Synergies

/ˈsɪnədʒiz/

Hiệp lực

Ghi chú: Synergies là những lợi ích cộng hưởng khi hai công ty kết hợp trong giao dịch JV/M&A.
  • Collocation: revenue synergies, cost synergies, realize synergies
  • Example: We expect to realize $50 million in annual cost synergies within the first two years.
  • Native alternative: synergies, synergy benefits

Risk Mitigation

/rɪsk ˌmɪtɪˈɡeɪʃn/

Giảm thiểu rủi ro

Ghi chú: Risk Mitigation là các biện pháp giảm thiểu rủi ro trong giao dịch JV/M&A.
  • Collocation: risk mitigation strategies, risk mitigation measures, effective risk mitigation
  • Example: Our risk mitigation strategies include fixed-price contracts and phased implementation.
  • Native alternative: risk mitigation, risk reduction

Closing Argument

/ˈkloʊzɪŋ ˈɑːrɡjəmənt/

Lập luận kết thúc

Ghi chú: Closing Argument là lập luận kết thúc thuyết phục trong bài trình bày JV/M&A.
  • Collocation: strong closing argument, compelling closing argument, closing argument is
  • Example: Our closing argument is simple: this deal makes strategic sense and creates significant value.
  • Native alternative: closing argument, closing statement
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

6 mẫu câu cho JV/M&A presentation: opening with rationale, presenting strategic fit, explaining value creation, detailing deal structure, highlighting synergies, closing with conviction.

The strategic rationale for this deal is...

Công thức: The strategic rationale for this deal is + pillars/drivers. Dùng để mở đầu bài trình bày với lý do chiến lược.
Opening with rationale

The strategic rationale for this deal is based on three pillars: market expansion, technology access, and cost synergies.

Substitution drill

Thay pillars: market timing, execution excellence, operational efficiency / strong fundamentals, proven track record, attractive valuation.

This deal represents a strong strategic fit because...

Công thức: This deal represents a strong strategic fit because + reason. Dùng để trình bày sự phù hợp chiến lược.
Presenting strategic fit

This deal represents a strong strategic fit because it aligns with our expansion strategy into the Asian market.

Substitution drill

Thay reason: it complements our existing product portfolio / it provides access to new customer segments.

The primary value creation drivers are...

Công thức: The primary value creation drivers are + drivers. Dùng để giải thích các động lực tạo giá trị.
Explaining value creation

The primary value creation drivers are revenue synergies of $50 million and cost optimization of $30 million annually.

Substitution drill

Thay drivers: market expansion, technology access, cost synergies / revenue growth, operational efficiency.

The proposed deal structure includes...

Công thức: The proposed deal structure includes + components. Dùng để chi tiết hóa cấu trúc giao dịch.
Detailing deal structure

The proposed deal structure includes a 60-40 joint venture with a clear exit strategy and governance framework.

Substitution drill

Thay components: a 50-50 partnership / a majority acquisition / a strategic alliance.

We expect to realize synergies in...

Công thức: We expect to realize synergies in + areas. Dùng để nhấn mạnh các hiệp lực dự kiến.
Highlighting synergies

We expect to realize synergies in revenue growth of $50 million and cost savings of $30 million within the first two years.

Substitution drill

Thay areas: revenue synergies, cost synergies, operational efficiencies / market expansion, technology access.

In conclusion, this deal makes strategic sense because...

Công thức: In conclusion, this deal makes strategic sense because + reasons. Dùng để kết thúc bài trình bày với lập luận thuyết phục.
Closing with conviction

In conclusion, this deal makes strategic sense because it creates significant value, aligns with our strategy, and has manageable risks.

Substitution drill

Thay reasons: it creates value, aligns with strategy, has manageable risks / it drives growth, enhances capabilities, mitigates risks.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · JV/M&A Presentation

Nghe một bài JV/M&A pitch mẫu 10 phút. Đây là mô hình để bạn thực hành mock JV/M&A presentation.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm pitch structure và Q&A flow.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện các patterns: deal rationale, strategic fit, value creation, deal structure.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích defense techniques: acknowledge concern, provide evidence, restate recommendation.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm (pitch opening, thesis statement, closing recommendation).

Transcript

[Pitch Opening]Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here. Let me walk you through the investment case for our proposed acquisition of TechCorp. I'll cover the strategic rationale, the deal structure, the value creation drivers, and our recommendation.

[Strategic Rationale]The strategic rationale for this deal is based on three pillars: market expansion into the Asian market, access to cutting-edge AI technology, and significant cost synergies.

[Strategic Fit]This deal represents a strong strategic fit because it aligns perfectly with our 5-year growth strategy.

[Value Creation]The primary value creation drivers are revenue synergies of $50 million and cost optimization of $30 million annually.

[Deal Structure]The proposed deal structure includes a 60-40 joint venture with a clear exit strategy and governance framework.

[Synergies]We expect to realize synergies in revenue growth of $50 million and cost savings of $30 million within the first two years.

[Closing Recommendation]In conclusion, this deal makes strategic sense because it creates significant value, aligns with our strategy, and has manageable risks. My recommendation is clear: we should approve this acquisition by the end of this month. Thank you. I'm happy to take any questions.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: What are the three pillars of the strategic rationale?
  • Q2: Why does this deal represent a strong strategic fit?
  • Q3: What are the primary value creation drivers?
  • Q4: What does the proposed deal structure include?
  • Q5: What is the speaker's recommendation?

Sample Answers

1. Three pillars: market expansion into Asian market, access to AI technology, cost synergies.
2. It aligns perfectly with the 5-year growth strategy.
3. Revenue synergies of $50 million and cost optimization of $30 million annually.
4. A 60-40 joint venture with clear exit strategy and governance framework.
5. Approve the acquisition by the end of this month.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Pitch opening

Let me walk you through the investment case for our proposed acquisition of TechCorp. I'll cover the strategic rationale, the deal structure, the value creation drivers, and our recommendation.

Shadow 2 · Thesis statement

The strategic rationale for this deal is based on three pillars: market expansion, technology access, and cost synergies.

Shadow 3 · Closing recommendation

My recommendation is clear: we should approve this acquisition by the end of this month.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: strategic rationale, three pillars, value creation, deal structure.
  • Giọng tự tin, chắc chắn khi trình bày thesis và recommendation.
  • Giọng calm, reassuring khi address concerns.
  • Pause sau mỗi pillar để hội đồng tiêu hóa thông tin.

Speaking · JV/M&A Presentation

Luyện 3 vòng tăng dần: pitch ngắn → pitch đầy đủ → pitch hoàn chỉnh. Mục tiêu: tự tin trình bày và bảo vệ recommendation trước hội đồng.

Round 1 · Pitch 5 phút

Trình bày pitch ngắn 5 phút: opening, rationale, strategic fit, value creation, recommendation.

Opening: Good morning. Let me walk you through the investment case for our proposed acquisition.

Rationale: The strategic rationale is based on three pillars: market expansion, technology access, and cost synergies.

Strategic Fit: This deal represents a strong strategic fit with our growth strategy.

Value Creation: The primary value creation drivers are revenue synergies and cost optimization.

Recommendation: My recommendation is clear: we should approve this acquisition now.

Round 2 · Pitch 7 phút

Mở rộng pitch lên 7 phút: thêm chi tiết deal structure, synergies specifics, risk mitigation.

Opening (1 min): Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here. Let me walk you through the investment case for our proposed acquisition of TechCorp. I'll cover the strategic rationale, the deal structure, the value creation drivers, and our recommendation.

Rationale (2 min): The strategic rationale for this deal is based on three pillars: market expansion into the Asian market, access to cutting-edge AI technology, and significant cost synergies. This deal represents a strong strategic fit because it aligns perfectly with our 5-year growth strategy.

Value Creation (2 min): The primary value creation drivers are revenue synergies of $50 million and cost optimization of $30 million annually. We expect to realize these synergies within the first two years.

Deal Structure (1 min): The proposed deal structure includes a 60-40 joint venture with a clear exit strategy and governance framework.

Closing (1 min): In conclusion, this deal makes strategic sense because it creates significant value, aligns with our strategy, and has manageable risks. My recommendation is clear: we should approve this acquisition by the end of this month. Thank you. I'm happy to take any questions.

Round 3 · Pitch 10 phút

Sau khi pitch 7 phút, mở rộng lên 10 phút: thêm chi tiết market data, execution details, risk mitigation specifics, financial projections.

Opening (1 min): Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here. Let me walk you through the investment case for our proposed acquisition of TechCorp. I'll cover the strategic rationale, the deal structure, the value creation drivers, and our recommendation.

Rationale (2 min): The strategic rationale for this deal is based on three pillars: market expansion into the Asian market, access to cutting-edge AI technology, and significant cost synergies. This deal represents a strong strategic fit because it aligns perfectly with our 5-year growth strategy. The Asian market represents a $500 billion opportunity, and TechCorp's AI technology gives us a competitive advantage.

Value Creation (2 min): The primary value creation drivers are revenue synergies of $50 million and cost optimization of $30 million annually. We expect to realize these synergies within the first two years through cross-selling opportunities and operational efficiencies.

Deal Structure (2 min): The proposed deal structure includes a 60-40 joint venture with a clear exit strategy and governance framework. We will have majority control with 60% ownership, ensuring strategic direction alignment.

Risk Mitigation (1 min): We've identified key risks including integration risk and market risk. Our mitigation strategies include a dedicated integration team and phased market entry.

Closing (1 min): In conclusion, this deal makes strategic sense because it creates significant value, aligns with our strategy, and has manageable risks. My recommendation is clear: we should approve this acquisition by the end of this month. Thank you. I'm happy to take any questions.

Model Answer · 1-Page JV/M&A Memo

JV/M&A Memo

TechCorp Acquisition — Investment Recommendation

1. Strategic Rationale

Our strategic rationale is based on three pillars: market expansion into the Asian market ($500B opportunity), access to cutting-edge AI technology, and significant cost synergies ($30M annually).

2. Strategic Fit

This deal represents a strong strategic fit because it aligns perfectly with our 5-year growth strategy and provides competitive advantage in AI technology.

3. Value Creation

Primary value creation drivers: Revenue synergies of $50 million and cost optimization of $30 million annually, realizable within the first two years through cross-selling and operational efficiencies.

4. Deal Structure

Proposed deal structure: 60-40 joint venture with clear exit strategy and governance framework. We will have majority control with 60% ownership, ensuring strategic direction alignment.

5. Risk Mitigation

Key risks: Integration risk and market risk. Mitigation strategies: Dedicated integration team and phased market entry.

6. Recommendation

Recommendation: Approve this acquisition by the end of this month. This deal makes strategic sense because it creates significant value, aligns with our strategy, and has manageable risks.

Common Mistakes

4 lỗi thường gặp trong JV/M&A presentation

Tránh các lỗi này để mock JV/M&A presentation của bạn chuyên nghiệp và thuyết phục.

Tránh

Nên

Nên

Tránh

Nên

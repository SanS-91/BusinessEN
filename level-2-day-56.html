Relationship Management

Day 56 tập trung vào quản lý quan hệ: relationship management, stakeholder communication, và long-term relationships. Đây là kỹ năng quan trọng để duy trì mối quan hệ bền vững với ngân hàng, nhà đầu tư và đối tác.

Relationship management không chỉ là giao tiếp. Đó là xây dựng niềm tin, duy trì sự minh bạch, và tạo ra các mối quan hệ đối tác lâu dài dựa trên sự tin cậy lẫn nhau. Một relationship manager giỏi biết cách cân bằng giữa lợi ích của công ty và lợi ích của đối tác.

Block 1 · 15 phút

Vocabulary & Relationship Terms

8 thuật ngữ relationship management cốt lõi. Mỗi mục có IPA, nghĩa, collocation, ví dụ, ghi chú và native alternative.

Relationship Management

/rɪˈleɪʃnʃɪp ˈmænɪdʒmənt/

Quản lý quan hệ

Ghi chú: Relationship management là quá trình xây dựng và duy trì mối quan hệ với các bên liên quan.
  • Collocation: relationship management, effective relationship management, relationship management strategy
  • Example: Our relationship management strategy focuses on transparency and trust.
  • Native alternative: relationship management, stakeholder management

Stakeholder Communication

/steɪkhoʊldər kəˌmjuːnɪkeɪʃn/

Giao tiếp với các bên liên quan

Ghi chú: Stakeholder communication là giao tiếp với các bên liên quan: ngân hàng, nhà đầu tư, đối tác.
  • Collocation: stakeholder communication, effective stakeholder communication, stakeholder communication strategy
  • Example: We are committed to transparent stakeholder communication throughout the project.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder communication, stakeholder engagement

Long-term Relationship

/lɒŋ tɜːrm rɪˈleɪʃnʃɪp/

Mối quan hệ dài hạn

Ghi chú: Long-term relationship là mối quan hệ dài hạn, bền vững giữa các bên.
  • Collocation: long-term relationship, build long-term relationship, maintain long-term relationship
  • Example: We value our long-term relationship with the bank and are committed to transparency.
  • Native alternative: long-term relationship, long-term partnership

Trust Building

/trʌst ˈbɪldɪ/

Xây dựng niềm tin

Ghi chú: Trust building là quá trình xây dựng niềm tin giữa các bên trong mối quan hệ đối tác.
  • Collocation: trust building, trust building process, trust building activities
  • Example: Trust is the foundation of our partnership, and we are committed to trust building.
  • Native alternative: trust building, building trust

Stakeholder Engagement

/ˈsteɪkhoʊldər ɪnˈɡeɪdʒmənt/

Sự tham gia của các bên liên quan

Ghi chú: Stakeholder engagement là sự tham gia tích cực của các bên liên quan vào dự án.
  • Collocation: stakeholder engagement, effective stakeholder engagement, stakeholder engagement strategy
  • Example: We are committed to regular stakeholder engagement throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder engagement, stakeholder involvement

Relationship Building

/rɪˈleɪʃnʃɪp ˈbɪldɪŋ/

Xây dựng mối quan hệ

Ghi chú: Relationship building là quá trình xây dựng mối quan hệ bền vững với các bên liên quan.
  • Collocation: relationship building, relationship building process, relationship building activities
  • Example: We believe in building long-term partnerships based on mutual trust and respect.
  • Native alternative: relationship building, building relationships

Stakeholder Management

/steɪkhoʊldər ˈmænɪdʒmənt/

Quản lý các bên liên quan

Ghi chú: Stakeholder management là quản lý mối quan hệ với các bên liên quan một cách hiệu quả.
  • Collocation: stakeholder management, effective stakeholder management, stakeholder management plan
  • Example: Our stakeholder management plan ensures regular communication and engagement.
  • Native alternative: stakeholder management, relationship management

Long-term Partnership

/lɒŋ tɜrm ˈpɑːrtnərʃɪp/

Quan hệ đối tác dài hạn

Ghi chú: Long-term partnership là quan hệ đối tác dài hạn, bền vững giữa các bên.
  • Collocation: long-term partnership, build long-term partnership, maintain long-term partnership
  • Example: We believe in building long-term partnerships based on mutual trust and shared success.
  • Native alternative: long-term partnership, long-term relationship
Block 2 · 20 phút

Phrases & Sentence Patterns

6 mẫu câu relationship management: valuing relationship, committing to communication, building trust, regular engagement, building long-term partnerships, relationship management strategy.

We value our long-term relationship with...

Công thức: We value our long-term relationship with + stakeholder + commitment. Dùng để thể hiện sự trân trọng mối quan hệ.
Valuing relationship

We value our long-term relationship with the bank and are committed to transparency.

Substitution drill

Thay stakeholder: the investor / the partner / the stakeholder.

We are committed to transparent stakeholder communication...

Công thức: We are committed to transparent stakeholder communication + timeframe. Dùng để cam kết giao tiếp minh bạch.
Committing to communication

We are committed to transparent stakeholder communication throughout the project.

Substitution drill

Thay timeframe: throughout the project / throughout the partnership / throughout the relationship.

Trust is the foundation of our partnership...

Công thức: Trust is the foundation of our partnership, and we are committed to + action. Dùng để nhấn mạnh niềm tin.
Building trust

Trust is the foundation of our partnership, and we are committed to trust building.

Substitution drill

Thay action: trust building / transparency / open communication.

We are committed to regular stakeholder engagement...

Công thức: We are committed to regular stakeholder engagement + timeframe. Dùng để cam kết tham gia thường xuyên.
Regular engagement

We are committed to regular stakeholder engagement throughout the project lifecycle.

Substitution drill

Thay timeframe: throughout the project lifecycle / throughout the partnership / throughout the relationship.

We believe in building long-term partnerships based on...

Công thức: We believe in building long-term partnerships based on + principles. Dùng để thể hiện triết lý đối tác.
Building long-term partnerships

We believe in building long-term partnerships based on mutual trust and shared success.

Substitution drill

Thay principles: mutual trust and shared success / transparency and trust / mutual respect and trust.

Our relationship management strategy focuses on...

Công thức: Our relationship management strategy focuses on + focus areas. Dùng để trình bày chiến lược quản lý quan hệ.
Relationship management strategy

Our relationship management strategy focuses on transparency and trust.

Substitution drill

Thay focus areas: transparency and trust / transparency and communication / trust and mutual respect.

Block 3 · 20 phút

Listening · Relationship Management

Nghe một buổi thảo luận về relationship management mẫu. Đây là mô hình để bạn thực hành thảo luận về relationship management.

Nhiệm vụ nghe 20 phút

  1. Nghe toàn bộ 1 lần để nắm valuing relationship và building trust.
  2. Nghe lần hai để nhận diện các patterns: valuing relationship, committing to communication, building trust.
  3. Nghe lần ba để phân tích relationship techniques: regular engagement, building long-term partnerships.
  4. Làm comprehension questions và đối chiếu đáp án mẫu.
  5. Shadowing 3 câu trọng tâm.

Transcript

[Valuing Relationship]Good morning. Thank you for meeting with us today. We value our long-term relationship with the bank and are committed to transparency.

[Committing to Communication]We are committed to transparent stakeholder communication throughout the project.

[Building Trust]Trust is the foundation of our partnership, and we are committed to trust building.

[Regular Engagement]We are committed to regular stakeholder engagement throughout the project lifecycle.

[Building Long-term Partnerships]We believe in building long-term partnerships based on mutual trust and shared success.

[Relationship Management Strategy]Our relationship management strategy focuses on transparency and trust. Thank you.

Comprehension Questions

  • Q1: What is the company committed to in terms of relationship?
  • Q2: What is the company committed to in terms of communication?
  • Q3: What is the foundation of the partnership?
  • Q4: What is the company committed to in terms of engagement?
  • Q5: What does the company believe in building?

Sample Answers

1. Committed to transparency.
2. Committed to transparent stakeholder communication throughout the project.
3. Trust is the foundation of the partnership.
4. Committed to regular stakeholder engagement throughout the project lifecycle.
5. Believes in building long-term partnerships based on mutual trust and shared success.

Shadowing Challenge

Shadow 1 · Valuing relationship

We value our long-term relationship with the bank and are committed to transparency.

Shadow 2 · Building trust

Trust is the foundation of our partnership, and we are committed to trust building.

Shadow 3 · Building long-term partnerships

We believe in building long-term partnerships based on mutual trust and shared success.

Pronunciation Focus

  • Nhấn vào các từ khóa: long-term relationship, stakeholder communication, trust building.
  • Giọng chân thành, chắc chắn khi thể hiện sự trân trọng và cam kết.
  • Giọng tin cậy, tôn trọng khi thảo luận về niềm tin và đối tác.
  • Pause sau mỗi cam kết để đối tác tiêu hóa thông tin.
